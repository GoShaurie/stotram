जटाटवी गलज्जल प्रवाह पावितस्थले
गलेवलम्ब्य लम्बितां भुजङ्गतुङ्गमालिकाम् ।
डमड्डमड्डमड्डमन्निनादवड्डमर्वयं
चकार चण्डताण्डवं तनोतु नः शिवः शिवम् ॥ १ ॥

jaṭāṭavī galajjala pravāha pāvitasthalē
galēvalambya lambitāṁ bhujaṅgatuṅgamālikām |
ḍamaḍḍamaḍḍamaḍḍamanninādavaḍḍamarvayaṁ
cakāra caṇḍatāṇḍavaṁ tanōtu naḥ śivaḥ śivam || 1 ||

jataatavii galajjala pravaaha paavita sthale
galevalambya lambitaam bhujanga tunga maalikaam |
damaddamaddamaddamanninaada vaddamarvayam
chakaara chanda taandavam tanotu nah shivah shivam || ||

From the forest of his matted lock, water flows and wets his neck,
On which hangs the greatest of snake-like a garland,
And his drum incessantly plays damat, damat, damat, damat,
And Shiva is engaged in the very vigorous manly dance,
To bless and shower, prosperity on all of us.

जटाटवी (jaṭāṭavī) – forest of matted locks (jaṭā:matted locks | ṭavī:forest); गलज्जल (galajjala) – flowing water (galat:flowing | jala:water); प्रवाह (pravāha) – stream; पावितस्थले (pāvitasthale) – purified place (pāvita:purified | sthale:place); गलेवलम्ब्य (galevalambya) – hanging on the neck (gale:on the neck | avalambya:hanging); लम्बितां (lambitām) – hanging; भुजङ्गतुङ्गमालिकाम् (bhujaṅgatuṅgamālikām) – snake-like garland (bhujaṅga:snake | tuṅga:like | mālikām:garland); डमड्डमड्डमड्डमन्निनादवड्डमर्वयं (ḍamaḍḍamaḍḍamaḍḍamanninādavaḍḍamarvayaṁ) – The ḍamaḍḍama sound of the damaru or drum resounding and causing great wonder or astonishment (ḍamaḍḍamaḍḍamaḍḍam:drum sound | nināda:sound | vaḍḍamarvayaṁ:great wonder or astonishment); चकार (cakāra) – performed; चण्डताण्डवं (caṇḍatāṇḍavaṁ) – vigorous dance (caṇḍa:vigorous | tāṇḍavaṁ:dance); तनोतु (tanotu) – may he grant; नः (naḥ) – us; शिवः (śivaḥ) – Shiva; शिवम् (śivam) – auspiciousness;
