[p. 901,2] : the pale or faintly discernible streak of the young moon's crescent, Kir. (cf. candra- and śaśāṅka-l˚), the act of delineation, drawing, painting, Śak. (v.l.)

[p. 901,2] : writing, handwriting, L.

[p. 901,2] : a drawing, likeness, figure, impression (cf. mṛga- and savya-pāda-l˚)

[p. 901,2] : the drawing of lines with fragrant substances (on the face, arms, breast &c.), L.

[p. 901,2] : a hem, border, rim, edge, horizon, Kām.; VarBṛS.

[p. 901,2] : the crest (= śikhā or cūḍāgra), L.
