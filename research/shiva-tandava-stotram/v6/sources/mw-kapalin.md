[p. 250,2] kapālin : mfn. bearing a pot (to receive food, as a beggar), Nār.

[p. 250,2] : furnished with or bearing skulls, Yājñ. iii, 243; BhP.; Kum.

[p. 250,2] kapālin : m. f. (ī, inī) a man or woman of low caste (son or daughter of a Brāhman mother and a fisherman father)

[p. 250,2] : the follower of a particular Śaiva sect (carrying skulls of men as ornament and eating and drinking from them; cf. kāpālika), Prab.; Kathās. &c.

[p. 250,2] kapālin : m. N. of Śiva, MBh. i; Bālar. &c.

[p. 250,2] : of one of the eleven Rudras, MBh. ii; Hariv. &c.

[p. 250,2] : of a demon causing diseases, Hariv. 9557

[p. 250,2] : of a teacher
