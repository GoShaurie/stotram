ललाट चत्वरज्वलद्धनञ्जय स्फुलिङ्गभा
निपीत पञ्चसायकं नमन्निलिम्पनायकम् ।
सुधामयूखलेखया विराजमान शेखरं
महाकपालि सम्पदे शिरोजटालमस्तु नः ॥ ६ ॥

lalāṭa catvarajvaladdhanañjaya sphuliṅgabhā
nipīta pañcasāyakaṁ namannilimpanāyakam |
sudhāmayūkhalēkhayā virājamāna śēkharaṁ
mahākapāli sampadē śirōjaṭālamastu naḥ || 6 ||

lalaata chatvara jvalad dhananjaya sphulingabhaa
nipiita panchasaayakam naman nilimpanaayakam |
sudhaa mayuukha lekhayaa viraajamaana shekharam
mahaa kapaali sampade shirah jataalam astu nah || ||

May he with the raging fire
In his forehead, who burnt the God of love,
May He who is forever being saluted by king of devas,
And may he who has collected
The cool ambrosia like crescent moon on his head,
And may he who wears the collection of skulls,
Bless us to create wealth for us.

ललाट (lalāṭa) – forehead; चत्वर (catvara) – square; ज्वलत् (jvalat) – burning; धनञ्जय (dhanañjaya) – Arjuna; स्फुलिङ्गभा (sphuliṅgabhā) – spark-like radiance (sphuliṅga:spark | bhā:radiance); निपीत (nipīta) – consumed; पञ्चसायकं (pañcasāyakaṁ) – five-arrowed (Kama) (pañca:five | sāyaka:arrow); नमन् (naman) – saluting; निलिम्पनायकम् (nilimpanāyakam) – leader of the gods (nilimpa:god | nāyaka:leader); सुधामयूखलेखया (sudhāmayūkhalekhayā) – with nectar-like moonbeam (sudhā:nectar | mayūkha:moonbeam | lekhayā:with line); विराजमान (virājamāna) – shining; शेखरं (śekharaṁ) – crest; महाकपालि (mahākapāli) – great skull-bearer (mahā:great | kapāli:skull-bearer); सम्पदे (sampade) – for wealth; शिरोजटालम् (śirojaṭālam) – matted hair on head (śiraḥ:head | jaṭālam:matted hair); अस्तु (astu) – may be; नः (naḥ) – for us;
