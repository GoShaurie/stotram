[p. 789,2] mayū/kha : m. (prob. fr. √ 1. mi) a kind of peg (esp. for hanging woven cloth or skins upon), RV.; AV.; Br.; ŚrS.

[p. 789,2] : the pin or gnomon of a sun-dial (= kīla), L.

[p. 789,2] : a ray of light, flame, brightness, lustre, Up.; Kāv.; Var. &c. (also f(ā). , L.; once n. in KauṣUp.)

[p. 789,2] : a partic. Agni, GṛS.

[p. 789,2] : N. of wk. (by Śaṃkara-miśra).
