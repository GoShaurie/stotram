[p. 1207,3] sā/yaka : mfn. intended or fitted to be discharged or hurled, RV. (Naigh. ii, 20)

[p. 1207,3] sā/yaka : m. (in RV. also n.) a missile, arrow, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 1207,3] sā/yaka : m. a symbolical expression for the number ‘five’ (from the 5 arrows of the god of love), Sāh.

[p. 1207,3] : a sword, MBh.; R.

[p. 1207,3] : the latitude of the sky, Gaṇit.

[p. 1207,3] : Saccharum Sara, L.

[p. 1207,3] : N. of a man, Pravar.
