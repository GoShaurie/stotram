## Verse 6

**Witness numbering:** A: 6 | A': 6 | B: 6 | C: 6 | D: 6 | E: 6

**Chosen reading:**

```
ललाटचत्वरज्वलद्धनञ्जयस्फुलिङ्गभा
निपीतपञ्चसायकं नमन्निलिम्पनायकम् ।
सुधामयूखलेखया विराजमानशेखरं
महाकपालि सम्पदे शिरो जटालमस्तु नः ॥ ६ ॥
```

1. **Pāda 4, substantive variant**:
   - "महाकपालि सम्पदे शिरो जटालमस्तु नः" in A, A', C, D ("शिरोजटालमस्तु"), and E.
   - "महः कपालि सम्पदे सरिज्जटालमस्तु नः" in B. A and A' also print this line in parentheses after the verse as an alternative.
   - Chosen **महाकपालि सम्पदे शिरो जटालमस्तु नः** (4 of 5 witnesses).
   - **Human check:** the two readings construe differently ("may the matted head of the great skull-bearer be for our prosperity" vs "may the radiance, skull-bearing and having the river in its locks, …"). The variant is worth an annotation.
2. **(स्फुलिङ्गया निपीत)**: A prints this in parentheses after pāda 2 as a variant for स्फुलिङ्गभा-निपीत, and A' prints it as "(स्फुलिङ्गया निपीतप)". No other witness has it. Chosen **स्फुलिङ्गभा / निपीत…**.
3. **Spacing in pāda 4**: "शिरो जटालमस्तु" in A, C, E; "शिरोजटालमस्तु" in D. B has the other reading. Chosen **शिरो जटालमस्तु**. C hyphenates महा-/कपालि across a page break (pp. 468–469), which is book layout. No witness separates महा from कपालि.
4. Anusvara spellings in E (स्फुलिंगभा, पंचसायकं, संपदे). Orthographic only.
5. The compound …स्फुलिङ्गभा- / निपीत… runs across the pāda 1/2 break. A, A' and E print a hyphen and C prints a spaced hyphen ("भा - निपीत"). D has no hyphen and B joins the two. Hyphen dropped.
