## Verse 9

**Witness numbering:** A: 9 | A': 9 | B: 9 | C: 9 | D: 9 | E: 9

**Chosen reading:**

```
प्रफुल्लनीलपङ्कजप्रपञ्चकालिमप्रभा
वलम्बिकण्ठकन्दलीरुचिप्रबद्धकन्धरम् ।
स्मरच्छिदं पुरच्छिदं भवच्छिदं मखच्छिदं
गजच्छिदान्धकच्छिदं तमन्तकच्छिदं भजे ॥ ९ ॥
```

1. **Avagraha at the pāda 1/2 junction, प्रभा-ऽवलम्बि vs प्रभा-वलम्बि** (orthographic, **human check**):
   - "…प्रभा- / ऽवलम्बि…" in A and A'; "प्रभाऽवलम्बि" in B.
   - "…प्रभा- / वलम्बि…" with no avagraha in C ("प्रभा-वलम्बि"), D ("प्रभा- / -वलम्बि") and E.
   - Chosen by majority (3 vs 2): line 2 begins **वलम्बि…** with no avagraha. The word is avalambi either way (prabhā + avalambi, which fuses to prabhāvalambi). The avagraha is only an optional reading aid. Without it, the start of line 2 can look like a word "valambi", and D glosses it that way ("वलम्बि (valambi) – hanging"). A human may prefer to restore ऽ for clarity.
2. **गजच्छिदान्धक vs गजच्छिदांधक**: A' writes anusvara. Orthographic only.
3. Anusvara spellings in E (पंकज, प्रपंच). Orthographic only.
4. Spacing: D prints spaces inside the compounds. The others do not.
