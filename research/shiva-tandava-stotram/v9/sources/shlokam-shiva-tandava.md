प्रफुल्ल नील पङ्कज प्रपञ्च कालिमप्रभा-
-वलम्बि कण्ठ कन्दली रुचि प्रबद्ध कन्धरम् ।
स्मरच्छिदं पुरच्छिदं भवच्छिदं मखच्छिदं
गजच्छिदान्धकच्छिदं तमन्तकच्छिदं भजे ॥ ९ ॥

praphulla nīla paṅkaja prapañca kālimaprabhā-
-valambi kaṇṭha kandalī ruci prabaddha kandharam |
smaracchidaṁ puracchidaṁ bhavacchidaṁ makhacchidaṁ
gajacchidāndhakacchidaṁ tamantakacchidaṁ bhajē || 9 ||

praphulla niila pankaja prapancha kaalima prabhaa-
-valambi kantha kandalii ruchi prabaddha kandharam |
smara cchidam pura cchidam bhava cchidam makha cchidam
gaja cchida andhaka cchidam tam antaka cchidam bhaje || ||

I salute him, who shines with a black neck
Similar to the well opened blue lotus,
On which all the temples depend for prayer,
And him who destroyed God of love, the three cities,
The worldly problems and yaga destroyers,
And him who destroyed elephant faced Asura and also God of death.,

प्रफुल्ल (praphulla) – blooming; नील (nīla) – blue; पङ्कज (paṅkaja) – lotus; प्रपञ्च (prapañca) – world; कालिमप्रभा (kālimaprabhā) – black radiance (kālima:blackness | prabhā:radiance); वलम्बि (valambi) – hanging; कण्ठ (kaṇṭha) – neck; कन्दली (kandalī) – stem; रुचि (ruci) – beauty; प्रबद्ध (prabaddha) – bound; कन्धरम् (kandharam) – shoulder; स्मरच्छिदं (smaracchidaṁ) – destroyer of Cupid (smara:Cupid | chidaṁ:destroyer); पुरच्छिदं (puracchidaṁ) – destroyer of cities (pura:cities | chidaṁ:destroyer); भवच्छिदं (bhavacchidaṁ) – destroyer of worldly problems (bhava:worldly problems | chidaṁ:destroyer); मखच्छिदं (makhacchidaṁ) – destroyer of sacrifices (makha:sacrifices | chidaṁ:destroyer); गजच्छिद (gajacchida) – destroyer of elephant (gaja:elephant | chida:destroyer); अन्धकच्छिदं (andhakacchidaṁ) – destroyer of Andhaka (andhaka:Andhaka | chidaṁ:destroyer); तमन्तकच्छिदं (tamantakacchidaṁ) – destroyer of Yama (tama:Yama | antaka:destroyer); भजे (bhaje) – I worship;
