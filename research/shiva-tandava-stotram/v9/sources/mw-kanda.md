[p. 249,2] kanda : m. n. (√ kan Comm. on Uṇ. iv, 98), a bulbous or tuberous root, a bulb, MBh.; BhP.; Suśr. &c.

[p. 249,2] : the bulbous root of Amorphophallus Campanulatus, L.

[p. 249,2] : garlic, L.

[p. 249,2] : a lump, swelling, knot, Suśr. i, 258, 9

[p. 249,3] kanda : [cf. Gk. κόνδος, κονδύλος; O. H. G. hnūtr, hnūta]

[p. 249,3] : an affection of the female organ (considered as a fleshy excrescence, but apparently prolapsus uteri, W.)

[p. 249,3] : N. of a metre (of four lines of thirteen syllables each)

[p. 249,3] : (in mus.) a kind of time
