## Verse 14

**Witness numbering:** A: 14 | A': 14 (appendix) | B: 14 | C: absent | D: absent | E: 14

**Chosen reading:**

```
निलिम्पनाथनागरीकदम्बमौलमल्लिका
निगुम्फनिर्भरक्षरन्मधूष्णिकामनोहरः ।
तनोतु नो मनोमुदं विनोदिनीमहर्निशं
परश्रियः परं पदं तदङ्गजत्विषां चयः ॥ १४ ॥
```

**Witnesses:** only three independent witnesses print this verse: A (verse 14; A' prints it only in an appendix after the closing इति, as one of "two [verses] that devotee can add" for a 17-verse version), B (verse 14) and E (श्लोक १४). It is **absent** from C (Ramakrishna Math) and D (shlokam), which have 13 verses plus 2 phalaśruti verses.

1. **कदम्बमौलमल्लिका vs कदम्बमौलिमल्लिका** (pāda 1): मौल in A, A', B (and bhaktibharat.com). मौलि in E, which also prints a space: "निलिम्पनाथनागरी कदम्बमौलिमल्लिका-". Chosen **मौल** (2 vs 1). **Human check:** maula/mauli is a plausible lexical variant.
2. **विनोदिनीमहर्निशं vs विनोदिनींमहर्निशं** (pāda 3): E adds an anusvara. Chosen **विनोदिनीमहर्निशं** (A, B).
3. **परश्रियः vs परश्रियं** (pāda 4): परश्रियः in A, A', B; परश्रियं in E (bhaktibharat: the corrupt "परिश्रय"). Chosen **परश्रियः**.
4. **तदङ्गज vs तदंगज** (E): orthographic only.
5. Spacing: E prints "निगुम्फनिर्भर क्षरन्मधूष्णिका…" with a space. A and B do not. Chosen: no space.
6. The compound …मल्लिका- / निगुम्फ… runs across the pāda 1/2 break. Hyphen dropped.
7. **Independence caveat:** B's text agrees with A letter for letter in this verse. Whether B descends from A cannot be determined.
