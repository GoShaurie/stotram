[p. 794,3] mahar : ind. (for mahas) the fourth of the seven worlds which rise one above the other (supposed to be the abode of those saints who survive a destruction of the world, Pur.; Vedāntas.; cf. IW. 55 n. 2).

[p. 803,3] mahar : &c. See p. 794, col. 3.
