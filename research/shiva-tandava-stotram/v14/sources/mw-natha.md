[p. 534,3] nātha/ : n. refuge, help, AV.; TBr.

[p. 534,3] nātha/ : m. a protector, patron, possessor, owner, lord (often ifc., esp. in names of gods and men, e.g. goviṅda-, jagan- &c.; but also mf(ā)n. possessed of occupied by, furnished with, cf. sa-)

[p. 534,3] nātha/ : m. a husband (esp. in voc.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 534,3] : a rope passed through the nose of a draft ox, L.

[p. 534,3] : N. of sev. authors, Cat.
