[p. 541,2] nir—bhara : mf(ā)n. ‘without weight or measure’, excessive, vehement, violent

[p. 541,2] : deep, sound (as sleep), ardent (as an embrace), Kāv. &c.

[p. 541,2] : full of, abounding in Kathās.; Pur. &c. (also ˚rita with instr. Cat.)
