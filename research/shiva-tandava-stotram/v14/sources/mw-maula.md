[p. 837,1] maula : mf(ā)n. (fr. mūla) derived from roots (as poison), Hcar.

[p. 837,1] : handed down from antiquity, ancient (as a custom), MBh.

[p. 837,1] : holding office from previous generations, hereditary (as a minister or warrior), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 837,1] : aboriginal, indigenous, Mn. viii, 62; 259

[p. 837,1] maula : m. an hereditary minister (holding his office from father and grandfather), Ragh.; Daś.

[p. 837,1] maula : m. pl. aboriginal inhabitants who have emigrated, L.

[p. 837,1] maula : m. (with pārthivāḥ) = mūlaprakṛtayaḥ, Kām.
