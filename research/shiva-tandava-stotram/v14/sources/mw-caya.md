[p. 389,1] 1. & 2. caya, &c. : See √ 1. & 3. ci.

[p. 394,2] 1. caya : mfn. ‘collecting’, see vṛtaṃ-

[p. 394,2] caya : m. (iii, 3, 56, Kāś.; g. vṛṣādi) a mound of earth (raised to form the foundation of a building or raised as a rampart), MBh. iii, 11699; Hariv.; R.; Pañcat.

[p. 394,2] : a cover, covering, W.

[p. 394,2] : a heap, pile, collection, multitude, assemblage, MBh.; Hariv. &c.

[p. 394,2] : (in med.) accumulation of the humors (cf. saṃ-), Suśr.

[p. 394,2] : the amount by which each term increases, common increase or difference of the terms, Bījag. (cf. agni-).

[p. 394,3] 2. caya : mfn. ifc. ‘revenging’, see ṛṇaṃ-.
