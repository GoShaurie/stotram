## ORDER OF THE TWO PHALAŚRUTI VERSES (human decision needed)

The brief proposed **16 = पूजावसानसमये दशवक्त्रगीतं…** and 17 = the other closing verse. **No witness has that order.** All six (A, A', B, C, D, E) print **इमं हि नित्यमेवमुक्तमुत्तमोत्तमं स्तवं… first** and **पूजावसानसमये… last**. bhaktibharat.com and hindu-blog.com do the same. The witnesses agree on the order and differ only in the numbers:
- A, B, E (17-verse texts): इमं हि = 16, पूजावसान = 17 (B leaves it unnumbered).
- A', C, D (13 + 2 texts): इमं हि = 14, पूजावसान = 15.

This pack therefore numbers them **v16 = इमं हि…** and **v17 = पूजावसानसमये…**, following the unanimous evidence. Each source id matches the vyakhya page number (vyakhya-sts-16 = इमं हि). If the user still wants पूजावसान as 16, swap the folders `v16` and `v17` and the verse numbers inside `text.txt` and `fulltext/text.txt`. The source files would keep their own locators.
