[p. 96,1] 1. a/va : m. favour, RV. i, 128, 5

[p. 96,1] : (cf. nirava/.)

[p. 96,1] 2. a/va : ind. (as a prefix to verbs and verbal nouns expresses) off, away, down, RV. &c.

[p. 96,1] : (exceptionally as a preposition with abl.) down from, AV. vii, 55, 1

[p. 96,1] : (for another use of this preposition See ava-kokila.)

[p. 96,1] 3. ava : (only gen. du. avo/r with vām, ‘of you both’, corresponding to sa/ tva/m, ‘thou’, &c.), this, RV. vi, 67, 11; vii, 67, 4 and (vā for vām), x, 132, 5

[p. 96,1] ava : [Zd. pron. ava; Slav. ovo; cf. also the syllable, αὐ in αὐ-τός, αὖ, αὖθι, &c.; Lat. au-t, au-tem, &c.]
