[p. 987,2] vi-vāha/ : m. leading away (of the bride from her father's house), taking a wife, marriage with (instr. with or without saha), AV.; &c. (eight kinds of marriage are enumerated in Mn. iii, 21, viz. Brāhma, Daiva, Ārṣa, Prājāpatya, Āsura, Gāndharva, Rākṣasa, and Paiśāca; cf. Yājñ. i, 58-61 and IW. 190 &c.)

[p. 987,2] : a partic. wind, Śak., Sch. (prob. w.r. for vi-vaha)

[p. 987,2] : a vehicle (and ‘marriage’), AitBr. vii, 13

[p. 987,2] vi-vāha/ : n. a partic. high number, Buddh.
