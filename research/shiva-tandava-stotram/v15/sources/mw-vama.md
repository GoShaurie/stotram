[p. 941,1] 1. vāma : m. (for 2. and 3. See col. 2) the act of vomiting g. jvalādi

[p. 941,2] 2. vāma/ : mf(ī/ or ā)n. (fr. √ 1. van; for 1. See col. 1) lovely, dear, pleasant, agreeable, fair, beautiful, splendid, noble, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 941,2] : (ifc.) striving after, eager for, intent upon, fond of Kāv.; Kathās.; Rājat.

[p. 941,2] vāma/ : m. the female breast, L.

[p. 941,2] : the god of love, L.

[p. 941,2] : N. of Śiva, BhP.

[p. 941,2] : of a Rudra, ib.

[p. 941,2] : of Varuṇa, L.

[p. 941,2] : (with Śaivas) = vāma-deva-guhya, Sarvad.

[p. 941,2] : of a son of Ṛcīka, MBh. (B. rāma)

[p. 941,2] : of a son of Kṛṣṇa and Bhadrā, BhP.

[p. 941,2] : of a prince (son of Dharma), Cat.

[p. 941,2] : of a son of Bhaṭṭa-nārāyaṇa, Kṣitīś. (cf. -deva)

[p. 941,2] : of one of the Moon's horses, VP.

[p. 941,2] vāma/ : m. or n. a kind of pot-herb, Chenopodium Album, L.

[p. 941,2] vāma/ : n. a lovely thing, any dear or desirable good (as gold, horses &c.), wealth, fortune, RV.; AV.; Br.; ChUp.

[p. 941,2] vāma/ : mfn. relating to a mare, Pat. (cf. under ī f. above).

[p. 941,2] 3. vā/ma : mf(ā)n. (perhaps originally identical with 2. above) left, not right, being or situated on the left side, ŚBr. &c. &c. (the quivering of the left eye or arm is supposed to be a good omen in women and of the left arm a bad omen in men)

[p. 941,2] : reverse, adverse, contrary, opposite, unfavourable, Kāv.; Kathās.

[p. 941,2] : crooked, oblique (am ind. sideways), BhP.

[p. 941,2] : refractory, coy (in love), Sāh.

[p. 941,2] : acting in the opposite way or differently, Śak. iv, 18

[p. 941,2] : hard, cruel, Kāv.; Pur.

[p. 941,3] : vile, wicked, base, low, bad, Kir.

[p. 941,3] vā/ma : m. or n. the left side (vāmād dakṣiṇam, from the left to the right; vāmena, on the left side), Kāv.; Kathās.

[p. 941,3] vā/ma : m. the left hand, RāmatUp.

[p. 941,3] : a snake, L.

[p. 941,3] : an animal, sentient being, L.

[p. 941,3] vā/ma : n. adversity, misfortune, Kathās.

[p. 941,3] : the left-hand practices of the followers of the Tantras (= vāmācāra, q.v.), Cat.
