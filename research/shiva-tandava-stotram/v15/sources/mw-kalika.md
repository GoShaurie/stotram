[p. 277,3] 1. kālika : m. a species of heron (Ardea jaculator), L. (v.l. kālīka)

[p. 277,3] : N. of a king of the Nāgas, Lalit.

[p. 277,3] : of a prince, Pañcad.

[p. 279,1] 2. kālika : mf(ī, Pāṇ. v, 1, 108)n. relating to or connected with or depending on time, Bhāṣāp.

[p. 279,1] : fit for any particular season, seasonable, MBh. iii, 868

[p. 279,1] : lasting a long time, Pāṇ. v, 1, 108

[p. 279,1] : (often ifc., e.g. āsanna-k˚, relating to a time near at hand, impending, Pāṇ. v, 4, 20, Sch.; māsa-k˚, monthly, MBh. ii, 2080).

[p. 279,2] 1. and 2. kālika . : See col. 1 and p. 277, col. 3.
