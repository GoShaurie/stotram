[p. 785,3] ma/ntra : m. (rarely n.; ifc. f(ā). ), ‘instrument of thought’, speech, sacred text or speech, a prayer or song of praise, RV.; AV.; TS.

[p. 785,3] : a Vedic hymn or sacrificial formula, that portion of the Veda which contains the texts called ṛc or yajus or sāman (q.v.) as opp. to the Brāhmaṇa and Upaniṣad portion (see IW. 5 &c.), Br.; GṛŚrS. &c.

[p. 786,1] : a sacred formula addressed to any individual deity (e.g. om śivāya namaḥ), RTL. 61

[p. 786,1] : a mystical verse or magical formula (sometimes personified), incantation, charm, spell (esp. in modern times employed by the Śāktas to acquire superhuman powers; the primary Mantras being held to be 70 millions in number and the secondary innumerable, RTL. 197-202), RV. (i, 147, 4); ĀśvŚr.; Mn.; Kathās.; Suśr.

[p. 786,1] : consultation, resolution, counsel, advice, plan, design, secret, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 786,1] : N. of Viṣṇu, Viṣṇ.

[p. 786,1] : of Śiva, MBh.

[p. 786,1] : (in astrol.) the fifth mansion, VarYogay.
