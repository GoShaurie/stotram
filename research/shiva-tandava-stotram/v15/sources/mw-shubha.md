[p. 1083,2] śubha : mf(ā)n. splendid, bright, beautiful, handsome (often f. voc., śubhe, ‘fair one!’ in addressing a beautiful woman), Mn.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1083,2] : pleasant, agreeable, suitable, fit, capable, useful, good (applied to persons and things), ib.

[p. 1083,2] : auspicious, fortunate, prosperous, ib.

[p. 1083,2] : good (in moral sense), righteous, virtuous, honest, ŚvetUp.; Mn. &c.

[p. 1083,2] : pure (as an action), Yājñ., Sch.

[p. 1083,2] : eminent, distinguished, W.

[p. 1083,2] : learned, versed in the Vedas, ib.

[p. 1083,2] śubha : m. water, L.

[p. 1083,2] : the Phenila tree (Sapindus Detergens), L.

[p. 1083,2] : a he-goat, L. (prob. w.r. for stubha)

[p. 1083,2] : the 23rd of the astrol. Yogas, L.

[p. 1083,2] : N. of a man (cf. g. tikādi), Kathās.

[p. 1083,2] : of a son of Dharma, BhP.

[p. 1083,2] : of an author, Cat.

[p. 1083,2] śubha : m. (also f(ā). ) a city floating in the sky (cf. śaubha = vyomacāri-pura), MW.

[p. 1083,2] śubha : (am), n. anything bright or beautiful &c.

[p. 1083,2] : beauty, charm, good fortune, auspiciousness, happiness, bliss, welfare, prosperity, Kauś.; Kāv.; Kathās.

[p. 1083,2] : benefit, service, good or virtuous action, Kāv.; VarBṛS.; Kathās.

[p. 1083,2] : the wood of Cerasus Puddum, L.
