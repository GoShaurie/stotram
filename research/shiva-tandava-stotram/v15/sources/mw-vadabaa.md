[p. 915,1] vaḍabā : (ā), f., see next.

[p. 915,1] va/ḍabā : f. (also written va/davā, ba/ḍavā, ba/ḍabā) a female horse, mare, TS. &c. &c.

[p. 915,1] : the nymph Aśvinī (who, in the form of a mare as wife of Vivasvat or the Sun, became the mother of the two Aśvins; cf. aśvinī), Pur.; Daś.

[p. 915,1] : a partic. constellation represented by a horse's head, W.

[p. 915,1] : a female slave, L.

[p. 915,1] : a harlot, prostitute, L.

[p. 915,1] : = dvija-strī, L.

[p. 915,1] : N. of a woman (having the patr. Prātītheyī), GṛS.

[p. 915,1] : of a wife of Vasu-deva (called paricārikā), Hariv.

[p. 915,1] : of a river, MBh.

[p. 915,1] : of a place of pilgrimage, Viṣṇ.
