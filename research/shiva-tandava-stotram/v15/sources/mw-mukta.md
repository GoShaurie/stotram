[p. 819,3] mukta : muktā, mukti See p. 816 &c.

[p. 820,3] mukta : mfn. loosened, let loose, set free, relaxed, slackened, opened, open, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 820,3] : liberated, delivered, emancipated (esp. from sin or worldly existence), Mn.; MBh. &c. (with instr. or ifc. = released from, deprived or destitute of; cf. Pāṇ. ii, 1, 38)

[p. 820,3] : fallen or dropped down (as fruit), Hariv.

[p. 820,3] : abandoned, relinquished, quitted, given up, laid aside, deposed, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 820,3] : sent forth, emitted, discharged, poured out, hurled, thrown, ib.

[p. 820,3] : left free (as a road), Megh.

[p. 820,3] : uttered (as sound), MBh.

[p. 820,3] : shed (as tears), Pañcat.

[p. 820,3] : let fly, applied (as a kick), Ragh.

[p. 820,3] : gone, vanished, disappeared (esp. ibc.; cf. below)

[p. 820,3] mukta : m. N. of one of the 7 sages under Manu Bhautya, MBh.

[p. 820,3] : of a cook, Rājat.

[p. 820,3] mukta : n. the spirit released from corporeal existence, W.
