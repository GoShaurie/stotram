[p. 530,2] nala : m. (cf. naḍa/, naḻa/) a species of reed, Amphidonax Karka (8-12 feet high), Gobh.; MBh. &c.

[p. 530,2] : a measure of length, MBh. (v.l. tala)

[p. 530,2] : a partic. form of constellation in which all the planets or stars are grouped in double mansions, Var.

[p. 530,2] : the 50th year of the cycle of Jupiter which lasts 60 years, Cat.

[p. 530,2] : N. of a divine being mentioned with Yama, Karmapr. (= pitṛ-deva, or -daiva, L.; a deified progenitor, W.)

[p. 530,2] : of a Daitya, BrahmaP.

[p. 530,2] : of a king of the Niṣadhas (son of Vīra-sena and husband of Damayantī), MBh.; Pur.

[p. 530,2] : of a son of Niṣadha and father of Nabha or Nabhas, Hariv.; Ragh.; VP.

[p. 530,2] : of a descendant of the latter Nala (s˚ of Su-dhanvan and f˚ of Uktha), Hariv.; VP.

[p. 530,2] : of a son of Yadu, Pur.

[p. 530,2] : of a monkey-chief (son of Tvaṣṭṛ or Viśva-karman; cf. -setu), MBh.; R.

[p. 530,2] : of a medic. author, Cat.

[p. 530,2] nala : n. the blossom of Nelumbium Speciosum, L. (cf. nalina, ˚nī)

[p. 530,2] : smell, odour, L. (cf. √ nal).
