## Verse 15

**Witness numbering:** A: 15 | A': 15 (appendix) | B: 15 | C: absent | D: absent | E: 15

**Chosen reading:**

```
प्रचण्डवाडवानलप्रभाशुभप्रचारणी
महाष्टसिद्धिकामिनीजनाऽवहूतजल्पना ।
विमुक्तवामलोचनाविवाहकालिकध्वनिः
शिवेति मन्त्रभूषणा जगज्जयाय जायताम् ॥ १५ ॥
```

**Witnesses:** only three independent witnesses print this verse: A (verse 15; A' prints it only in the appendix, as for v14), B (verse 15) and E (श्लोक १५). It is **absent** from C and D.

1. **प्रचारणी vs प्रचारिणी** (pāda 1): प्रचारणी in A, A', B. E's verse text reads "प्रचारिणी", but E's own padaccheda reads प्रचारणी. Chosen **प्रचारणी**.
2. **जनाऽवहूतजल्पना vs जनावहूत जल्पना** (pāda 2): A, A' and B have the avagraha and no space. E (and bhaktibharat.com) have no avagraha and a space. Chosen **जनाऽवहूतजल्पना** (2 vs 1). This is orthographic only.
3. **Pāda 3, substantive**: "विमुक्तवामलोचनाविवाहकालिकध्वनिः" is in A, A', B. "विमुक्तवामलोचनो विवाहकालिकध्वनिः" is in E (bhaktibharat.com: "विमुक्त वाम लोचनो विवाहकालिकध्वनिः"). Chosen **…लोचनाविवाह…** (A, B). **Human check.**
4. **Pāda 4, substantive**: "मन्त्रभूषणा" in A, A', B; "मन्त्रभूषणो" in E (bhaktibharat: the corrupt "मन्त्रभूषगो"). Chosen **मन्त्रभूषणा**. This goes together with item 3: E's text makes the subject masculine, while A and B have a feminine subject (…प्रचारणी, …जल्पना, …ध्वनिः, …भूषणा).
5. **Caveat:** this verse has the thinnest attestation in the pack, and two of the three witnesses (A, B) agree letter for letter. A human should confirm the text against a printed edition (for example, Gita Press) if one becomes available.
