 mUrDan

 (H1)  [Printed book page 826,1]

mUrDan   m. (Uṇ. i, 158 ) the forehead, head in general, skull, (fig.) the highest or first part of anything, top, point, summit, front (of battle), commencement, beginning, first, chief (applied to persons), RV. &c., &c., (mUrDni with √vft &c., to be above everything, prevail; with √Df, or A-√dA = mUrDnA √1. kf, to place on the head, hold in high honour)  [ID=166271 ]

  the base (in geom.; opp. to agra; perhaps w.r. for buDna), Col.  [ID=166272 ]

  (in gram.) the roof or top of the palate (as one of the 8 Sthānas or places of utterance), Pāṇ. i, 1, 9, Sch.  [ID=166273 ]

  (with Buddhists) ‘the summit’, N. of a state of spiritual exaltation, Divyāv.  [ID=166274 ]
