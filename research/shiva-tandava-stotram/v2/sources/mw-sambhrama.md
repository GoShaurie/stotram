 samBrama

 (H2)  [Printed book page 1179,3]

sam-Brama   m. (ifc. f(A). ) whirling round, haste, hurry, flurry, confusion, agitation, bustling  [ID=237065 ]

  activity, eagerness, zeal (At and ena, ‘excitedly, hurriedly’; acc. with √kf or √gam, and dat., ‘to get into a flurry about, show great eagerness or zeal’; with √tyaj or vi-√muc, ‘to compose one's self’), MBh.; Kāv. &c.  [ID=237066 ]

  awe, deference, respect, Kāv.; Kathās.  [ID=237067 ]

  error, mistake, delusion (ifc. = ‘feigning or seeming to be’), Rājat.; Kathās.  [ID=237068 ]

  grace, beauty (v.l. for vi-Brama), Bhartṛ.  [ID=237069 ]

  N. of a class of beings attending on Śiva, Cat.  [ID=237070 ]

sam-Brama   mfn. agitated, excited, rolling about (as the eyes), BhP.  [ID=237071 ]
