 vIci

 (H1)  [Printed book page 1004,3]

vIci   f. (prob. fr. vi + 2. aYc) going or leading aside or astray, aberration, deceit, seduction, RV. x, 10, 6  [ID=203444 ]

  also m. (L.) and f(I).  (ifc. ˚ci, or ˚cika) a wave, ripple, Kāv.; VarBṛS. &c.  [ID=203445 ]

  ‘wave’ as N. of a subdivision of a chapter (called pravAha, ‘river’), Sadukt.  [ID=203446 ]

  (prob. for a-vIci) a partic. hell, R. (L. also = suKa, avakASa, svalpa, alpa, Ali, kiraRa).   [ID=203447 ]
