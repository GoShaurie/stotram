 pAvaka

 (H2)  [Printed book page 623,2]

pAvaka   mf(A)n. pure, clear, bright, shining, RV.; VS.; AV. (said of Agni, Sūrya and other gods, of water, day and night &c.; according to native Comms. it is mostly = soDaka, ‘cleansing, purifying’)  [ID=123247 ]

pAvaka   m. N. of a partic. Agni (in the Purāṇas said to be a son of Agni Abhimānin and Svāhā or of Antardhāna and Śikhaṇḍinī), TS.; TBr.; KātyŚr.; Pur.  [ID=123248 ]

pAvaka   m. (ifc. f(A). ) fire or the god of fire, Up.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.  [ID=123249 ]

pAvaka   m. N. of the number 3 (like all words for ‘fire’, because fire is of three kinds See agni), Sūryas.  [ID=123250 ]

  a kind of Ṛṣi, a saint, a person purified by religious abstraction or one who purified from sin, MBh.  [ID=123251 ]
