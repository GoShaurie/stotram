 SeKara

 (H1)  [Printed book page 1088,2]

SeKara   m. (fr. or connected with SiKara) the top or crown of the head, Kathās.  [ID=220788 ]

  a chaplet or wreath of flowers worn on the top of the head, crown, diadem, crest, Hariv.; Kām.; Pur. &c.  [ID=220789 ]

  a peak, summit, crest (of a mountain), ib.; Rājat.  [ID=220790 ]

  (mostly ifc.) the highest part, chief or head or best or most beautiful of (-tA f.), Ṛt.; Caurap.; Dhūrtas.  [ID=220791 ]

  (in music) a partic. Dhruva or introductory verse of a song (recurring as a kind of refrain)  [ID=220792 ]

  N. of an author (with Bawwa), Cat.  [ID=220793 ]

  of a grammatical work, ib.  [ID=220794 ]
