Bram   cl. 1. P. (Dhātup. xx, 20) Bramati (ep. also ˚te) and cl. 4. P. (xxvi, 96), BrAmyati or Bramyati (Pot. BramyAt, PārGṛ.; pf. baBrAma, 3. pl. baBramuH or BremuH, MBh.; Kāv. &c.; fut. BramitA Gr.; Bramizyati, MBh.; aor. aBramIt, ib.; inf. Bramitum or BrAntum, MBh.; Kāv. &c.; ind.p. BramitvA, BrAntvA, -BrAmya or -Bramya, ib.),

to wander or roam about, rove, ramble (with deSam, to wander through or over a country; with BikzAm, go about begging), MBh.; Kāv. &c.;

to fly about (as bees), Kāv.; Var.;

to roll about (as the eyes), Kāvyād.;

to wag (as the tongue), ŚārṅgP.;

to quiver (as the fetus in the womb), BhP.;

to move to and fro or unsteadily, flicker, flutter, reel, totter, ŚBr.; Kālid.; Pur.;

to move round, circulate, revolve (as stars), MBh.; Hariv.; Sūryas.;

to spread, be current (as news), Daś.;

to waver, be perplexed, doubt, err, Bhag.; Pur.; Siddh. :

Pass. aor. aBrAmi (impers., with te, ‘you have wandered or roamed about’), R. :

Caus. BrAmayati or Bramayati (mc. also ˚te; aor. abiBramat:

Pass. BrAmyate or Bramyate), to cause to wander or roam, drive or move about, agitate, MBh.; Kāv. &c.;

 (with pawaham or ˚ha-GozaRAm), to move a drum about, proclaim by beat of drum, Kathās.;

to cause to move or turn round or revolve, swing, brandish, Up.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.;

to drive through (acc.) in a chariot, Cat.;

to disarrange, Kauś.;

to cause to err, confuse, Hariv.; MārkP.;

to move or roam about (aor. abiBramat ; B. abaBramat), R. :

Desid. biBramizati Gr.:

Intens. bamBramIti, bamBramyate (also with pass. meaning) and bamBrAnti (only Gr.), to roam about repeatedly or frequently, wander through, circumambulate, Hariv.; Var.; Śatr.   [ID=153524 ]
