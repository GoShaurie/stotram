[p. 854,2] 2. yu/j : mfn. (mostly ifc.; when uncompounded, the strong cases have a nasal, e.g. nom. yuṅ, yuñjau, yuñjas, but aśva-yuk &c., Pāṇ. vii, 1, 71) joined, yoked, harnessed, drawn by, RV. &c. &c. (cf. aśva-, hari-, hayottama-yuj)

[p. 854,2] : furnished or provided or filled with, affected by, possessed of (instr., mostly comp.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 854,2] : bestowing, granting (e.g. kāma-yuj, ‘g˚ wishes’), Hariv.

[p. 854,2] : exciting, an exciter (e.g. yuṇ bhiyaḥ, an exc˚ of fear), Bhaṭṭ.

[p. 854,2] : being in couples or pairs, even (not odd or separate), Lāṭy.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 854,3] yu/j : m. a yoke-fellow, companion, comrade, associate, RV.; AV.; Br.

[p. 854,3] : a sage who devotes his time to abstract contemplation, W.

[p. 854,3] : a pair, couple, the number ‘two’, Pañcar.

[p. 854,3] : du. the two Aśvins, L.

[p. 854,3] : (in astron.) the zodiacal sign Gemini.
