पूजावसानसमये दशवक्त्रगीतं
यः शम्भुपूजनपरं पठति प्रदोषे ।
तस्य स्थिरां रथगजेन्द्र तुरङ्गयुक्तां
लक्ष्मीं सदैव सुमुखीं प्रददाति शम्भुः ॥ १५ ॥

pūjāvasānasamayē daśavaktragītaṁ
yaḥ śambhupūjanaparaṁ paṭhati pradōṣē |
tasya sthirāṁ rathagajēndra turaṅgayuktāṁ
lakṣmīṁ sadaiva sumukhīṁ pradadāti śambhuḥ || 15 ||

puujaavasaana samaye dashavaktra giitam
yah shambhu puujana param pathati pradoshe |
tasya sthiraam ratha gajendra turanga yuktaam
lakshmiim sadaiva sumukhiim pradadaati shambhuh || ||

He who sings this song composed by the ten-headed one,
At the end of every worship or,
Reads it after worship of Shiva on the Pradosha day,
Will get the blessing of Lord Shiva, chariots, elephants and horses,
As well as the affectionate sight of the god of wealth.

पूजावसानसमये (pūjāvasānasamaye) – at the end of worship (pūjā:worship | avasāna:end | samaye:at the time); दशवक्त्रगीतं (daśavaktragītam) – song by the ten-headed (daśa:ten | vaktra:heads | gītam:song); यः (yaḥ) – who; शम्भुपूजनपरं (śambhupūjanaparam) – devoted to Shiva's worship (śambhu:Shiva | pūjana:worship | param:devoted to); पठति (paṭhati) – reads; प्रदोषे (pradōṣe) – on the Pradosha day; तस्य (tasya) – his; स्थिरां (sthirām) – steady; रथगजेन्द्रतुरङ्गयुक्तां (rathagajendraturaṅgayuktām) – with chariots, elephants, horses (ratha:chariot | gaja:elephant | indra:lord | turaṅga:horse | yuktām:endowed with); लक्ष्मीṁ (lakṣmīṁ) – wealth; सदैव (sadaiva) – always; सुमुखीं (sumukhīm) – gracious; प्रददाति (pradadāti) – grants; शम्भुः (śambhuḥ) – Shiva;
