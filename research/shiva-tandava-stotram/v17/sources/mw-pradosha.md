[p. 680,1] 1. pra-˚doṣa : mfn. (for 2. See col. 2) corrupt, bad, wicked, Śiś.

[p. 680,1] pra-˚doṣa : m. defect, fault, disordered condition (of the body or of a country), mutiny, rebellion, Pañcat.

[p. 680,2] 2. pra-doṣa : m. (for 1. See under pra-duṣ) the first part of the night, evening (also personified as a son of Doṣā and associated with Niśitha and Vyuṣṭa), MBh.; Kāv.; Pur. &c.
