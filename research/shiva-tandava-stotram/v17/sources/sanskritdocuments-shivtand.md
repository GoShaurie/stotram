पूजावसानसमये दशवक्त्रगीतं
  यः शम्भुपूजनपरं पठति प्रदोषे ।
तस्य स्थिरां रथगजेन्द्रतुरङ्गयुक्तां
  लक्ष्मीं सदैव सुमुखीं प्रददाति शम्भुः ॥ १७॥
(लक्ष्मीं प्रसादसमये प्रददाति शम्भुः)

[Page note after the text:] The stotra is in panchachAmara Chanda, in which there are 16 varna-s per line, each line begins with a laghu and the laghu and guru varna-s alternate. So there are eight LG (laghu-guru) pairs, making up 16 syllables of each line.  The last shloka is in Vasanta-tilakA metre.  Some of the versions of the stotra carry 15-19 verses.
