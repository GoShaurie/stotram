[p. 1259,2] stotra/ : n. praise, eulogium, a hymn of praise, ode, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 1259,2] : (in ritual) N. of the texts or verses which are sung (in contradistinction to the Śastras [q.v.] which are recited), TS.; Br.; ŚrS.
