[p. 853,1] yukta : yukti &c. See cols. 2, 3.

[p. 853,2] yukta/ : mfn. yoked or joined or fastened or attached or harnessed to (loc. or instr.), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 853,2] : set to work, made use of, employed, occupied with, engaged in, intent upon (instr. loc. or comp.), ib.

[p. 853,2] : ready to, prepared for (dat.), MBh.

[p. 853,2] : absorbed in abstract meditation, concentrated, attentive, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 853,2] : skilful, clever, experienced in, familiar with (loc.), MBh.; R.

[p. 853,2] : joined, united, connected, combined, following in regular succession, RV.; ŚāṅkhŚr.; Var.; BhP. (am ind. in troops, ŚBr.)

[p. 853,2] : furnished or endowed or filled or supplied or provided with, accompanied by, possessed of (instr. or comp.), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 853,3] : come in contact with (instr.), R.

[p. 853,3] : (in astron.) being in conjunction with (instr.), ĀśvGṛ.

[p. 853,3] : (ifc.) added to, increased by (e.g. catur-yuktā viṃśatiḥ, twenty increased by four i.e. 24), VarBṛS.

[p. 853,3] : (ifc.) connected with, concerning, KātyŚr.

[p. 853,3] : (ifc.) subject to, dependent on MBh.

[p. 853,3] : fitted, adapted, conforming or adapting one's self to, making use of (instr., e.g. yuktaḥ kālena yaḥ, one who makes use of the right opportunity), Kām.

[p. 853,3] : fit, suitable, appropriate, proper, right, established, proved, just, due, becoming to or suitable for (gen. loc. or comp., e.g. āyati-yukta, suitable for the future; or ibc. See below; yuktam with yad or an inf. = it is fit or suitable that or to; na yuktam bhavatā, it is not seemly for you), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 853,3] : auspicious, favourable (as fate, time &c.), Mn.; R.

[p. 853,3] : prosperous, thriving, R.

[p. 853,3] : (with tathā) faring or acting thus, MBh.

[p. 853,3] : (in gram.) primitive (as opp. to ‘derivative’), Pāṇ. i, 2, 51

[p. 853,3] yukta/ : m. N. of a son of Manu Raivata, Hariv.

[p. 853,3] : of a Ṛṣi under Manu Bhautya, ib.

[p. 853,3] yukta/ : n. a team, yoke, ŚBr.

[p. 853,3] : junction, connection, Pāṇ. ii, 3, 4; 8 &c.

[p. 853,3] : fitness, suitableness, propriety (am ind. fitly, suitably, justly, properly, rightly; e/na, properly, suitably, RV. v, 27, 3; buddhi-yuktena, conformably to reason, Rājat.)
