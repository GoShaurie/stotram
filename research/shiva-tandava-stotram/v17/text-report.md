## Verse 17

**Witness numbering:** A: 17 | A': 15 | B: unnumbered | C: 15 | D: 15 | E: 17

**Chosen reading:**

```
पूजावसानसमये दशवक्त्रगीतं
यः शम्भुपूजनपरं पठति प्रदोषे ।
तस्य स्थिरां रथगजेन्द्रतुरङ्गयुक्तां
लक्ष्मीं सदैव सुमुखीं प्रददाति शम्भुः ॥ १७ ॥
```

**Numbering:** A prints this verse as ॥ १७॥ and E as श्लोक १७. B prints it **unnumbered**, ending with a bare ॥. A', C and D print it as ॥ १५ ॥. In **every** witness it is the **last** verse, after इमं हि नित्यमेव…. Metre: vasantatilakā, 14 syllables per pāda (A: "The last shloka is in Vasanta-tilakA metre").

1. **Pāda 4, substantive**: "लक्ष्मीं सदैव सुमुखीं प्रददाति शम्भुः" is in A, A', C, D, E. "लक्ष्मीं प्रसादसमये प्रददाति शम्भुः" is in B, and A and A' also print it in parentheses after the verse as an alternative. Chosen **लक्ष्मीं सदैव सुमुखीं प्रददाति शम्भुः** (4 of 5).
2. **शम्भु / तुरङ्ग vs शंभु / तुरंग**: E writes anusvara. Orthographic only. bhaktibharat.com has सुमुखिं (a short i), which is a typo.
3. C prints dashes at the half-lines ("गीतं—यः", "युक्तां—लक्ष्मीं"). This is book layout.
