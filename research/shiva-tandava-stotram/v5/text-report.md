## Verse 5

**Witness numbering:** A: 5 | A': 5 | B: 5 | C: 5 | D: 5 | E: 5

**Chosen reading:**

```
सहस्रलोचनप्रभृत्यशेषलेखशेखर
प्रसूनधूलिधोरणीविधूसराङ्घ्रिपीठभूः ।
भुजङ्गराजमालया निबद्धजाटजूटकः
श्रियै चिराय जायतां चकोरबन्धुशेखरः ॥ ५ ॥
```

1. **श्रियै vs श्रिये** (pāda 4): श्रियै in A, A', C, D, E. श्रिये in B, and A prints "(श्रिये)" as a parenthetical alternative after the verse number. Chosen **श्रियै**. Both forms are datives of śrī. Drafters should confirm the grammar against a dictionary or grammar, not from memory.
2. **विधूसराङ्घ्रि vs विधूसरांघ्रि**: E writes anusvara. Orthographic only. Chosen ङ्घ्रि.
3. Spacing: D prints spaces inside compounds ("सहस्रलोचन प्रभृत्यशेषलेख शेखर", "निबद्ध जाटजूटकः", "चकोरबन्धु शेखरः"). Chosen: the majority word division.
4. The compound …शेखर- / प्रसून… runs across the pāda 1/2 break. A and C use a hyphen, E uses a spaced en dash "–", and B joins the two. Hyphen dropped.
