सहस्रलोचन प्रभृत्यशेषलेख शेखर
प्रसूनधूलि धोरणी विधूसराङ्घ्रि पीठभूः ।
भुजङ्गराजमालया निबद्ध जाटजूटकः
श्रियै चिराय जायतां चकोरबन्धु शेखरः ॥ ५ ॥

sahasralōcana prabhr̥tyaśēṣalēkha śēkhara
prasūnadhūli dhōraṇī vidhūsarāṅghri pīṭhabhūḥ |
bhujaṅgarājamālayā nibaddha jāṭajūṭakaḥ
śriyai cirāya jāyatāṁ cakōrabandhu śēkharaḥ || 5 ||

sahasra lochana prabhrtya shesha lekha shekhara
prasuuna dhuuli dhoranii vidhuusara anghri piitha bhuuh |
bhujanga raaja maalayaa nibaddha jaata juutakah
shriyai chiraaya jaayataam chakora bandhu shekharah ||  ||

May he whose foot stool is decorated
By the ever flowing flower dust.
Falling the bent head of Indra and other Gods,
And may He, whose matted locks are tied by the king of serpents,
And may he, whose head is decorated
By the crescent moon who a friend of Chakora*
Shower prosperity for ever on me.
* A mythical bird which lives by drinking moon light.

सहस्रलोचन (sahasralocana) – thousand-eyed (sahasra:thousand | locana:eyes); प्रभृत्य (prabhṛtya) – beginning with; अशेषलेख (aśeṣalekha) – all remaining (aśeṣa:all | lekha:remaining); शेखर (śekhara) – crown; प्रसूनधूलि (prasūnadhūli) – flower dust (prasūna:flower | dhūli:dust); धोरणी (dhoraṇī) – flowing; विधूसराङ्घ्रि (vidhūsarāṅghri) – dusty feet (vidhūsara:dusty | aṅghri:feet); पीठभूः (pīṭhabhūḥ) – foot stool (pīṭha:stool | bhūḥ:ground); भुजङ्गराजमालया (bhujaṅgarājamālayā) – by the king of serpents (bhujaṅga:serpent | rāja:king | mālā:garland); निबद्ध (nibaddha) – tied; जाटजूटकः (jāṭajūṭakaḥ) – matted locks (jāṭa:matted | jūṭaka:locks); श्रियै (śriyai) – for prosperity; चिराय (cirāya) – for a long time; जायतां (jāyatāṁ) – may he shower; चकोरबन्धु (cakōrabandhu) – friend of Chakora (cakōra:Chakora | bandhu:friend); शेखरः (śekharaḥ) – crown;
