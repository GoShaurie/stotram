pIWa

 (H1)  [Printed book page 629,1]
 
pIWa   n. (rarely f(I). ; possibly corrupted fr. pi-sad, to sit upon) a stool, seat, chair, bench, GṛS.; MBh. &c.  [ID=124607 ]

  
  a religious student's seat (made properly of Kuśa grass), W.  [ID=124608 ]

  
  case, pedestal (esp. of an idol), Rājat.; Var., Sch.  [ID=124609 ]

  
  royal seat, throne, RāmatUp.  [ID=124610 ]

  
  place, office (cf. pIWADikAra)  [ID=124611 ]

  
  N. of various temples (erected on the 51 spots where the limbs of Pārvatī fell after she had been cut to pieces by the discus of Viṣṇu), L.  [ID=124612 ]

  
  a district, province, Pañc.  [ID=124613 ]

  
  a partic. posture in sitting, Cat.  [ID=124614 ]

  
  (in geom.) the complement of a segment, Col.  [ID=124615 ]

  
pIWa   m. a kind of fish, L.  [ID=124616 ]

  
  the sun, Gal.  [ID=124617 ]

  
  N. of an Asura, MBh.  [ID=124618 ]

  
  of a minister of Kaṃsa, Hariv.  [ID=124619 ]
