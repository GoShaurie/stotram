 banDu

 (H2)  [Printed book page 721,1]

banDu   m. connection, relation, association, RV. &c. &c. (ifc. with f(U).  = belonging to, coming under the head of i.e. ‘being only in name’; cf. kzatra-, dvija-b˚ &c.; ‘resembling’, Bālar. v, 56/57 , ‘frequented by’, ib. iii, 20 , ‘favourable for’, ib. iv, 87 ; cf. Pāṇ. vi, 1, 14)  [ID=142731 ]

  respect, reference (kena banDunA ‘in what respect?’), ŚBr.  [ID=142732 ]

  kinship, kindred, Mn. ii, 136  [ID=142733 ]

  a kinsman (esp. on the mother's side), relative, kindred, RV. &c. &c. (in law, a cognate kinsman in a remote degree, one subsequent in right of inheritance to the Sa-gotra; three kinds are enumerated, personal, paternal and maternal)  [ID=142734 ]

  a friend (opp. to ripu), MBh.; Kāv.; BhP.  [ID=142735 ]

  a husband, Ragh.  [ID=142736 ]

  a brother, L.  [ID=142737 ]

  Pentapetes Phoenicea, L. (= banDUka)  [ID=142738 ]

  N. of a metre, Col.  [ID=142739 ]

  (in astrol.) of the fourth mansion, Var.  [ID=142740 ]

  of a Ṛṣi with the patr. Gaupāyana or Laupāyana (author of RV. v, 24 and x, 5660), RAnukr.  [ID=142741 ]

  of Manmatha, L.  [ID=142742 ]
