praBfti

 (H3)  [Printed book page 685,1]
 
pra-˚Bfti   (pra-), f. bringing forward, offering (of sacrifice or praise), RV.; AV.  [ID=135638 ]

  
  a throw or stroke, RV.  [ID=135638.1 ]

  
  beginning, commencement, ŚBr. &c. &c. (ifc. = ‘commencing with’ or ‘et caetera  [Correction:  et cætera ]’ e.g. munayaH somaSravaH-praBftayaH, ‘the Munis beginning with S˚’ i.e. ‘the Munis, S˚ &c.’; in this sense also ˚tika)  [ID=135638.2 ]

  
pra-˚Bfti   ind. (after an abl. adv. or ifc.) beginning with, from—forward or upward, since, GṛŚrS.; Mn.; MBh. &c. (e.g. bAlyAt praBfti, ‘from boyhood upwards’; janma-pr˚, ‘from birth’; adya pr˚, ‘beginning from today, henceforth’; tataH or tadA pr˚, ‘thenceforth’ &c.)  [ID=135638.3 ]
