[p. 656,3] pra-caṇḍa : mf(ā)n. excessively violent, impetuous, furious, fierce, passionate, terrible, direful, formidable, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 656,3] : great, large, hot, burning, sharp (see comp. below)

[p. 656,3] pra-caṇḍa : m. a species of oleander with white flowers, L.

[p. 656,3] : N. of a Dānava, Kathās.

[p. 656,3] : of a goblin, MārkP.

[p. 656,3] : of a son of Vatsa-prī and Su-nandā, ib.
