[p. 759,1] bhuja—ga : See bhujaga.

[p. 759,2] bhujaga : m. (fr. bhuja + ga) ‘going in curves’, a snake, serpent, serpent-demon (ifc. f(ā). ), MBh.; Kāv. &c. (-tva n., MBh.)

[p. 759,2] bhujaga : n. (prob.) tin or lead, Kālac.
