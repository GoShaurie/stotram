[p. 830,2] mṛdaṃga : m. (prob. fr. mṛdam + ga, ‘going about while being beaten’; cf. mardala and Uṇ. i, 120, Sch.) a kind of drum, tabour, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 830,2] : noise, din, L.

[p. 830,2] : a bamboo cane, L.
