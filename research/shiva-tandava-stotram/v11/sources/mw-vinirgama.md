[p. 970,3] vi-nirgama : m. going out, departure from (abl.), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 970,3] : being spread or divulged, spreading abroad, MārkP.

[p. 970,3] : the last of the three divisions of an astrological house, VarBṛS.
