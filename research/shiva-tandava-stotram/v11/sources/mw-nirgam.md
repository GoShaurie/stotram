[p. 554,2] nir-√ gam : P. -gacchati (Subj. -gamāṇi pf. p. -jaganvān, RV.; ind.p. -gatya, MBh. &c., -gamya, MārkP.),
to go out, come forth (often with bahis), depart from (abl.), set out, start, RV. &c. &c.;
to come out or appear (as a bud), Śak.;
to go away, disappear, Rājat.; Pañc.;
to enter into any state, undergo (acc.), MBh.;
 (with nidrām) to fall asleep, Kathās. :
Caus. -gamayati, to cause or order to set out, BhP. :
Desid. -jigamiṣate, to wish to set out, ib.
