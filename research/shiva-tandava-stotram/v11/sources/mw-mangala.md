[p. 772,3] maṅgala : n. (accord. to Uṇ. v, 70 fr. √ maṅg) happiness, felicity, welfare, bliss (also pl.; ifc. f(ā). ), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 772,3] : anything auspicious or tending to a lucky issue (e.g. a good omen, a prayer, benediction, auspicious ornament or amulet, a festival or any solemn ceremony on important occasions &c.; cf. mfn. below), Kauś.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 772,3] : a good old custom, PārGṛ.; Mn.

[p. 772,3] : a good work, MBh.; BhP.

[p. 772,3] : (in music) a partic. composition, Saṃgīt.

[p. 772,3] : N. of the capital of Udyāna, Buddh.

[p. 772,3] maṅgala : m. N. of Agni, Gṛhyās.

[p. 772,3] : of the planet Mars, L.

[p. 772,3] : of a king belonging to the race of Manu, Cat.

[p. 772,3] : of a Buddha, Lalit.

[p. 772,3] : of a poet, Cat.

[p. 772,3] : of a chief of the Cālukyas, ib.

[p. 772,3] : the smell of jasmine, L.
