जयत्वदभ्र विभ्रम भ्रमद्भुजङ्गमश्वस-
-द्विनिर्गमत् क्रमस्फुरत् कराल फालहव्यवाट् ।
धिमिद्धिमिद्धिमिध्वनन् मृदङ्ग तुङ्ग मङ्गल-
-ध्वनिक्रम प्रवर्तित प्रचण्ड ताण्डवः शिवः ॥ ११ ॥

jayatvadabhra vibhrama bhramadbhujaṅgamaśvasa-
-dvinirgamat kramasphurat karāla phālahavyavāṭ |
dhimiddhimiddhimidhvanan mr̥daṅga tuṅga maṅgala-
-dhvanikrama pravartita pracaṇḍa tāṇḍavaḥ śivaḥ || 11 ||

jayatvadabhra vibhrama bhramad bhujangama ashvasa-
-dvinirgamat krama sphurat karaala phaala havyavaat |
dhimi dhimi dhimi dhvanan mrdanga tunga mangala-
-dhvanikrama pravartita prachanda taandavah shivah || ||

Victory to the great Shiva, who has the fire burning in his forehead,
Which is increased by the breath of the snake wandering in the sky,
And to Him who dances to the changing tunes and fierce sound,
Of Dhimi, dhimi, dhimi coming out the auspicious drum.

जयतु (jayatu) – victory to; अदभ्र (adabhra) – great; विभ्रम (vibhrama) – wandering; भ्रमत् (bhramat) – moving; भुजङ्गम (bhujaṅgama) – snake; अश्वस (aśvasa) – breath; द्विनिर्गमत् (dvinirgamat) – coming out; क्रमस्फुरत् (kramasphurat) – increasing; कराल (karāla) – fierce; फाल (phāla) – forehead; हव्यवाट् (havyavāṭ) – fire; धिमिद्धिमिद्धिमिध्वनन् (dhimiddhimiddhimidhvanan) – sound of dhimi; मृदङ्ग (mr̥daṅga) – drum; तुङ्ग (tuṅga) – auspicious; मङ्गल (maṅgala) – auspicious; ध्वनिक्रम (dhvanikrama) – sound sequence (dhvani:sound | krama:sequence); प्रवर्तित (pravartita) – initiated; प्रचण्ड (pracaṇḍa) – fierce; ताण्डवः (tāṇḍavaḥ) – dance; शिवः (śivaḥ) – Shiva;
