[p. 1294,1] 1. havya/ : n. (for 2. See col. 2) anything to be offered as an oblation, sacrificial gift or food (in later language often opp., to kaivya, q.v.), RV.; &c.

[p. 1294,2] 2. ha/vya : (or havya/), mf(ā)n. to be called or invoked, RV.; AV.; VS.

[p. 1294,2] ha/vya : m. N. of a son of Manu Svāyambhuva, Hariv.

[p. 1294,2] : of a son of Atri, VP.
