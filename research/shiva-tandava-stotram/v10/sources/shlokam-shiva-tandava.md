अखर्व सर्वमङ्गला कलाकदम्बमञ्जरी
रसप्रवाह माधुरी विजृम्भणा मधुव्रतम् ।
स्मरान्तकं पुरान्तकं भवान्तकं मखान्तकं
गजान्तकान्धकान्तकं तमन्तकान्तकं भजे ॥ १० ॥

akharva sarvamaṅgalā kalākadambamañjarī
rasapravāha mādhurī vijr̥mbhaṇā madhuvratam |
smarāntakaṁ purāntakaṁ bhavāntakaṁ makhāntakaṁ
gajāntakāndhakāntakaṁ tamantakāntakaṁ bhajē || 10 ||

akharva sarva mangalaa kalaa kadamba manjarii
rasa pravaaha maadhurii vijrmbhanaa madhu vratam |
smara antakam pura antakam bhava antakam makha antakam
gaja antaka andhaka antakam tama antaka antakam bhaje || ||

I salute him who is like the bee who drinks the sweetened honey,
That flows from the flower bunch of collection of arts of the Goddess,
And him who destroyed God of love, the three cities,
The worldly problems and yaga destroyers,
And him who destroyed elephant faced Asura and also God of death.

अखर्व (akharva) – unwavering; सर्वमङ्गला (sarvamaṅgalā) – all-auspicious (sarva:all | maṅgalā:auspicious); कलाकदम्बमञ्जरी (kalākadambamañjarī) – cluster of arts (kalā:arts | kadamba:cluster | mañjarī:flower bunch); रसप्रवाह (rasapravāha) – flow of nectar (rasa:nectar | pravāha:flow); माधुरी (mādhurī) – sweetness; विजृम्भणा (vijṛmbhaṇā) – expansion; मधुव्रतम् (madhuvratam) – bee (madhu:honey | vratam:vow); स्मरान्तकं (smarāntakaṃ) – destroyer of love (smara:love | antakaṃ:destroyer); पुरान्तकं (purāntakaṃ) – destroyer of cities (pura:cities | antakaṃ:destroyer); भवान्तकं (bhavāntakaṃ) – destroyer of worldly problems (bhava:worldly problems | antakaṃ:destroyer); मखान्तकं (makhāntakaṃ) – destroyer of sacrifices (makha:sacrifices | antakaṃ:destroyer); गजान्तकान्धकान्तकं (gajāntakāndhakāntakaṃ) – destroyer of elephant-faced demon (gajāntaka:elephant-faced demon | andhaka:blind demon | antakaṃ:destroyer); तमन्तकान्तकं (tamantakāntakaṃ) – destroyer of death (tama:darkness | antaka:death | antakaṃ:destroyer); भजे (bhaje) – I worship;
