[p. 1042,2] vrata/ : n. (ifc. f(ā). ; fr. √ 2. vṛ) will, command, law, ordinance, rule, RV.

[p. 1042,2] : obedience, service, ib.; AV.; ĀśvGṛ.

[p. 1042,2] : dominion, realm, RV.

[p. 1042,2] : sphere of action, function, mode or manner of life (e.g. śuci-vr˚, ‘pure manner of life’, Śak.), conduct, manner, usage, custom, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 1042,2] : a religious vow or practice, any pious observance, meritorious act of devotion or austerity, solemn vow, rule, holy practice (as fasting, continence &c.; vrata/ṃ-√ car, ‘to observe a vow’, esp. ‘to practise chastity’), ib.

[p. 1042,2] : any vow or firm purpose, resolve to (dat. loc., or comp.; vratāt or vrata-vaśāt, ‘in consequence of a vow’; cf. asi-dhārā-vrata and āsidhāraṃ vratam), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1042,2] : the practice of always eating the same food (cf. madhu-vr˚), L.

[p. 1042,2] : the feeding only on milk (as a fast or observance according to rule; also the milk itself), VS.; Br.; KātyŚr.

[p. 1042,2] : any food (in a-yācita-vr˚, q.v.)

[p. 1042,2] : = mahā-vrata (i.e. a partic. Stotra, and the day for it), Br.; ŚrS.;

[p. 1042,2] : (with gen. or ifc.) N. of Sāmans, ĀrṣBr. (L. also ‘month’; season; year; fire; ‘= Viṣṇu’; ‘N. of one of the seven islands of Antara-dvīpa’)

[p. 1042,2] vra/ta : m. (of unknown meaning), AV. v, 1, 7; ĀpŚr. xiii, 16, 8

[p. 1042,2] : N. of a son of Manu and Naḍvalā, BhP.

[p. 1042,2] : (pl.) N. of a country belonging to Prācya, L.
