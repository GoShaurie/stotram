[p. 342,1] gaja : m. an elephant, ṢaḍvBr. v, 3; Mn. &c. (ifc. f(ā). , R. ii, 57, 7)

[p. 342,1] : (= dig-g˚) one of the 8 elephants of the regions, W.

[p. 342,1] : (hence) the number ‘eight’, Sūryas.

[p. 342,1] : a measure of length (commonly Gaz, equal to two cubits = 1 3/4 or 2 Hastas), L.

[p. 342,1] : a mound of earth (sloping on both sides) on which a house may be erected, Jyot.

[p. 342,1] : = -puṭa, q.v.

[p. 342,1] : (in music) a kind of measure

[p. 342,1] : N. of a man, MBh. vi, 3997

[p. 342,1] : of an Asura (conquered by Śiva), KāśīKh. lxviii

[p. 342,1] : of an attendant on the sun, L.
