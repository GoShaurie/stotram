[p. 634,2] 1. pu/r : f. (√ pṝ) only instr. pl. pūrbhi/s, in abundance, abundantly, RV. v, 66, 4.

[p. 634,2] 2. pur : cl. 6. P. purati, to precede, go before, lead, Dhātup. xxviii, 56 (prob. invented to furnish an etymology for puras and purā below).

[p. 635,2] 3. pu/r : f. (in nom. sg. and before consonants pūr) a rampart, wall, stronghold, fortress, castle, city, town (also of demons), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 635,2] : the body (considered as the stronghold of the puruṣa, q.v.), BhP.

[p. 635,2] : the intellect (= mahat), VP.

[p. 635,2] : N. of a Daśa-rātra, KātyŚr. [Perhaps fr. √ pṝ and orig. identical with 1. pur; cf. Gk., πόλις]
