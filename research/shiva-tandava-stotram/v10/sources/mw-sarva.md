[p. 1184,3] sa/rva : mf(ā)n. (perhaps connected with sāra, q.v.; inflected as a pronoun except nom. acc. sg. n. sarvam, and serving as a model for a series of pronominals, cf. sarva-nāman) whole, entire, all, every (m. sg. ‘every one’; pl. ‘all’; n. sg. ‘everything’; sometimes strengthened by viśva [which if alone in RV. appears in the meaning ‘all’, ‘every’, ‘every one’] and nikhila; sarve'pi, ‘all together’; sarvaḥ ko'pi, ‘every one so ever’; gavāṃ sarvam, ‘all that comes from cows’; sarva with a negation = ‘not any’, ‘no’, ‘none’ or ‘not every one’, ‘not everything’), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 1184,3] : of all sorts, manifold, various, different, MBh. &c.

[p. 1184,3] : (with another adjective or in comp.; cf. below) altogether, wholly, completely, in all parts, everywhere, RV.; ChUp. &c.

[p. 1184,3] sa/rva : m. (declined like a subst.) N. of Śiva, MBh.

[p. 1184,3] : of Kṛṣṇa, Bhag.

[p. 1184,3] : of a Muni, Cat.

[p. 1184,3] : pl. N. of a people, MārkP.

[p. 1185,1] sa/rva : n. water, Naigh. i, 12.

[p. 1185,1] sarva : [cf. Gk. ὅλος for ὁλϝος Lat. salvus.]
