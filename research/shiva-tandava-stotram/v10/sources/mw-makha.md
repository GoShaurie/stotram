[p. 772,1] 1. makha/ : mfn. (prob. connected with √ 1. mah or √ maṃh) jocund, cheerful, sprightly, vigorous, active, restless (said of the Maruts and other gods), RV.; Br.

[p. 772,1] makha/ : m. a feast, festival, any occasion of joy or festivity, RV.; ŚāṅkhGṛ.

[p. 772,1] : a sacrifice, sacrificial oblation, ŚBr. &c. &c. (Naigh. iii, 17)

[p. 772,1] : (prob.) N. of a mythical being (esp. in makhasya śiraḥ, ‘Makha's head’), RV.; VS.; ŚBr. (cf. also comp.)

[p. 772,1] 2. makha : m. or n. (?) the city of Mecca, Kālac.
