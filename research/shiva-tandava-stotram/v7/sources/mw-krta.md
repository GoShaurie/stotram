[p. 301,3] : well done, proper, good, ŚBr. iv

[p. 301,3] : cultivated, Mn. x, 114

[p. 301,3] : relating or referring to, Yājñ. ii, 210

[p. 301,3] kṛta/ : (as), m. N. of one of the Viśve Devās, MBh. xiii, 4356

[p. 301,3] : of a son of Vasu-deva, BhP. ix, 24, 45

[p. 301,3] : of a son of Saṃnati and pupil of Hiraṇya-nābha, Hariv. 1080; BhP. xii, 6, 80

[p. 301,3] kṛta/ : (am), n. (with saha or with instr.) ‘done with’, away with, enough of, no need of, &c. (e.g. kṛtaṃ saṃdehena, away with doubt, Śak.; kṛtaṃ parihāsena, enough of joking, ib.)

[p. 301,3] : the past tense, AitBr. v, 1

[p. 301,3] : (a/m), deed, work, action, RV.; AV.; ŚvetUp.; Mn. &c.

[p. 301,3] : service done, kind action, benefit (cf. kṛta-jña and -ghna), MBh. v, 1692; Pañcat.

[p. 301,3] : magic, sorcery, SāmavBr.

[p. 301,3] : consequence, result, L.

[p. 301,3] : aim, Vop. i, 2

[p. 301,3] : stake at a game, RV.; AV.

[p. 301,3] : prize or booty gained in battle, ib.

[p. 301,3] : N. of the die or of the side of a die marked with four points or dots (this is the lucky or winning die), VS. xxx, 18; TS.; ŚBr. &c.

[p. 301,3] : (also the collective N. of the four dice in opposition to the fifth die called kali Comm. on VS. x, 28)

[p. 301,3] : (hence) the number ‘four’, VarBṛS.; Sūryas.

[p. 301,3] : N. of the first of the four ages of the world (also called satya or ‘the golden age’, comprehending together with the morning and evening dawn 4800 years of men [Mn.; MBh.; Hariv.] or according to the later conception [BhP. &c. Comm. on Mn. i, 69] 4800 years of the gods or 1728000 years of men)
