[p. 1073,3] śi/lpa : n. (of doubtful derivation) the art of variegating, variegated or diversified appearance, decoration, ornament, artistic work, VS.; Br.; Hariv.; Kathās.; BhP.

[p. 1073,3] : any manual art or craft, any handicraft or mechanical or fine art (64 such arts or crafts, sometimes called bāhya-kalā, ‘external or practical arts’, are enumerated, e.g. carpentering, architecture, jewellery, farriery, acting, dancing, music, medicine, poetry &c. [cf. IW. 185]; and 64 abhyantara-kalā, ‘secret arts’ e.g. kissing, embracing, and various other arts of coquetry), ŚāṅkhBr.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1073,3] : skill in any art or craft or work of art, ingenuity, contrivance, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1073,3] : any act or work (also m.), BhP.

[p. 1073,3] : ceremonial act, ceremony, rite (also m.), MW.

[p. 1073,3] : form, shape, Naigh. iii, 7 (cf. su-śilpa)

[p. 1073,3] : a partic. kind of Śastra or hymn (of a highly artificial character, recited on the 6th day of the Pṛṣṭhya Ṣaḍ-aha, at the Viśvajit &c.), Br.; ŚrS.

[p. 1073,3] : a kind of sacrificial ladle (?), L.

[p. 1073,3] : (du. with jamad-agneḥ) N. of two Sāmans, ĀrṣBr.

[p. 1073,3] śi/lpa : m. N. of a teacher, ŚBr.

[p. 1073,3] śilpa/ : mfn. variegated, VS.; TS.
