[p. 581,3] pattra—bhaṅga, m. (Kād.), pattra—bhaṅgi (Dharmaśarm.) and pattra—bhaṅgī (Naiṣ.), f. : a decoration consisting in lines or streaks drawn on the face and body with musk and other fragrant substances.
