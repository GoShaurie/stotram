[p. 428,2] jval : cl. 1. P. jva/lati (ep. also Ā.; p. ˚lat; aor. ajvālīt, Pāṇ. vii, 2, 2; 3. pl. ajvaliṣur, Bhaṭṭ. xv, 106)
to burn brightly, blaze, glow, shine, TS. i; ŚBr.; Gobh.; MBh. &c.;
to burn (as a wound), Suśr. :
Caus. jvalayati or jvāl˚, to set on fire, light, kindle, make radiant, illuminate, GopBr. ii, 5, 5 (Ā.), MBh. &c.:
Intens. jājvalati (MBh.) or ˚lyate (Pāṇ. iii, 1, 22, Kāś.; p. ˚lyamāna)
to flame violently, shine strongly, be brilliant, MBh.; R.; VP. iii, 2, 10; Rājat. i, 154.
