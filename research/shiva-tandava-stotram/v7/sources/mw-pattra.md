[p. 581,2] pa/ttra : n. (and m., Śāk.; ifc. f(ā and ī). ) (sometimes spelt patra) the wing of a bird, pinion, feather, VS.; ŚBr. &c.

[p. 581,2] : the feather of an arrow, R.; Ragh.

[p. 581,2] : a bird, L.

[p. 581,2] : any vehicle, a chariot, car, horse, camel &c., Mn.; MBh.; Kāv.

[p. 581,2] : a leaf, petal (regarded as the plumage of a tree or flower), KātyŚr.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 581,2] : the leaf of a partic. fragrant plant or a partic. plant with f˚ leaves, VarBṛS. xvi, 30

[p. 581,2] : Laurus Cassia (L.) and its leaf, Bhpr.

[p. 581,2] : a leaf for writing on, written l˚, l˚ of a book, paper

[p. 581,2] : a letter, document, Kāv.; Rājat.; Pañc. (pattram āropya, ‘having committed to paper’, Śak.)

[p. 581,2] : any thin leaf or plate of metal or gold-leaf, Var.; Suśr. (cf. paṭṭa)

[p. 581,2] : the blade of a sword or knife (cf. asi-)

[p. 581,2] : a knife, dagger, L.

[p. 581,2] : = -bhaṅga, Ragh.; Gīt.
