कराल फालपट्‍टिका धगद्धगद्धगज्ज्वल-
-द्धनञ्जयाहुतीकृत प्रचण्ड पञ्चसायके ।
धराधरेन्द्र नन्दिनी कुचाग्र चित्रपत्रक-
-प्रकल्पनैकशिल्पिनि त्रिलोचने रतिर्मम ॥ ७ ॥

karāla phālapaṭ-ṭikā dhagaddhagaddhagajjvala-
-ddhanañjayāhutīkr̥ta pracaṇḍa pañcasāyakē |
dharādharēndra nandinī kucāgra citrapatraka-
-prakalpanaikaśilpini trilōcanē ratirmama || 7 ||

karaala phaala pat-tikaa dhagaddhagaddhagajjvala-
-ddhananjaya aahutiikrta prachanda pancha saayake |
dharaadharendra nandinii kuchaagra chitra patraka-
-prakalpana eka shilpini trilochane ratih mama || ||

May He in whose dreadful forehead, fire burns “Dhahaga”, “Dhaga,”
May He who burnt the one with five arrows* as an offering to fire,
May He who is the only one who can write decorative lines,
On the tip of the breasts of the daughter of the mountain,
And May He with three eyes makes mind enjoy in him.
* The God of love

कराल (karāla) – dreadful; फालपट्टिका (phālapaṭṭikā) – forehead; धगद्धगद्धगज्ज्वल (dhagaddhagaddhagajjvala) – burning with fire; धनञ्जयाहुतीकृत (dhanañjayāhutīkr̥ta) – offering made of Dhananjaya (dhanañjaya:Dhananjaya | āhutīkr̥ta:made as offering); प्रचण्ड (pracaṇḍa) – fierce; पञ्चसायके (pañcasāyake) – one with five arrows (pañca:five | sāyake:arrows); धराधरेन्द्र (dharādharēndra) – lord of mountains (dharā:earth | dhara:holding | indra:lord); नन्दिनी (nandinī) – daughter; कुचाग्र (kucāgra) – tip of the breasts (kuca:breast | agra:tip); चित्रपत्रक (citrapatraka) – decorative lines (citra:decorative | patraka:lines); प्रकल्पन (prakalpana) – creation; एकशिल्पिनि (ēkaśilpini) – only artist (ēka:only | śilpini:artist); त्रिलोचने (trilōcanē) – three-eyed one (tri:three | lōcana:eye); रतिः (ratiḥ) – pleasure; मम (mama) – my;
