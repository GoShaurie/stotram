[p. 262,2] 1. ka/lpa : mf(ā)n. (√ kḷp), practicable, feasible, possible, ŚBr. ii, 4, 3, 3

[p. 262,2] : proper, fit, able, competent, equal to (with gen. loc., inf., or ifc.; e.g. dharmasya kalpaḥ, competent for duty; svakarmaṇi na kalpaḥ, not competent for his own work; yadā na śāsituṃ kalpaḥ, if he is not able to rule), BhP.

[p. 262,2] ka/lpa : m. a sacred precept, law, rule, ordinance (= vidhi, nyāya), manner of acting, proceeding, practice (esp. that prescribed by the Vedas), RV. ix, 9, 7; AV. viii, 9, 10; xx, 128, 6-11; MBh.

[p. 262,2] : (prathamaḥ kalpaḥ, a rule to be observed before any other rule, first duty, Mn. iii, 147; MBh. &c.; etena kalpena, in this way; cf. paśu-k˚, &c.)

[p. 262,2] : the most complete of the six Vedāṅgas (that which prescribes the ritual and gives rules for ceremonial or sacrificial acts), MuṇḍUp.; Pāṇ. &c.

[p. 262,2] : one of two cases, one side of an argument, an alternative (= pakṣa; cf. vikalpa), Sarvad.

[p. 262,2] : investigation, research Comm. on Sāṃkhyak.

[p. 262,2] : resolve, determination, MW.

[p. 262,2] : (in medic.) treatment of the sick, manner of curing, Suśr. ii

[p. 262,2] : the art of preparing medicine, pharmacy, Car.

[p. 262,2] : the doctrine of poisons and antidotes, Suśr. i

[p. 262,1] ka/lpa : m. (ifc.) having the manner or form of anything, similar to, resembling, like but with a degree of inferiority, almost (e.g. abhedya-kalpa, almost impenetrable; cf. prabhāta-k˚, mṛta-k˚, &c.; according to native grammarians, kalpa so used is an accentless affix [Pāṇ. v, 3, 67], before which a final s is left unchanged, and final ī and ū shortened, Pāṇ.; Vop.; kalpam ind., may be also connected with a verb, e.g. pacati-kalpam, he cooks pretty well, Kāś. on Pāṇ. viii, 1, 57)

[p. 262,3] ka/lpa : m. a fabulous period of time (a day of Brahmā or one thousand Yugas, a period of four thousand, three hundred and twenty millions of years of mortals, measuring the duration of the world; a month of Brahmā is supposed to contain thirty such Kalpas; according to the MBh., twelve months of Brahmā constitute his year, and one hundred such years his lifetime; fifty years of Brahmā's are supposed to have elapsed, and we are now in the śvetavārāha-kalpa of the fifty-first; at the end of a Kalpa the world is annihilated; hence kalpa is said to be equal to kalpānta below, L.; with Buddhists the Kalpas are not of equal duration), VP.; BhP.; Rājat. &c.

[p. 262,3] : N. of Mantras which contain a form of √ kḷp, TS. v; ŚBr. ix

[p. 262,3] : a kind of dance

[p. 262,3] : N. of the first astrological mansion, VarBṛS.

[p. 262,3] : N. of a son of Dhruva and Bhrami, BhP. iv, 10, 1

[p. 262,3] : of Śiva, MBh. xii, 10368

[p. 262,3] : the tree of paradise

[p. 262,3] : = -taru below, L.

[p. 262,3] : (with Jainas) a particular abode of deities (cf. -bhava and kalpātīta below)

[p. 262,3] ka/lpa : n. a kind of intoxicating liquor (incorrect for kalya), L.

[p. 263,1] 2. kalpa : Nom. Ā. ˚pāyate, to become a Kalpa, to appear as long as a Kalpa, Hcar.
