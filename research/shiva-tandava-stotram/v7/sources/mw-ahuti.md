[p. 162,3] 1. ā/-huti : f. offering oblations with fire to the deities

[p. 162,3] : any solemn rite accompanied with oblations, RV.; AV.; TS.; AitBr.; ŚBr.; MBh. &c.

[p. 162,3] ā/-huti : m. N. of a son of Babhru, MBh.; Hariv.; VP.

[p. 163,2] 2. ā/-huti : f. calling, invoking [sometimes with this sense in the oldest Vedic texts, but See the more correct form ā-hūti]
