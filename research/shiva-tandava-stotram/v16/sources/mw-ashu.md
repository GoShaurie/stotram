[p. 157,3] āśu/ : mfn. (√ 1. aś, Uṇ. i, 1), fast, quick, going quickly, RV.; AV.; ŚBr. &c.

[p. 157,3] āśu : (us), m. Ved. the quick one, a horse, RV.; AV.

[p. 157,3] āśu/ : (us or u), m n. rice ripening quickly in the rainy season, ŚBr.; KātyŚr.; L.

[p. 157,3] āśu/ : (u), n. N. of a Sāman

[p. 157,3] āśu/ : (u), ind. quickly, quick, immediately, directly, Suśr.; Megh.; Pañcat. &c.

[p. 157,3] āśu : [cf. Gk. ὠκύς, ὤκιστος; Lat. acu in acupedius, ôcissimus: of the same origin may be the Lat. aquila and accipiter.]
