[p. 1272,1] smaraṇa : n. the act of remembering or calling to mind, remembrance, reminiscence, recollection of (gen. or comp.), MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 1272,1] : memory, BhP.

[p. 1272,1] : a kind of rhet. figure (cf. smṛti), Kpr.

[p. 1272,1] : handing down by memory, tradition, traditional teaching or record or precept (iti smaraṇāt, ‘from its being so mentioned in the Smṛti’ q.v.), Kull. : Yājñ., Sch.

[p. 1272,2] : mental recitation (of the name of a deity), calling upon the name of a god, MW.
