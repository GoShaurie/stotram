[p. 742,1] brū : cl. 2. P. Ā. (Dhātup. xxiv, 35) bra/vīti, brūte/ (only pr. stem; the other forms are supplied by √ vac, cf. Pāṇ. ii, 4, 53; brūmi for bravīmi, R.; Subj. bra/vas, ˚vat, RV.; Impv. brūhi, ep. also bravīhi, bruvadhvam; brūtāt, Pāṇ. vii, 1, 35, Sch.; impf. abruvam for abravam, Up.; MBh.; pr.p. Ā. ep. bruvamāṇa for bruvāṇa; Prec. 2. pl. brūyāsta, Nal. xvii, 36, prob. w.r. for brūyās tat),
to speak, say, tell (either intrans.; or with acc. of pers. or thing; or with acc. of thing and acc. dat. gen. or loc. of person = to tell or relate anything to; with two acc. also = declare or pronounce to be, call), RV. &c. &c.;
to speak about any person or thing (acc. with or without prati, or adhikṛtya), Mn.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.;
to proclaim, predict, Var.;
to answer (either intrans. with punar or trans. with praśnam, ‘a question’), Mn.; MBh.;
 (with anyathā) to speak or decide or judge wrongly, Mn.; Pañcat.;
 (Ā., rarely P.) to call or profess one's self to be (nom., rarely with iti), RV.; Br.; MBh.;
 (Ā.) to designate for one's self, choose, AitBr.;
 (Ā.) to be told by itself, tell itself (tell its tale), Pāṇ. iii, 1, 89, Vārtt. 1, Pat.
