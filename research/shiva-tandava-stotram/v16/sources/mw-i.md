[p. 163,2] 1. i : the third vowel of the alphabet, corresponding to i short, and pronounced as that letter in kill &c.

[p. 163,3] 2. i : ind. an interjection of anger, calling, sorrow, distress, compassion, &c., (g. cādi, Pāṇ. i, 4, 57, &c.)

[p. 163,3] 3. i : base of Nom. and Acc. sg. du. and pl. of the demonstrative pronoun idam, ‘this’ or ‘that’

[p. 163,3] i : [cf. i/tara, itas, iti, i/d, idā/, iyat, iva, iha: cf. also Lat. id; Goth. ita; Eng. it; Old Germ. iz; Mod. Germ. es.]

[p. 163,3] 4. i : m. N. of Kāmadeva, L.
