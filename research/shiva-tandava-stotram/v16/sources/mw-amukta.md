[p. 82,1] a-mukta : mfn. not loosed, not let go, not liberated from birth and death, not liberated from Rāhu, still eclipsed, Viṣṇus.

[p. 82,1] a-mukta : n. a weapon that is always grasped and not thrown (as a knife, a sword, &c.)
