[p. 299,1] 1. kuhū/ : ūs, f. (fr. √ kuh = guh?), the new moon (personified as a daughter of Aṅgiras), AV.; Kāṭh.; TS.; AitBr. &c.

[p. 299,1] : the first day of the first quarter (on which the moon rises invisible), W.

[p. 299,1] : N. of one of the seven rivers of Plakṣa-dvīpa, VP.; BhP. v, 20, 10.

[p. 299,1] 2. kuhū : ind. = 2. kuhu.
