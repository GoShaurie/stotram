नवीनमेघमण्डली निरुद्ध दुर्धर स्फुरत्
कुहू निशीथिनी तमः प्रबन्ध बद्ध कन्धरः ।
निलिम्प निर्झरीधरस्तनोतु कृत्तिसिन्धुरः
कलानिधान बन्धुरः श्रियं जगद्धुरन्धरः ॥ ८ ॥

navīnamēghamaṇḍalī niruddha durdhara sphurat
kuhū niśīthinī tamaḥ prabandha baddha kandharaḥ |
nilimpa nirjharīdharastanōtu kr̥ttisindhuraḥ
kalānidhāna bandhuraḥ śriyaṁ jagaddhurandharaḥ || 8 ||

naviina megha mandalii niruddha durdhara sphurat
kuhuu nishiithinii tamah prabandha baddha kandharah |
nilimpa nirjharii dharah tanotu krtti sindhurah
kalaa nidhaana bandhurah shriyam jagat dhurandharah || ||

May He whose black neck is as dark
As several layers of new clouds,
Packed closely on the night of the new moon.
May He who wears the celestial river on his head,
May He who killed the Gajasura with an elephant head,
May He who is very handsome because of the crescent that he wears,
And may he who carries the entire burden of the world,
Bless us with all sorts of wealth.

नवीनमेघमण्डली (navīnameghamaṇḍalī) – new cloud formation (navīna:new | megha:cloud | maṇḍalī:formation); निरुद्ध (niruddha) – restrained; दुर्धर (durdhara) – difficult to hold; स्फुरत् (sphurat) – shining; कुहू (kuhū) – new moon night; निशीथिनी (niśīthinī) – night; तमः (tamaḥ) – darkness; प्रबन्ध (prabandha) – bound; बद्ध (baddha) – tied; कन्धरः (kandharaḥ) – neck; निलिम्प (nilimpa) – celestial; निर्झरीधरः (nirjharīdharah) – river bearer (nirjharī:river | dharaḥ:bearer); स्तनोतु (stanotu) – may bless; कृत्तिसिन्धुरः (kr̥ttisindhuraḥ) – elephant head (kr̥tti:head | sindhuraḥ:elephant); कलानिधान (kalānidhāna) – crescent moon (kalā:crescent | nidhāna:repository); बन्धुरः (bandhuraḥ) – handsome; श्रियम् (śriyam) – wealth; जगद्धुरन्धरः (jagaddhurandharaḥ) – world bearer (jagat:world | dhurandharaḥ:bearer);
