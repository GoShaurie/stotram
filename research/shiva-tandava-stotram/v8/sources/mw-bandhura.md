[p. 721,2] : curved, rounded, pleasant, beautiful, charming, Inscr.; Kālid.; Caurap.

[p. 721,2] : (ifc.) adorned with, Kād.

[p. 721,2] : undulating, uneven, L.

[p. 721,2] : deaf, L. (cf. badhira)

[p. 721,2] : injurious, mischievous, W.

[p. 721,2] bandhura : m. (only L.) a bird

[p. 721,2] : a goose

[p. 721,2] : Pentapetes Phoenicea

[p. 721,2] : Embelia Ribes

[p. 721,2] : a partic. bulbous plant growing on the Hima-vat mountain, L.

[p. 721,2] : oil-cake

[p. 721,2] : the vulva, L.

[p. 721,2] bandhura : n. a diadem, crest, L.
