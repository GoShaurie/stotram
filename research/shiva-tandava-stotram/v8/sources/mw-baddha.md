[p. 719,3] baddha : &c. See p. 720, col. 2.

[p. 720,2] baddha/ : mfn. bound, tied, fixed, fastened, chained, fettered, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 720,2] : captured, imprisoned, caught, confined, ib. (śatāt, ‘for a debt of a hundred’, Pāṇ. ii, 3, 24, Sch.)

[p. 720,2] : bound by the fetters of existence or evil, Kap.

[p. 720,2] : hanged, hung, R.

[p. 720,2] : tied up (as a braid of hair), Megh.

[p. 720,2] : (ifc.) stopped, checked, obstructed, impeded, restrained, suppressed, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 720,2] : girt with, ŚāṅkhŚr.

[p. 720,2] : (with instr. or ifc.) inlaid or studded with, set in MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 720,2] : attached to, riveted or fixed on (loc.), ib.

[p. 720,2] : joined, united, combined, formed, produced, ib.

[p. 720,2] : composed (as verses), R.

[p. 720,2] : (esp. ibc.; cf. below) conceived, formed, entertained, manifested, shown, betrayed, visible, apparent (cf. jāta ibc.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 720,2] : clenched (as the fist), Hariv.; R.

[p. 720,2] : folded (as the hands), Mṛcch.

[p. 720,2] : contracted (as friendship or enmity), R.; Śak.

[p. 720,2] : taken up (as an abode), Rājat.

[p. 720,2] : built, constructed (as a bridge), R.; Ragh.

[p. 720,2] : embanked (as a river), Rājat.

[p. 720,2] : congealed, clotted (as blood; opp. to drava), Suśr.

[p. 720,2] : alloyed (as quicksilver), L.

[p. 720,2] baddha/ : m. or n. ? (with Jainas) that which binds or fetters the embodied spirit (viz. the connection of the soul with deeds), MW.
