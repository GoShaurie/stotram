[p. 548,3] ni-˚dhā/na : n. putting or laying down, depositing, keeping, preserving, KātyŚr.; MBh. &c.

[p. 548,3] : laying aside (cf. daṇḍa-n˚)

[p. 548,3] : placing (the sacrificial fire), KātyŚr.

[p. 548,3] : place for depositing anything, receptacle (rarely m.; ifc. f(ī). ; cf. garbha-n˚), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 548,3] : a place of cessation or rest, W.

[p. 548,3] : anything laid up, a store, hoard, treasure (esp. the t˚ of Kubera), Mn.; Mṛcch.; Ragh. &c. (-tā f., Jātakam.)

[p. 548,3] ni-˚dhā/na : mfn. containing anything (gen.) in itself, TĀr.
