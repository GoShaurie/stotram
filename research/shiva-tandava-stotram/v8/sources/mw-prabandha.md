[p. 683,1] pra-˚bandha : m. a connection, band, tie (garbha-nāḍī-prab˚, the umbilical cord), Suśr.

[p. 683,1] : an uninterrupted connection, continuous series, uninterruptedness, continuance, Hariv.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 683,1] : a composition, (esp.) any literary production, Kāv.; Rājat.; Pratāp.

[p. 683,1] : a commentary, Naiṣ., Sch.
