[p. 11,1] añjali/ : See s.v. below.

[p. 11,1] añjali/ : m. (√ añj), the open hands placed side by side and slightly hollowed (as if by a beggar to receive food; hence when raised to the forehead, a mark of supplication), reverence, salutation, benediction

[p. 11,1] : a libation to the Manes (two hands full of water, udakāñjali), VP. &c.

[p. 11,1] : a measure of corn, sufficient to fill both hands when placed side by side, equal to a kuḍava.
