[p. 893,2] 1. lagna : mfn. (for 2. See p. 895, col. 2) adhered, adhering or clinging to, attached to, sticking or remaining in, fixed on, intent on, clasping, touching, following closely (with gen. or ifc.), MBh.; Kāv. &c. (with pṛṣṭhe, pṛṣṭha-tas; or pṛṣṭha ibc., following on a person's heels; with mārge, sticking to i.e. following the road; with hṛdaye, one who has penetrated the heart)

[p. 893,2] : one who has entered on a course of action, one who has begun to (inf.), Pañcat.

[p. 893,2] : meeting, intersecting, cutting (said of lines), Gol.

[p. 893,2] : immediately ensuing, Pañcat.

[p. 893,2] : passed (as days), Vet.

[p. 893,2] : consumed by, spent in (instr.), Kull. on Mn. vii, 127

[p. 893,2] : auspicious (see comp.)

[p. 893,2] : furious through being in rut (an elephant), L.

[p. 893,2] lagna : m. a bard or minstrel (who awakes the king in the morning), L.

[p. 893,2] lagna : m. n. (ifc. f(ā). ) the point of contact or intersection (of two lines), the point where the horizon intersects the ecliptic or path of the planets, Sūryas.; VarBṛS.

[p. 893,2] lagna : m. the point or arc of the equator which corresponds to a given point or arc of the ecliptic, ib.

[p. 893,2] : the division of the equator which rises in succession with each sign in an oblique sphere, ib.

[p. 893,2] : the point of the ecliptic which at a given time is upon the meridian or at the horizon (kṣitije lagnam, horizon Lagna; madhya-lagnam, meridian Lagna), ib.

[p. 893,2] : the moment of the sun's entrance into a zodiacal sign, ib.

[p. 893,2] : the rising of the sun or of the planets, ib.

[p. 893,2] : (in astrol.) a scheme or figure of the 12 houses or zodiacal signs (used as a horoscope)

[p. 893,2] : the whole of the first astrological house

[p. 893,2] : (also with śubha, śobhana, anukūla &c.) an auspicious moment or time fixed upon as lucky for beginning to perform anything, Rājat.; Kathās.; Hit.

[p. 893,3] : the decisive moment or time for action, decisive measure, Kathās.

[p. 893,3] 1. and 2. lagna . : See col. 2 and p. 895, col. 2.

[p. 895,2] 2. lagna : mfn. (also referable to 1. laj; for 1. lagna See p. 893, col. 2) ashamed, Pāṇ. vii, 2, 14, Sch.
