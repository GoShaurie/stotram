[p. 907,3] loc : (connected with √ ruc and √ lok) cl. 1. Ā. (Dhātup. vi, 3) locate (pf. luloce; fut. locitā &c. Gr.),
to see, behold, perceive (see ā- and nir-√ loc) :
Caus. or cl. 10. P. locayati (aor. alulocat), to speak;
to shine (bhāṣārthe, or bhāsārthe), Dhātup. xxxiii, 104 :
Desid. lulociṣate Gr.:
Intens. lolocyate, ib.
