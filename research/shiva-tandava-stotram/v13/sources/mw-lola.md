[p. 905,1] lola : mf(ā)n. moving hither and thither, shaking, rolling, tossing, dangling, swinging, agitated, unsteady, restless, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 905,1] : changeable, transient, inconstant, fickle, Kāv.; Kathās.

[p. 905,1] : desirous, greedy, lustful, (ifc.) eagerly desirous of or longing for (loc. inf. or comp.), Kāv.; Var. &c.

[p. 905,2] lola : m. the penis, Gal.

[p. 905,2] : N. of a man, MārkP.

[p. 908,2] lola : lolita &c. See p. 905, cols. 1, 2.
