[p. 95,1] 1. ali : m. (for alin, fr. ala, q.v.) ‘possessed of a sting’, a (large black) bee, Ragh. &c.

[p. 95,1] : a scorpion, L.

[p. 95,1] : a crow, L.

[p. 95,1] : the Indian cuckoo, L.

[p. 95,1] : spirituous liquor, L.

[p. 95,1] 2. ali : m. (in the Apabhraṃśa dialect) for ari, enemy (pl. alayas for arayas), Pat.

[p. 95,1] : also ala/vas is mentioned as a corrupt pronunciation for ara/yas, ŚBr.
