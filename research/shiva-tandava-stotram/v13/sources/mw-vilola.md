[p. 986,1] vi-lola : mf(ā)n. moving to and fro or from side to side, rolling, waving, tremulous, unsteady, Kāv.; Kathās. &c.

[p. 986,1] : unsteadier than (abl.), Subh.

[p. 986,2] vi-lola : vi-lolana, vi-lolita. See col. 1.
