[p. 172,3] ucca : mfn. (said to be fr. ca fr. √ añc with 1. ud), high, lofty, elevated

[p. 172,3] : tall, MBh.; Kum.; Śiś.; Kathās. &c.

[p. 172,3] : deep, Caurap.

[p. 172,3] : high-sounding, loud, Bhartṛ.; VarBṛS.

[p. 172,3] : pronounced with the Udātta accent, RPrāt.; VPrāt. &c.

[p. 172,3] : intense, violent, R.

[p. 172,3] ucca : m. height, MBh.

[p. 172,3] : the apex of the orbit of a planet, Kālas, R. &c.

[p. 172,3] : compar. ucca-tara, superl. ucca-tama;

[p. 172,3] ucca : [cf. Hib. uchdan, ‘a hillock’; Cambro-Brit. uched, ‘cleve’.]
