[p. 1261,3] stha . : See p. 1262, col. 3.

[p. 1262,3] stha : mf(ā)n. (or ṣṭha) (only ifc.) standing, staying, abiding, being situated in, existing or being in or on or among (see agni-, garbha-, jala-, naraka-, rājya-stha &c.)

[p. 1262,3] : occupied with, engaged in, devoted to performing, practising (see dhyāna-, yajña-, yoga-, savana-stha &c.)

[p. 1262,3] : a place, ground (ibc. = sthala), L.
