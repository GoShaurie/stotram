[p. 486,2] dur—mati/ : f. bad disposition of mind, envy, hatred, RV.; VS.; AV.

[p. 486,2] : false opinion or notions, Cāṇ.

[p. 486,2] dur—mati/ : mfn. weak-minded, silly, ignorant (rarely ‘malicious’, ‘wicked’)

[p. 486,2] dur—mati/ : m. fool, blockhead (rarely ‘scoundrel’, ‘villain’), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 486,2] : N. of the 55th year of the cycle of Jupiter (lasting 60 years), Var.; Sūryas.

[p. 486,2] : of a demon, Lalit.

[p. 486,2] : of a blockhead, Bharat.
