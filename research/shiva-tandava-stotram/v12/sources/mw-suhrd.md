[p. 1239,2] su—hṛd : &c. See s.v.

[p. 1239,3] su-hṛd : m. ‘good-hearted’, ‘kindhearted’, ‘well-disposed’, a friend, ally (also said of planets; suhṛdo janāḥ, ‘friends’), ŚrS.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1239,3] : N. of the fourth astrol. mansion, VarBṛS.

[p. 1239,3] su-hṛd : f. a female friend, Gobh.; Kauś.

[p. 1239,3] su-hṛd : mfn. (only ifc.) fond of, liking or devoted to, Bālar.

[p. 1239,3] : very similar to, closely resembling, ib.
