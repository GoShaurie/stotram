[p. 573,2] pakṣa/ : m. (ifc. f(ā or ī). ) a wing, pinion (in one passage n.), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 573,2] : a symbol. N. of the number two, Var.; Hcat.

[p. 573,2] : a feather, the feathers on both sides of an arrow (cf. gārdhra-p˚)

[p. 573,2] : the fin of a fish (cf. nis-tvak-p˚)

[p. 573,2] : the shoulder

[p. 573,2] : the flank or side or the half of anything, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 573,1] : the side or wing of a building, AV.

[p. 573,3] : the wing or flank of an army, MBh.; Hariv.

[p. 573,3] : the half of a lunar month (the first half from new moon to full moon was called pūrva or āpūryamāṇa, later śukla or śuddha; the other half apara or apa-kṣīyamāṇa, later kṛṣṇa or tāmisra; each fortnight consists of 15 Tithis or lunar days called prathamā, dvitīyā &c.), Br.; GṛŚrS.; MBh.; Var. &c.

[p. 573,3] : a side, party, faction

[p. 573,3] : multitude, number, troop, set, class of beings

[p. 573,3] : partisan, adherent, follower, friend (śatru- ‘the enemy's side’ or ‘a partisan of the enemy’; mahā-, ‘one who has many adherents’), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 573,3] : side i.e. position, place, stead (˚kṣe ifc. instead of or by way of), ib.

[p. 573,3] : quantity (see keśa-)

[p. 573,3] : one of two cases or one side of an argument, an alternative (˚kṣe, ‘on the other hand’, with atra, ‘in this case’, pakṣāntare, ‘in the other case’), Pāṇ., Sch.

[p. 573,3] : a point or matter under discussion, a thesis, a particular theory, a position advanced or an argument to be maintained (cf. pūrva-, uttara-)

[p. 573,3] : an action or lawsuit, Yājñ., Sch.

[p. 573,3] : (in logic) the proposition to be proved in a syllogism, Tarkas.; Bhāṣāp.

[p. 573,3] : any supposition or view, motion, idea, opinion (mukhyaḥ pakṣaḥ, ‘an excellent idea’, Śak., Sch.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 573,3] : the sun, Sāy. on RV. iii, 53, 16

[p. 573,3] : N. of sev. men, VP.

[p. 573,3] : (in alg.) a primary division or the side of an equation in a primary division

[p. 573,3] : the wall of a house or any wall, L.

[p. 573,3] : an army, L.

[p. 573,3] : favour, L.

[p. 573,3] : contradiction, rejoinder, L.

[p. 573,3] : the ash-pit of a fireplace, L.

[p. 573,3] : a royal elephant, L.

[p. 573,3] : a limb or member of the body, L.

[p. 573,3] : the feathers of the tail of a peacock, a tail, L.
