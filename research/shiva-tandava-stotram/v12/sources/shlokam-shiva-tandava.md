दृषद्विचित्र तल्पयोर्भुजङ्ग मौक्तिक स्रजो-
-र्गरिष्ठ रत्नलोष्ठयोः सुहृद्विपक्ष पक्षयोः ।
तृणारविन्द चक्षुषोः प्रजामही महेन्द्रयोः
समप्रवृत्तिकः कदा सदाशिवं भजाम्यहम् ॥ १२ ॥

dr̥ṣadvicitra talpayōrbhujaṅga mauktika srajō-
-rgariṣṭha ratnalōṣṭhayōḥ suhr̥dvipakṣa pakṣayōḥ |
tr̥ṇāravinda cakṣuṣōḥ prajāmahī mahēndrayōḥ
samapravr̥ttikaḥ kadā sadāśivaṁ bhajāmyaham || 12 ||

drshad vichitra talpayor bhujanga mauktika srajo-
-r garishtha ratna loshthayoh suhrd vipaksha pakshayoh |
trna aravinda chakshushoh prajaa mahii mahendrayoh
sama pravrttikah kadaa sadaashivam bhajaami aham ||  ||

When will I be able to worship that eternal shiva,
With a feeling of equanimity towards snake and a garland,
Towards great gems and dirt or friends and enemies,
Or Towards a blade of grass and lotus like eyes,
Or emperor and ordinary men.

दृषद्विचित्र (dṛṣadvicitra) – varied stones (dṛṣad:stone | vicitra:varied); तल्पयोः (talpayoḥ) – of the beds; भुजङ्ग (bhujaṅga) – snake; मौक्तिक (mauktika) – pearl; स्रजः (srajaḥ) – garland; गरिष्ठ (gariṣṭha) – greatest; रत्नलोष्ठयोः (ratnaloṣṭhayoḥ) – of gems and clods (ratna:gem | loṣṭha:clod); सुहृद्विपक्ष (suhr̥dvipakṣa) – friend and foe (suhr̥d:friend | vipakṣa:foe); पक्षयोः (pakṣayoḥ) – of the sides; तृणारविन्द (tṛṇāravinda) – grass and lotus (tṛṇa:grass | aravinda:lotus); चक्षुषोः (cakṣuṣoḥ) – of the eyes; प्रजामही (prajāmahī) – subjects and earth (prajā:subjects | mahī:earth); महेन्द्रयोः (mahendrayoḥ) – of the great Indra; समप्रवृत्तिकः (samapravṛttikaḥ) – equanimous; कदा (kadā) – when; सदाशिवं (sadāśivam) – eternal Shiva; भजामि (bhajāmi) – worship; अहम् (aham) – I;
