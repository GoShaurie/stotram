[p. 1152,1] 1. sama : mfn. (connected with 7. sa and with 2. sama/ and samāna; cf. samaha, used as pron.: declined like sarva, e.g. samasmai, RV. vi, 51, 6) any, every, RV.

[p. 1152,1] sama : [cf. Gk. ἁμός, ἀμός; Goth. suma; Angl.Sax. sum; Eng. some.]

[p. 1152,1] 2. sama/ : mf(ā/)n. (prob. originally identical with prec.; cf. samāna) even, smooth, flat, plain, level, parallel (karṇa-s˚, ‘on a level with the ear’; bhūmi- or bhūmeḥ samaṃ-√ kṛ, ‘to make level with the earth’), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 1152,1] : same, equal, similar, like, equivalent, like to or identical or homogeneous with (instr., e.g. mayā sama, ‘like to me’; or gen., rarely abl.), like in or with regard to anything (instr. gen. loc., or -tas, or comp.; samaṃ-√ kṛ, ‘to make equal, balance’), ib.

[p. 1152,1] : always the same, constant, unchanged, fair, impartial towards (loc. or gen.), ib.

[p. 1152,1] : even (not ‘odd’), a pair, VarBṛS.

[p. 1152,1] : having the right measure, regular, normal, right, straight (samaṃ-√ kṛ, ‘to put right or in order’), AitBr. &c. &c.

[p. 1152,1] : equable, neutral, indifferent, VarBṛS.

[p. 1152,1] : equally distant from extremes, ordinary, common, middling, Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1152,1] : just, upright, good, straight, honest, ib.

[p. 1152,1] : easy, convenient, Pañcat.

[p. 1152,1] : full, complete, whole, entire, L.

[p. 1152,1] sama/ : m. peace (perhaps w.r. for śama), R.; Kām.

[p. 1152,1] : the point of intersection of the horizon and the meridian line, Gol.

[p. 1152,1] : N. of partic. zodiacal signs (esp. Vṛṣa, Karkaṭa, Kanyā, Vṛścika, Makara, and Mīna), MW.

[p. 1152,1] : a kind of straight line placed over a numerical figure to mark the process of extracting the square root, ib.

[p. 1152,1] : (in music) a kind of time, Saṃgīt.

[p. 1152,1] : a grass-conflagration, L.

[p. 1152,1] : a Jina, Gal.

[p. 1152,1] : N. of a son of Dharma, VP.

[p. 1152,1] : of a son of Dhṛtarāṣṭra, MBh.

[p. 1152,1] : of a king of the Nandi-vegas (v.l. śama), ib.

[p. 1152,1] sama/ : (am), n. level ground, a plain (same/ bhū/myāḥ, ‘on level ground’), AV.; ŚBr.; Mn.; MBh.; R.

[p. 1152,1] : equability, equanimity, imperturbability, MBh.

[p. 1152,1] : likeness, similarity, equality (ena, ‘equally, in the same manner’), Kāś. on Pāṇ. ii, 3, 18

[p. 1152,1] : right measure or proportion (ena, ‘exactly, precisely’), ŚBr.

[p. 1152,1] : settlement, compensation, Mn. viii, 177

[p. 1152,1] : good circumstances, Mṛcch.

[p. 1152,1] : (in rhet.) a partic. figure, sameness of objects compared to one another, Pratāp.; Kuval.

[p. 1152,1] : (in geom.) a mean proportional segment (described as a fourth proportional to the two perpendiculars and the link or segment, and used for solving problems in a trapezium), Col.
