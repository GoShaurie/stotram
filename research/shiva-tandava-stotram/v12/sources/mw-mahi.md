[p. 794,1] mahī/ : (ī/), f. See mahī/, p. 803, col. 2.

[p. 803,2] 1. mahī/ : f. (cf. 2. ma/h), ‘the great world’, the earth (cf. urvī, pṛthivī), RV. &c. &c. (in later language also = ground, soil, land, country)

[p. 803,2] : earth (as a substance), Mn. vii, 70

[p. 803,2] : the base of a triangle or other plane figure, Col.

[p. 803,2] : space, RV. iii, 56, 2; v, 44, 6 &c.

[p. 803,2] : a host, army, ib. iii, 1, 12; vii, 93, 5 &c.

[p. 803,2] : a cow, RV.; VS. (Naigh. ii, 11)

[p. 803,2] : du. heaven and earth, RV. i, 80, 11; 159, 1 &c. (Naigh. iii, 30)

[p. 803,2] : pl. waters, streams, RV. ii, 11, 2; v, 45, 3 &c.

[p. 803,2] : Hingtsha Repens, L.

[p. 803,2] : a kind of metre, Col.

[p. 803,2] : N. of a divine being (associated with Iḍā and Sarasvatī, RV. i, 13, 9; Sāy.; cf. Naigh. i, 11)

[p. 803,2] : of a river, MBh.; Hariv.

[p. 803,2] : of the number ‘one’, Gaṇit.

[p. 803,3] 2. mahī : in comp. for maha.
