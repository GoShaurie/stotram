[p. 759,1] bhuja : m. (ifc. f(ā). ) the arm, MBh.; Kāv. &c. (bhujayor antaram, the breast, Bhartṛ.; cf. bhujāntara)

[p. 759,1] : the hand, Pāṇ. vii, 3, 61

[p. 759,1] : the trunk of an elephant, MBh. iii, 15736

[p. 759,1] : a branch, bough, BhP.

[p. 759,1] : a bending, curve, coil (of a serpent; see comp. below)

[p. 759,1] : the side of any geometrical figure, KātyŚr., Sch.

[p. 759,1] : the base of a triangle, Sūryas.

[p. 759,1] : the base of a shadow, ib.

[p. 759,1] : the supplement of 2 or 4 right angles or the complement of 3 right angles, MW.
