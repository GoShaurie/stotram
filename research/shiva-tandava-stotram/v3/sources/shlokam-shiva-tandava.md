धराधरेन्द्र नन्दिनी विलासबन्धु बन्धुर
स्फुरद्दिगन्त सन्तति प्रमोद मानमानसे ।
कृपाकटाक्षधोरणी निरुद्ध दुर्धरापदि
क्वचिद्दिगम्बरे मनोविनोदमेतु वस्तुनि ॥ ३ ॥

dharādharēndra nandinī vilāsabandhu bandhura
sphuraddiganta santati pramōda mānamānasē |
kr̥pākaṭākṣadhōraṇī niruddha durdharāpadi
kvaciddigambarē manōvinōdamētu vastuni || 3 ||

dharaadharendra nandinii vilaasa bandhu bandhura
sphurad diganta santati pramoda maana maanase |
krpaa kataaksha dhoranii niruddha durdhara aapadi
kvachid digambare manovinodam etu vastuni ||  ||

The consort of the ever sportive daughter of the mountain,
Whose mind rejoices at her side long glances,
With the stream of merciful look which removes hardships,
Makes my mind take pleasure in him who wears the directions as apparel.

धराधरेन्द्र (dharādharendra) – lord of mountains (dharā:earth | dhara:bearer | indra:lord); नन्दिनी (nandinī) – daughter; विलासबन्धु (vilāsabandhu) – companion in play (vilāsa:play | bandhu:companion); बन्धुर (bandhura) – charming; स्फुरद्दिगन्त (sphuraddiganta) – shining horizon (sphurat:shining | diganta:horizon); सन्तति (santati) – continuity; प्रमोद (pramoda) – joy; मानमानसे (mānamānase) – in the mind of pride (māna:pride | mānase:in the mind); कृपाकटाक्षधोरणी (kṛpākaṭākṣadhoraṇī) – stream of merciful glance (kṛpā:mercy | kaṭākṣa:glance | dhoraṇī:stream); निरुद्ध (niruddha) – restrained; दुर्धरापदि (durdharāpadi) – in difficult situations (durdhara:difficult | āpadi:situations); क्वचित् (kvacit) – somewhere; दिगम्बरे (digambare) – sky-clad (dik:direction | ambare:clad); मनोविनोदमेतु (manovinodametu) – may delight the mind (manaḥ:mind | vinoda:delight | etu:may); वस्तुनि (vastuni) – in the object;
