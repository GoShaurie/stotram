 (H1)  [Printed book page 553,3]

ni-√ruD   P. Ā. -ruRadDi, -rundDe, to hold back, stop, hinder, shut up, confine, restrain, check, suppress, destroy, RV. &c. &c.; 

to keep away, ward off, remove, RV.; Br. ; 

to surround or invest (a place), Rājat.; BhP.; 

to close (lit. ‘a door’ or fig. ‘heart, mind’ &c.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.; 

to catch or overtake, Mṛcch. i, 20  : 

Caus. -roDayati, to shut or cause to be shut, Rājat.  [ID=109411 ]
