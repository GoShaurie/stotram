 vilAsa

 (H2)  [Printed book page 985,1]

vi-lAsa   m. (ifc. f(A). ) shining forth, appearance, manifestation, Ṛt.; Gīt.  [ID=199655 ]

  sport, play, pastime, pleasure, diversion (esp. with women &c.; but also applied to any playful action or gesture), MBh.; Kāv. &c. (˚sAya, ‘for sport’)  [ID=199656 ]

  coquetry, affectation of coyness, wantonness (a form of feminine gesture considered as indicative of amorous sentiments), Hariv.; Kāv.; Daśar.; Sāh.  [ID=199657 ]

  liveliness, joviality (considered as a masculine virtue), Daśar. ii, 9   [ID=199658 ]

  wantonness, lust, Daśar.; Sāh.  [ID=199659 ]

  grace, charm, beauty, BhP.  [ID=199660 ]

  N. of a gram. wk.  [ID=199661 ]

  (with AcArya) of a preceptor, Cat.  [ID=199662 ]
