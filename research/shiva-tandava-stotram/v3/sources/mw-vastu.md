   [Printed book page 932,3]

2. vastu   n. (for 1. See  p. 931, col. 3) the seat or place of (see kapila-, vraRa-v˚)  [ID=189124 ]

  any really existing or abiding substance or essence, thing, object, article, Kāv.; VarBṛS.; Kathās. &c. (also applied to living beings, e.g. Śak. ii, 7/8 )  [ID=189125 ]

  (in phil.) the real (opp. to a-v˚, ‘that which does not really exist, the unreal’; a-dvitIya-v˚, ‘the one real substance or essence which has no second’), IW. 53 n. 1 ; 103  &c.  [ID=189126 ]

  the right thing, a valuable or worthy object, object of or for (comp.), Kāv.  [ID=189127 ]

  goods, wealth, property (cf. -vinimaya and -hAni)  [ID=189128 ]

  the thing in question, matter, affair, circumstance, MBh.; Kāv. &c.  [ID=189129 ]

  subject, subject matter, contents, theme (of a speech &c.), plot (of a drama or poem), Kāv.; Daśar.; Sāh. &c.  [ID=189130 ]

  (in music) a kind of composition, Saṃgīt.  [ID=189131 ]

  natural disposition, essential property, W.  [ID=189132 ]

  the pith or substance of anything, W.  [ID=189133 ]
