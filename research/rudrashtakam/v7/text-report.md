This file is the v7 slice of `fulltext/text-report.md`; witnesses, sigla and conventions are explained there. Sigla: G = `gitapress-ramcharitmanas` (Gita Press, Hindi tr.), K = `kashiraj-ramcharitmanas` (Kāśirāja critical ed.), B = `belvedere-1926-ramcharitmanas`, S = `sanskritdocuments-rudra8` (S-2007 = its 2007 encoding), E = `greenmesg-rudrashtakam` (F = `shlokam-rudrashtakam` votes with E), W = `wikisource-sa-rudrashtakam` (no vote). Independent votes: G, K, B, S, E.

Meter: bhujaṅgaprayāta (LGG LGG LGG LGG, 12 syllables a pāda). Vidyut scan of the chosen text: **pāda a scans LGL LGG LGG LGG.** In यावद् उमा° the d joins the next vowel, so syllable 3 is light. All witnesses share this; G, K, E, F and W print the virāma and a space (S-2007: yAvat.h), marking a recitation pause that makes it heavy. Pādas b–d are regular. Expect T3.1 to flag v7a.

## Chosen text

न यावद् उमानाथपादारविन्दं
भजन्तीह लोके परे वा नराणाम् ।
न तावत्सुखं शान्ति सन्तापनाशं
प्रसीद प्रभो सर्वभूताधिवासम् ॥ ७ ॥

## Variants

- a: न यावद् उमानाथपादारविन्दं (K E; न यावद् उमानाथ पादारविन्दं G W). S and B write न यावदुमानाथ° joined. Spelling of the same words; see the meter note.
- b: भजन्तीह लोके (G S E; भजंतीह K W). B: भजन्तीहलोके (joined). Same.
- c: **शान्ति सन्तापनाशं** (G S E W) vs **शान्तिसन्तापनाशं** (K B). **Slight sense difference.** Spaced: no happiness, no peace, no end of torment. G's Hindi follows this: "न तो इहलोक और परलोकमें सुख-शान्ति मिलती है और न उनके तापोंका नाश होता है". Joined: "the end of peace-and-torment", which is awkward. The majority reading is chosen.
- d: सर्वभूताधिवासम् in all.
