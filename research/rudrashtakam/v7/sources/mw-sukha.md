[p. 1220,3] su—kha/ : &c., see sukha/ s.v.

[p. 1220,3] sukha/ : mfn. (said to be fr. 5. su + 3. kha, and to mean originally ‘having a good axle-hole’; possibly a Prākṛt form of su-stha, q.v.; cf. duḥkha) running swiftly or easily (only applied to cars or chariots, superl. sukha/-tama), easy, RV.

[p. 1220,3] : pleasant (rarely with this meaning in Veda), agreeable, gentle, mild (comp. -tara/), VS. &c. &c.

[p. 1220,3] : comfortable, happy, prosperous (= sukhin), R.

[p. 1220,3] : virtuous, pious, MW.

[p. 1220,3] sukha/ : m. N. of a man g. śivādi

[p. 1220,3] : (scil. daṇḍa) a kind of military array, Kām.

[p. 1221,1] sukha : (am), n. ease, easiness, comfort, prosperity, pleasure, happiness (in m. personified as a child of Dharma and Siddhi, MārkP.), joy, delight in (loc.; sukham-√ kṛ ‘to give pleasure’; mahatā sukhena, ‘with great pleasure’), the sky, heaven, atmosphere (cf. 3. kha), L.

[p. 1221,1] sukha/ : n. water, Naigh. i, 12

[p. 1221,1] : N. of the fourth astrol. house, VarBṛS.

[p. 1221,1] : the drug or medicinal root called Vṛddhi, MW.
