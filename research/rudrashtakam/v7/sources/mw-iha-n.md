[p. 169,3] iha/ : ind. (fr. pronom. base 3. i), in this place, here

[p. 169,3] : to this place

[p. 169,3] : in this world

[p. 169,3] : in this book or system

[p. 169,3] : in this case (e.g. teneha na, ‘therefore not in this case’ i.e. the rule does not apply here)

[p. 169,3] : now, at this time, RV. &c. &c.;
