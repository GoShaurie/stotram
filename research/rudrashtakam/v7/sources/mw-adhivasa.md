[p. 22,1] 1. adhi-vāsa/ [ŚBr.] or adhī-vāsa/ [RV.; ŚBr. &c.], as, m. or 1. adhī-vāsas [Vait.], as, n. : an upper garment, mantle.

[p. 22,1] 2. adhi-vāsa : m. an inhabitant

[p. 22,1] : a neighbour

[p. 22,1] : one who dwells above

[p. 22,1] : a habitation, abode, settlement, site

[p. 22,1] : sitting before a person's house without taking food till he ceases to oppose or refuse a demand (commonly called ‘sitting in dharṇā’)

[p. 22,1] : pertinacity.

[p. 22,1] 3. adhi-vāsa : m. perfume, fragrance

[p. 22,1] : application of perfumes or fragrant cosmetics.
