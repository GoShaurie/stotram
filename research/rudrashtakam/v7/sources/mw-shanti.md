[p. 1064,3] śā/nti : f. tranquillity, peace, quiet, peace or calmness of mind, absence of passion, averting of pain (śānti! śānti! śānti! may the three kinds of pain be averted!), indifference to objects of pleasure or pain, KaṭhUp.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1064,3] : alleviation (of evil or pain), cessation, abatement, extinction (of fire &c.), AV. &c. &c.

[p. 1064,3] : a pause, breach, interruption, Hcat.

[p. 1064,3] : any expiatory or propitiatory rite for averting evil or calamity, Br. &c. (cf. RTL. 346)

[p. 1064,3] : peace, welfare, prosperity, good fortune, ease, comfort, happiness, bliss, MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 1064,3] : destruction, end, eternal rest, death, Kāv.; Kathās.; BhP.

[p. 1064,3] : = śānti-kalpa, BhP.

[p. 1064,3] : Tranquillity &c. personified (as a daughter of Śraddhā, as the wife of Atharvan, as the daughter of Dakṣa and wife of Dharma), Hariv.; Prab.; Pur.

[p. 1064,3] śā/nti : m. N. of a son of Indra, MBh.

[p. 1064,3] : of Indra in the tenth Manv-antara, Pur.

[p. 1064,3] : of a Tuṣita (son of Viṣṇu and Dakṣiṇā), ib.

[p. 1064,3] : of a son of Kṛṣṇa and Kālindī, ib.

[p. 1064,3] : of a Ṛṣi, MBh.

[p. 1064,3] : of a son of Aṅgiras, ib.

[p. 1064,3] : of a disciple of Bhūti, MārkP.

[p. 1064,3] : of a son of Nīla and father of Su-śānti, VP.

[p. 1064,3] : (with Jainas) of an Arhat and Cakra-vartin, L.

[p. 1064,3] : of a teacher (also called ratnākara-ś˚), Buddh.
