[p. 538,1] 1. nāśa : m. (√ 1. naś) attainment (see dūṇ˚).

[p. 538,1] 2. nāśa : m. (√ 2. naś) the being lost, loss, disappearance, destruction, annihilation, ruin, death, Br.; Mn.; MBh. &c. (ifc. destroying, annihilating, cf. karma-nāśā, graha-nāśa, duḥ-svapna-n˚)

[p. 538,1] : flight, desertion, W.

[p. 538,1] : (arithm.) elimination, ib.
