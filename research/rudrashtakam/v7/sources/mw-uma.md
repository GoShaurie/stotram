[p. 217,1] u/mā : f. (perhaps fr. √ ve, BRD.) flax (Linum Usitatissimum), ŚBr. vi; Kauś.; Pāṇ.

[p. 217,1] : turmeric (Curcuma Longa), Car.

[p. 217,1] : N. of the daughter of Himavat (wife of the god Śiva; also called Pārvatī and Durgā; the name is said to be derived from u mā, ‘O [child], do not [practise austerities]’ the exclamation addressed to Pārvatī by her mother), Hariv. 946; ŚivaP.; Kum. i, 26; R.; Ragh. &c.

[p. 217,1] : N. of several women

[p. 217,1] : fame, reputation, L.

[p. 217,1] : quiet, tranquillity, L.
