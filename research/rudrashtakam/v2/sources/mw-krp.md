[p. 305,1] 1. kṛ/p : f. (only instr. kṛpā/) beautiful appearance, beauty, splendour, RV.; VS. iv, 25.

[p. 305,1] 2. kṛp : cl. 6. Ā. kṛpate (impf. akṛ-panta; aor. 3. pl. akṛpran and cakṛpa/nta, 3. sg. akrapiṣṭa; pr. p. kṛ/pamāṇa),
to mourn, long for (acc.), RV.;
to lament, implore, RV.; AV. v, 19, 3 :
cl. 10. P. kṛpayati (impf. akṛpayat; p. kṛpa/yat gen. sg. m. kṛpayata/s, RV. viii, 46, 16),
to mourn, grieve, lament (with acc.), RV.;
to pity, BhP. viii, 7, 40;
to be weak, Dhātup. xxxv, 17 (cf. kṛpāya and √ krap.)
