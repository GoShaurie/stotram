[p. 355,1] 1. giri : loc. of 1. gi/r.

[p. 355,1] 2. giri : f. id. g. kṛṣy-ādi.

[p. 355,2] 3. giri/ : m. (for gari, Zd. gairi cf. guru/, ga/rīyas; ifc. Pāṇ. vi, 2, 94) a mountain, hill, rock, elevation, rising-ground (often connected with pa/rvata, ‘a mountain having many parts’ [cf. pa/rvan] RV.; AV.), RV. &c.

[p. 355,2] : the number ‘eight’ (there being 8 mountains which surround mount Meru), Śrut.

[p. 355,2] : a cloud, Naigh. i, 10; Nir.; Sāy.

[p. 355,2] : a particular disease of the eyes, Pāṇ. vi, 2, 2, Sch. (kiri, Kāś.), Uṇ.

[p. 355,2] : = -guḍa, L.

[p. 355,2] : a peculiar defect in mercury, L.

[p. 355,2] : = gairīyaka, L.

[p. 355,2] : a honorific N. given to one of the ten orders of the Daś-nāmī Gosains (founded by ten pupils of Śaṃkarācārya; the word giri is added to the name of each member; cf. gairika)

[p. 355,2] : N. of a son of Śvaphalka, VP.

[p. 355,2] giri/ : f. (= girikā) a mouse, L., Sch.

[p. 355,2] giri/ : mfn. coming from the mountains, RV. vi, 66, 11

[p. 355,2] giri/ : f. venerable, L. (R. iv, 37, 2, Sch.);

[p. 355,2] giri : [cf. Slav. gora; Afghan. ghur.]
