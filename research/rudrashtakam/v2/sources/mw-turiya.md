[p. 451,1] 1. turīya : Nom. ˚ya/ti, to go, Naigh. ii, 14.

[p. 451,1] 2. turī/ya : mfn. (for ktur˚ [Zend khtuiria] fr. catu/r) (Pāṇ. v, 2, 51, Vārtt. 1) Ved. 4th, RV. &c.

[p. 451,1] : consisting of 4 parts, ŚBr. ix

[p. 451,1] turī/ya : n. the 4th state of spirit (pure impersonal Spirit or Brahma), Up. (MaitrUp.; NṛsUp. ii, 2, 1 &c.; RTL. 35), Vedāntas.

[p. 451,1] turī/ya : mfn. being in that state of soul, NṛsUp.

[p. 451,1] : tu/r˚, a 4th, constituting the 4th part

[p. 451,1] tu/rīya : n. a 4th part, AV.; Kāṭh. &c. (with yantra, ‘a quadrant’, Śaṃkar. xxvii).
