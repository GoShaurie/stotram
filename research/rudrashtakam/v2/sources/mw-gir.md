[p. 355,1] 1. gi/r : mfn. (√ 1. gṝ) addressing, invoking, praising, RV.

[p. 355,1] gi/r : f. (ī/r) invocation, addressing with praise, praise, verse, song, RV. (the Maruts are called ‘sons of praise’, sūna/vo gi/raḥ, i, 37, 10), AV.

[p. 355,1] : speech, speaking, language, voice, words (e.g. mānuṣīṃ giraṃ √ 1. kṛ, to assume a human voice, Nal. i, 25; girāṃ prabhaviṣṇuḥ [VarBṛS.] or pati [VarYogay.] = gir-īśa, q.v.; tad-girā, on his advice, Kathās. lxxv), ChUp.; Mn.; Yājñ.; MBh. &c.

[p. 355,1] : = gīr-devī, fame, celebrity, W.

[p. 355,1] : a kind of mystical syllable, RāmatUp.;

[p. 355,1] gir : [cf. Hib. gair, ‘an outcry, shout’; Gk. γῆρυς.]

[p. 355,1] 2. gir : mfn. (√ 2. gṝ) ifc. ‘swallowing’, see gara- and muhur-gi/r.

[p. 355,1] 3. gi/r : m. = giri/, a mountain, RV. v, 41, 14 and vii, 39, 5; Śiś. iv, 59.
