[p. 619,2] 1. pāra/ : mfn. (fr. √ pṛ; in some meanings also fr. √ pṝ) bringing across, RV. v, 31, 8

[p. 619,2] pāra/ : n. (rarely m.) the further bank or shore or boundary, any bank or shore, the opposite side, the end or limit of anything, the utmost reach or fullest extent, RV. &c. &c. (dūre/ pāre/, at the farthest ends, RV.; pāraṃ-√ gam &c. with gen. or loc., to reach the end, go through, fulfil, carry out [as a promise], study or learn thoroughly [as a science] MBh.; R. &c.; pāraṃ-√ nī, to bring to a close, Yājñ.)

[p. 619,2] : a kind of Tuṣṭi (s.v.), Sāṃkhyas. Sch.

[p. 619,2] pāra/ : m. crossing (see duṣ- and su-)

[p. 619,2] : quicksilver, L.

[p. 619,2] : a partic. personification, SāmavBr.; Gaut.

[p. 619,2] : N. of a sage, MārkP.

[p. 619,2] : of a son of Pṛthu-ṣeṇa (Rucirāśva) and father of Nīpa, Hariv.

[p. 619,2] : of a s˚ of Samara and f˚ of Pṛthu, ib.

[p. 619,2] : of a son of Aṅga, and f˚ of Divi-ratha, VP.

[p. 619,2] : (pl.) of a class of deities under the 9th Manu, BhP.

[p. 620,1] 2. pāra : (for 1. See p. 619), Vṛddhi form of para in comp.

[p. 620,1] 3. pāra : m. = pāla, a guardian, keeper (see brahma-dvāra-p˚).
