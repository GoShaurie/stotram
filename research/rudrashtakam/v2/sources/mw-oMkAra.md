[p. 236,1] oṃ—kāra : (oṃ-k˚), m. the sacred and mystical syllable om, the exclamation om, pronouncing the syllable om, Mn. ii, 75; 81; Kathās.; Bhag. &c., (cf. vijayoṃkāra, kṛtoṃkāra)

[p. 236,1] : a beginning, prosperous or auspicious beginning of (e.g. a science), Bālar.

[p. 236,1] : N. of a Liṅga
