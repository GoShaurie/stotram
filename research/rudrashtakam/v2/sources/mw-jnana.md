[p. 426,1] jñāna : n. knowing, becoming acquainted with, knowledge, (esp.) the higher knowledge (derived from meditation on the one Universal Spirit), ŚāṅkhŚr. xiii; Gobh.; Mn. &c.

[p. 426,1] : ‘knowledge about anything, cognizance’, see -tas & a- (jñānād a-jñānād vā, knowingly or ignorantly, xi, 233)

[p. 426,1] : conscience, MBh.

[p. 426,1] : = ˚nendriya, KaṭhUp. vi, 10

[p. 426,1] : engaging in (gen., e.g. sarpiṣas, ‘in sacrifice with clarified butter’), Pāṇ. ii, 2, 10, Vārtt., Pat.

[p. 426,1] : N. of a Śakti, Rasik. xiv, 36; RāmatUp. i, 90, Sch.
