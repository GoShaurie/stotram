[p. 653,1] pra-˚kāśam : ind. openly, publicly, before the eyes of all, Mn.; MBh. &c. (˚śaṃ nābhyudaikṣata, ‘he did not look up openly’, R.)

[p. 653,1] : aloud, audibly (esp. in dram., opp. to ātma-gatam, sva-gatam &c.)
