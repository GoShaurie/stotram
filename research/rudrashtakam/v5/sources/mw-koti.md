[p. 312,3] koṭi : f. the curved end of a bow or of claws, &c., end or top of anything, edge or point (of a sword), horns or cusps (of the moon), MBh. &c.

[p. 312,3] : the highest point, eminence, excellence, Pañcat.; Ratnāv.; Sarvad.

[p. 312,3] : ‘a point or side in an argument or disputation’, (if there are two) ‘alternative’, see -dvaya below

[p. 312,3] : the highest number in the older system of numbers (viz. a Krore or ten millions), Mn.; Yājñ.; MBh. &c.

[p. 312,3] : the complement of an arc to 90 degrees

[p. 312,3] : the perpendicular side of a right-angled triangle, Sūryas.

[p. 312,3] : Medicago esculenta, L.
