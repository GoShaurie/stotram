[p. 826,2] mū/la : n. (or m. g. ardharcādi; ifc. f(ā or ī). ; prob. for 3. mū/ra See above) ‘firmly fixed’, a root (of any plant or tree; but also fig. the foot or lowest part or bottom of anything), RV. &c. &c. (mūlaṃ √ kṛ or bandh, to take or strike root)

[p. 826,2] : a radish or the root of various other plants (esp. of Arum Campanulatum, of long pepper, and of Costus Speciosus or Arabicus), L.

[p. 826,2] : the edge (of the horizon), Megh.

[p. 826,2] : immediate neighbourhood (mama mūlam = to my side), R.

[p. 826,2] : basis, foundation, cause, origin, commencement, beginning (mūlād ārabhya or ā mūlāt, from the beg˚; mūlāt, from the bottom, thoroughly; mūlaṃ kramataś ca, right through from beginning, Divyāv.), Mn.; MBh. &c. (ibc. = chief, principal, cf. below; ifc. = rooted in, based upon, derived from)

[p. 826,2] : a chief or principal city, ib.

[p. 826,2] : capital (as opp. to ‘interest’), SāmavBr.; Prab.

[p. 826,2] : an original text (as opp. to the commentary or gloss), R.; Kathās.; Suśr.

[p. 826,2] : a king's original or proper territory, Mn. vii, 184

[p. 826,2] : a temporary (as opp. to the rightful) owner, Mn. viii, 202

[p. 826,2] : an old or hereditary servant, a native inhabitant, MW.

[p. 826,2] : the square root, Sūryas.

[p. 826,2] : a partic. position of the fingers (= mūta-bandha), Pañcar.

[p. 826,2] : a copse, thicket, L.

[p. 826,2] mū/la : n. also m. and f(ā). N. of the 17th (or 19th) lunar mansion, AV. &c. &c.

[p. 826,2] mū/la : m. herbs for horses, food, Divyāv.

[p. 826,2] : N. of Sadā-śiva, Cat.

[p. 826,2] mū/la : mfn. original, first, Cat.

[p. 826,2] : = nija, own, proper, peculiar, L.
