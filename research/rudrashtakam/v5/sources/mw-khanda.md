[p. 336,1] khaṇḍa : mf(ā)n. broken, having chasms or gaps or breaks, Suśr.; VarBṛS.; Pāṇ. ii, 1, 30, Kāś.

[p. 336,1] : deficient, defective, crippled (cf. ṣaṇḍa), Āp.; ŚāṅkhŚr. xvi, 18, 18, Sch.

[p. 336,1] : (in comp. or ifc. Pāṇ. ii, 2, 38; Pat.)

[p. 336,1] : not full (as the moon), KātyŚr., Sch.; Subh.

[p. 336,1] khaṇḍa : m. n. (g. ardharcādi) ‘a break or gap’, cf. kedāra-kh˚

[p. 336,1] : a piece, part, fragment, portion, R.; Suśr.; Megh. &c. (indoḥ kh˚ or tārādhipa-kh˚ [cf. also khaṇḍendu] ‘the crescent’, Prasannar.)

[p. 336,1] : treacle or molasses partially dried, candied sugar, Bhpr.; Naiṣ.; Sāh.

[p. 336,1] : a section of a work, part, chapter (e.g. of AitĀr.; KenUp. &c.)

[p. 336,1] : a continent, Gaṇit.

[p. 336,1] : (in alg.) a term in an equation, Gaṇit.

[p. 336,1] khaṇḍa : m. n. a party, number, multitude, assemblage, MBh. (sometimes not to be distinguished from ṣaṇḍa), R. i, 30, 15 &c. (ifc. m. or n. cf. Kāś. on Pāṇ. iv, 2, 38 and 51)

[p. 336,1] khaṇḍa : m. a flaw in a jewel, L.

[p. 336,1] : a calf with horns half grown, Gal.

[p. 336,1] : (in music) a kind of measure

[p. 336,1] khaṇḍa : m. pl. N. of a people (v.l. ṣaṇḍa), VarBṛS.

[p. 336,1] khaṇḍa : n. a variety of sugarcane, W.

[p. 336,1] : black salt (viḍ-lavaṇa), L. (cf. uttara-, karka-, kāla-, kāśī-, śrī-, sitā-.)
