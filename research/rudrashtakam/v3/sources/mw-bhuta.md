[p. 761,3] bhūta/ : mf(ā)n. become, been, gone, past (n. the past), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 761,3] : actually happened, true, real (n. an actual occurrence, fact, matter of fact, reality), Yājñ.; R. &c.

[p. 761,3] : existing, present, Kaṇ.

[p. 761,3] : (ifc.) being or being like anything, consisting of, mixed or joined with, Prāt.; Up.; Mn. &c. (also to form adj. out of adv., e.g. ittham-, evam-, tathā-bh˚)

[p. 761,3] : purified, L.

[p. 761,3] : obtained, L.

[p. 761,3] : fit, proper, L.

[p. 761,3] : often w.r. for bhṛta

[p. 761,3] bhūta/ : m. a son, child, L.

[p. 761,3] : a great devotee or ascetic, L.

[p. 761,3] : (pl.) N. of an heretical sect (with Jainas, a class of the Vyantaras), L.

[p. 761,3] : N. of Śiva, L.

[p. 761,3] : of a priest of the gods, L.

[p. 761,3] : of a son of Vasu-deva and Pauravī, BhP.

[p. 761,3] : of a son-in-law of Dakṣa and father of numerous Rudras, ib.

[p. 761,3] : of a Yakṣa, Cat.

[p. 761,3] bhūta/ : n. (cf. above) that which is or exists, any living being (divine, human, animal, and even vegetable), the world (in these senses also m.), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 761,3] : a spirit (good or evil), the ghost of a deceased person, a demon, imp, goblin (also m.), GṛS.; Up.; Mn. &c. (cf. RTL. 241)

[p. 761,3] : an element, one of the 5 elements (esp. a gross el˚ = mahā-bh˚, q.v.; but also a subtle el˚ = tan-mātra, q.v.; with Buddhists there are only 4 el˚), Up.; Sāṃkhyak.; Vedāntas. &c.

[p. 761,3] : N. of the number ‘five’ (cf. mahā-bh˚ and pāñcabhautika)

[p. 761,3] : well-being, welfare, prosperity, VS.; TS.; AitBr.
