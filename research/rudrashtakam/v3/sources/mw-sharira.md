[p. 1057,3] śa/rīra : n. (once in R. m.; ifc. f(ā). ; either fr. √ śri and orig. = ‘support or supporter’, cf. 2. śaraṇa and Mn. i, 7; or accord. to others, fr. √ śṝ, and orig. = ‘that which is easily destroyed or dissolved’) the body, bodily frame, solid parts of the body (pl. the bones), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 1057,3] : any solid body (opp. to udaka &c.), MBh.; VarBṛS.; Pañcat.

[p. 1057,3] : one's body i.e. one's own person, Mn. xi, 229

[p. 1057,3] : bodily strength, MW.

[p. 1057,3] : a dead body, ib.
