[p. 759,1] bhuja-ṃ-ga and -ṃ-gama, : see bhujaṃga and bhujaṃgama.

[p. 759,2] bhujaṃga : m. (fr. bhujam ind. p. of √ bhuj + ga) a serpent, snake, serpent-demon, Kāv.; Suśr.

[p. 759,2] : N. of the number eight, Sūryas.

[p. 759,2] : the paramour of a prostitute, Kāvyād.; Sāh.

[p. 759,2] : the dissolute friend of a prince, Kāvyād.

[p. 759,2] : any constant companion of a prince, Hcar.

[p. 759,2] : a lover (see pṛthivī-bh˚), the keeper of a prostitute, L.

[p. 759,2] : a species of Daṇḍaka metre, VarBṛS., Sch.

[p. 759,2] : N. of a man, Rājat.

[p. 759,2] bhujaṃga : n. (prob.) tin or lead, L.
