[p. 728,3] bāla : mf(ā)n. (cf. vāla) young, childish, infantine, not full-grown or developed (of persons and things), GṛS.; Up.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 728,3] : newly risen, early (as the sun or its rays), Ragh.

[p. 728,3] : new or waxing (as the moon), ib.; Kum.

[p. 728,3] : puerile, ignorant, simple, foolish, Mn.; Hariv.; Kāv.

[p. 728,3] : pure (as an animal fit for sacrifice), L.

[p. 728,3] bāla : m. a child, boy (esp. one under 5 years), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 728,3] : (in law) a minor (minors are classified as kumāra or boys under 5 years of age, śiśu under 8, pogaṇḍa from the 5th to the end of the 9th or till the 16th year, and kiśora from the 10th to the 16th year)

[p. 728,3] : a fool, simpleton, Mn.; Pañcat.

[p. 728,3] : any young animal, L.

[p. 728,3] : a colt, foal, L.

[p. 728,3] : a five years old elephant, L.

[p. 728,3] : Cyprinus Denticulatus or Rohita, L.

[p. 728,3] : N. of a Rakṣas, VP.

[p. 728,3] : of a prince, Rājat.

[p. 728,3] bāla : n. Andropogon Muricatus, L.

[p. 728,3] : heat, L.
