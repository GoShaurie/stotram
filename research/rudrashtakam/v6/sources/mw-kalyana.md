[p. 263,2] kalyā/ṇa : mf(ī/)n. (g. bahv-ādi) beautiful, agreeable, RV.; ŚBr. &c.

[p. 263,2] : illustrious, noble, generous

[p. 263,2] : excellent, virtuous, good (kalyāṇa voc. ‘good sir’; kalyāṇi, ‘good lady’)

[p. 263,2] : beneficial, salutary, auspicious

[p. 263,2] : happy, prosperous, fortunate, lucky, well, right, RV. i, 31, 9; iii, 53, 6; TS.; AV.; ŚBr.; Nir. ii, 3; MBh.; R.

[p. 263,2] kalyā/ṇa : m. a particular Rāga (sung at night)

[p. 263,2] : N. of a Gandharva

[p. 263,2] : of a prince (also called Bhaṭṭa-śrī-kalyāṇa)

[p. 263,2] : of the author of the poem Gītā-gaṅgā-dhara

[p. 263,2] kalyā/ṇa : n. good fortune, happiness, prosperity

[p. 263,2] : good conduct, virtue (opposed to pāpa), ŚBr.; Bhag.; R.; Ragh.; Pañcat.; Mn. iii, 60, 65; Suśr.

[p. 263,2] : a festival, Mn. viii, 292

[p. 263,2] : gold, L.

[p. 263,2] : heaven, L.

[p. 263,2] : N. of the eleventh of the fourteen Pūrvas or most ancient writings of the Jainas, L.

[p. 263,2] : a form of salutation (‘Hail!’ ‘May luck attend you!’), Śāntiś.
