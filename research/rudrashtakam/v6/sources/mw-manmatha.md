[p. 788,3] manmatha : m. (either an Intens. form fr. √ math, or fr. man = manas + matha, ‘agitating’; cf. mandeha and mandhātṛ) love or the god of love, amorous passion or desire (ifc. f(ā). ), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 788,3] : Feronia Elephantum, L.

[p. 788,3] : the 29th (3rd) year in a 60 years' cycle of Jupiter, VarBṛS.

[p. 788,3] : N. of a physician and various other men, Cat.
