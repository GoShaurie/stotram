[p. 260,2] kalā : f. See kalā below.

[p. 261,1] kalā/ : f. (etym. doubtful) a small part of anything, any single part or portion of a whole, esp. a sixteenth part, RV. viii, 47, 17; TS.; ŚBr.; Mn. &c.

[p. 261,1] : a digit or one-sixteenth of the moon's diameter, Hit.; Kathās.

[p. 261,1] : (personified as a daughter of Kardama and wife of Marīci, BhP.)

[p. 261,1] : a symbolical expression for the number sixteen, Hcat.

[p. 261,1] : interest on a capital (considered as a certain part of it), Śiś. ix, 32

[p. 261,1] : a division of time (said to be 1/900 of a day or 1.6 minutes, Mn. i, 64; Hariv.; or 1/1800 of a day or 0.8 minutes, Comm. on VP.; or 2 minutes and 26 54/201 seconds, Suśr.; or 1 minute and 35 205/301 seconds, or 8 seconds, BhavP.);

[p. 261,1] : the sixtieth part of one-thirtieth of a zodiacal sign, a minute of a degree, Sūryas.

[p. 261,1] : (in prosody) a syllabic instant

[p. 261,1] : a term for the seven substrata of the elements or Dhātus of the human body (viz. flesh, blood, fat, phlegm, urine, bile, and semen; but according to Hemacandra, rasa, ‘chyle’, asthi, ‘bone’, and majjan, ‘marrow’, take the place of phlegm, urine, and bile), Suśr.

[p. 261,1] : an atom (there are 3015 Kalās or atoms in every one of the six Dhātus, not counting the rasa, therefore in all 18090)

[p. 261,1] : (with Pāśupatas) the elements of the gross or material world, Sarvad.

[p. 261,1] : an embryo shortly after conception (cf. kalana)

[p. 261,1] : a designation of the three constituent parts of a sacrifice (viz. mantra, dravya, and śraddhā, Nīlak. on MBh. (ed. Bomb.) xiv, 89, 3)

[p. 261,1] : the menstrual discharge, L.
