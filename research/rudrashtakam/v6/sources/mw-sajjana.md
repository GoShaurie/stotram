[p. 1131,2] sajjana : mfn. (for saj-jana See p. 1135, col. 1) hanging round (e.g. kaṇṭha-s˚, ‘h˚ r˚ the neck’), Mn. ii, 63

[p. 1131,2] sajjana : n. a flight of steps or Ghāt leading down to the water, ferry, L.

[p. 1131,2] : equipment, preparation &c., L.

[p. 1131,2] sajjana : n. caparisoning an elephant (also f(ā). )

[p. 1131,2] sajjana : n. a guard, sentry, L.

[p. 1131,2] saj-jana : See under sat, p. 1135, col. 1.

[p. 1135,1] saj—jana : mfn. (for sajjana See p. 1131, col. 2) well-born, respectable, virtuous, Hariv.

[p. 1135,1] saj—jana : m. a good or virtuous or wise man, Mn. &c. &c.

[p. 1135,1] : N. of various men, Rājat.; Buddh.; Cat.
