[p. 836,1] mo/ha : m. (√ 1. muh; ifc. f(ā). ) loss of consciousness, bewilderment, perplexity, distraction, infatuation, delusion, error, folly, AV. &c. &c. (moham-√ brū, to say anything that leads to error; mohaṃ-√ yā, to fall into error; mohāt ind. through folly or ignorance)

[p. 836,1] : fainting, stupefaction, a swoon, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 836,1] : (in phil.) darkness or delusion of mind (preventing the discernment of truth and leading men to believe in the reality of worldly objects)

[p. 836,1] : (with Buddhists) ignorance (one of the three roots of vice, Dharmas. 139)

[p. 836,1] : a magical art employed to bewilder an enemy (= mohana), Cat.

[p. 836,1] : wonder, amazement, L.

[p. 836,1] : Infatuation personified (as the offspring of Brahmā), VP.
