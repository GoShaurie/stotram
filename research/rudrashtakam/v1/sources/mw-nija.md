[p. 547,1] ni-ja/ : mf(ā)n. (√ jan) innate, native, of one's own party or country (with ripu m. an enemy in one's own country, Hit.; m. pl. one's own people, Rājat.)

[p. 547,1] : constant, continual, AV.; Br.; Mn.; MBh. &c. (in later Sanskṛt used as a reflex. possess. pron. = sva, my own, his own, our own &c.)
