[p. 557,3] : immersed, plunged, L.

[p. 557,3] : immovable, L.

[p. 557,3] : extinction of the flame of life, dissolution, death or final emancipation from matter and re-union with the Supreme Spirit, MBh. &c. &c.

[p. 557,3] : (with Buddhists and Jainas) absolute extinction or annihilation (= śūnya, L.) of individual existence or of all desires and passions, MWB. 137-139 &c.

[p. 557,3] : perfect calm or repose or happiness, highest bliss or beatitude, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 557,3] : bathing of an elephant, L.
