[p. 947,2] 1. vā/sas : n. (for 2. See col. 2) cloth, clothes, dress, a garment (du. an upper and undergarment; cf. vāso-yuga), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 947,2] : the ‘clothing’ or feathers of an arrow, MBh.; R. &c. (only ifc.; cf. barhiṇa-v˚)

[p. 947,2] : cotton, L.

[p. 947,2] : a pall, MW.

[p. 947,2] : a screen, ib.

[p. 947,2] : (with markaṭasya) a cobweb, L.

[p. 947,2] : du. (with samudrasya) N. of two Sāmans, ĀrṣBr.

[p. 947,3] 2. vāsas : n. lodging for the night, night-quarters, PraśnUp.
