[p. 147,1] ā-√ mī : P. -mināti (RV. vi, 30, 2, &c.) to destroy, neutralize, curtail, RV.;
to put aside or away, cause to disappear or vanish, displace;
to exchange, RV. i, 92, 10, &c.;
to put or push out of place, TBr. :
Ā. (impf. ā/minanta) to vanish, disappear, RV. i, 79, 2 :
Intens. (p. -me/myāna) to change, alter, RV. i, 96, 5.
