[p. 1276,2] sva—rūpa : n. (ifc. f(ā). ) o˚'s own form or shape, the f˚ or sh˚ of (gen. or comp.; with or without śabdasya or śabda-sva-r˚, ‘a word itself or in its own form’ [opp. to its synonyms or varieties]; with nāmnām = ‘names themselves’), MBh.; Pañcat.; BhP. &c.

[p. 1276,3] : own condition, peculiarity, character, nature (eṇa or ibc., ‘by nature’, ‘in reality’ ‘by itself’), RPrāt.; NṛsUp.; Mn. &c.

[p. 1276,3] : peculiar aim, W.

[p. 1276,3] : kind, sort, ib.

[p. 1276,3] : a partic. relation (in phil. See under sambandha), MW.

[p. 1276,3] : occurrence, event, Campak.; Uttamac.; Siṃhās.

[p. 1276,3] sva—rūpa : mfn. having o˚'s own peculiar form or character, MW.

[p. 1276,3] : having a like nature or char˚, similar, like, Sāṃkhyak. (w.r. for sa-r˚)

[p. 1276,3] : pleasing, handsome (for sa-r˚), L.

[p. 1276,3] : wise, learned, L.

[p. 1276,3] sva—rūpa : m. N. of a Daitya, MBh.

[p. 1276,3] : of a son of Su-nandā, MārkP.

[p. 1276,3] : of a pupil of Caitanya, W.

[p. 1276,3] sva—rūpa : m. or n. N. of a place, Cat.
