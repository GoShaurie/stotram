[p. 397,3] 1. cid : in comp. for cit.

[p. 398,1] 2. cid : ind. even, indeed, also (often merely laying stress on a preceding word; requiring a preceding simple verb to be accentuated [Pāṇ. viii, 1, 57] as well as a verb following, if cid is preceded by an interrogative pron. [48]; in Class. only used after interrogative pronouns and adverbs to render them indefinite, and after jātu, q.v.), RV.; VS.; AV.

[p. 398,1] : like (added to the stem of a subst., e.g. agni-, rāja-), Nir. i, 4; Pāṇ. viii, 2, 101

[p. 398,1] : cid-cid or cid-ca or cid-u, as well as, both-and, RV.
