[p. 126,3] ā-kāśa/ : m. (Ved.) or (later) n. (ifc. f(ā). ) a free or open space, vacuity, AitBr.; ŚBr.; MBh. &c.

[p. 127,1] : the ether, sky or atmosphere, Naigh.; ŚBr.; Mn. &c.

[p. 127,1] ā-kāśa/ : n. (in philos.) the subtle and ethereal fluid (supposed to fill and pervade the universe and to be the peculiar vehicle of life and of sound), Vedāntas. &c.

[p. 127,1] : Brahma (as identical with ether), L.

[p. 127,1] : = ākāśa-bhāṣita below Comm. on Śak.
