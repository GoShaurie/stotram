नमामीशमीशाननिर्वाणरूपं विभुं व्यापकं ब्रह्मवेदस्वरूपम् ।
निजं निर्गुणं निर्विकल्पं निरीहं चिदाकाशमाकाशवासं भजेऽहम् ॥ १॥

2007 encoding (rudra8.itx, ITRANS as served):
namAmIshamIshAna nirvANarUpaM vibhuM vyApakaM brahmavedasvarUpam.h |
nijaM nirguNaM nirvikalpaM nirIhaM chidAkAshamAkAshavAsaM bhaje.aham.h || 1||

Hindi bhāvārtha (as printed):
हे मोक्षस्वरूप, समर्थ, व्यापक, ब्रह्म और वेदस्वरूप, ईशान दिशा के स्वामी (सर्वसम्पदा के स्वामी, जिसकी सत्ता से सबकी सत्ता हैं; ईश्वरों के ईश्वर; ब्रह्मादि के नियन्ता तथा ईशान कोण में ग्यारह रुद्ररूप से रहनेवाले ।) श्रीशिवजी ! मैं आपको नमस्कार करता हूँ । स्वतंत्र एवं स्वयं प्रकट होनेवाले, तीनों गुणों से रहित, भेदरहित (विकल्प, परिवर्तन या प्रभेदों आदि से रहित), इच्छारहित, चेतन, आकाशरूप एवं आकाश को ही वस्त्ररूप में धारण करनेवाले दिगम्बर (अथवा आकाश को भी आच्छादित करनेवाले, आकाश के समान निर्लिप्त और सबके आधारभूत) ! आपको मैं भजता हूँ ॥ १॥

English meaning (as printed):
I adore You, the guardian of the north-east quarter and Ruler of the whole universe, eternal bliss personified, the omnipresent and all-pervading Brahma manifest in the form of the Vedas. I worship Lord Siva, shining in His own glory, devoid of material attributes (beyod the Gunas - Sattva, Rajas, Tamas), undifferentiated, desireless, all-pervading consciousness, having nothing to wrap about Himself except ether (or enveloping ether itself). 1


## Note on ārṣa forms (printed after the whole hymn; copied from the whole-text excerpt)

Note printed after the English meaning:
The Rudrashtakam composed by Tulasidasji in Ramacharitamanas has some words likely to be influenced by Hindi construction.  They have to be considered as what is known as ArShaprayogAH (common term for linguistic usages in Sanskrit, which although not correct as per grammatical rules are still exempted and deemed valid on account of their having been used by some ancient sages carrying the rich linguistic and artistic heritage.)  Some examples are 
शम्भु  in  सम्बोधनं  instead of  शम्भो ।
बालेन्दु  without  विसर्गः ।
भुजङ्गा  in  स्त्रीलिङ्गम् । mainly to match the words Ganga 
हरतोषये  instead of  हरतोषाय ।
पुरारी  instead of  पुरारिः  or  पुरारे
मन्मथारी  instead of  मन्मथारिः  or  मन्मथारे
