[p. 416,2] jas : cl. 1. Ā. (p. ja/samāna) to be exhausted or starved, RV. i, 112, 6; vii, 68, 8:
P. ja/sati, to go, Naigh. ii, 14: cl. 4. P. to liberate, Dhātup. xxvi, 102 :
Caus. jāsayati (aor. ajījasata, 2. du. jajasta/m) to exhaust, weaken, cause to expire, RV. iv, 50, 11; ŚBr. ii, 2, 2, 19; xii, 4, 3, 9;
to hurt (cf. Pāṇ. ii, 3, 56), Dhātup. xxxii, 127;
to strike, xxxiii, 44;
to contemn, ib.;
 cf. uj-, ni-;
 projjāsana.
