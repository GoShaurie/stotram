[p. 483,2] 1. duḥkha/ : mfn. (according to grammarians properly written duṣ-kha and said to be from dus and kha [cf. su-kha/]; but more probably a Prākritized form for duḥ-stha, q.v.) uneasy, uncomfortable, unpleasant, difficult, R.; Hariv. (compar. -tara, MBh.; R.)

[p. 483,2] duḥkha/ : n. (ifc. f(ā). ) uneasiness, pain, sorrow, trouble, difficulty, ŚBr. xiv, 7, 2, 15; Mn.; MBh. &c. (personified as the son of Naraka and Vedanā, VP.)

[p. 483,2] duḥkha/ : n. impers. it is difficult to or to be (inf. with an acc. or nom. R. vii, 6, 38; Bhag. v, 6)

[p. 483,2] : duḥkham -√ as, to be sad or uneasy, Ratn. iv, 19/20

[p. 483,2] : -√ kṛ, to cause or feel pain, Yājñ. ii, 218; MBh. xii, 5298.

[p. 483,3] 2. duḥkha : Nom. P. ˚khati, to pain, SaddhP.
