[p. 412,1] ja/pa : mfn. ‘muttering, whispering’, see karṇe-, ku-

[p. 412,1] ja/pa : m. (Pāṇ. iii, 3, 61; oxyt. g. uñchādi) muttering prayers, repeating in a murmuring tone passages from scripture or charms or names of a deity, &c., muttered prayer or spell, AitBr. ii, 38; ŚBr. ii; ŚāṅkhŚr.; Nir. &c.
