[p. 822,1] : having no horns, hornless (as a cow or goat), Var.

[p. 822,1] : stripped of top leaves or branches, lopped (as a tree), MBh.

[p. 822,1] : pointless, blunt, Kathās.

[p. 822,1] : without awns or a beard (a kind of corn), L.

[p. 822,1] : low, mean, W.

[p. 822,1] : the trunk of a lopped tree, W.

[p. 822,1] : N. of Rāhu, L.

[p. 822,1] : of a Daitya, Hariv.

[p. 822,1] : of a king, Buddh.
