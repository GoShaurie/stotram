[p. 813,2] māla : m. (derivation doubtful) N. of a district (lying west and south-west of Bengal), Megh.

[p. 813,2] : of one of the 7 islands, of Antara-dvīpa; L., of Viṣṇu, L.

[p. 813,2] : of the son of a Śūdra and a Sūta, L.

[p. 813,2] : pl. N. of a barbarous tribe or people, MBh.

[p. 813,2] māla : n. a field, Inscr.; MBh.

[p. 813,2] : a forest or wood near a village, L.

[p. 813,2] : fraud, artifice, L.

[p. 813,2] : (in some comp.) = mālā, a wreath, garland.
