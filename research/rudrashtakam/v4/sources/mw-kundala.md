[p. 290,1] kuṇḍala : n. (ifc. f(ā). ; g. sidhmādi and ardharcādi) a ring, earring, ĀśvGṛ.; Lāṭy.; Mn. &c.

[p. 290,1] : a bracelet, Śiś. vi, 27

[p. 290,1] : a fetter, tie, L.

[p. 290,1] : the coil of a rope, L.

[p. 290,1] : a particular disease of the bladder, Car.

[p. 290,1] kuṇḍala : m. (in music) a kind of measure

[p. 290,1] : N. of a Nāga, MBh. i, 2154

[p. 290,1] kuṇḍala : m. pl. N. of a people (v.l. kuṇṭhaka), MBh. vi

[p. 1325,1] kuṇḍala or kuṇḍalaka : (also) a coil (of rope), Jātakam.
