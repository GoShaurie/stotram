[p. 469,3] day : cl. 1. Ā. da/yate (p. da/yamāna, RV. &c.; aor. adayiṣṭa, Bhaṭṭ.; pf. ˚yāṃ cakre, Pāṇ. iii, 1, 37)
to divide, impart, allot (with gen., ii, 3, 52; acc. RV.);
to partake, possess, RV.; Nir.;
to divide asunder, destroy, consume, RV. vi, 6, 5; x, 80, 2;
to take part in, sympathize with, have pity on (acc., vii, 23, 5; AV.; ŚBr. xiv; Bhaṭṭ.; gen. Daś.; Bhaṭṭ.; Kathās. cxxi, 104);
to repent, RV. vii, 100, 1;
to go, Dhātup. :
Caus. (Pot. dayayet) to have pity on (gen.), BhP. ii, 7, 42 :
Intens. dandayyate, dād˚, Vop. xx, 8 f.;
 cf. ava-, nir-ava-, vi-.
