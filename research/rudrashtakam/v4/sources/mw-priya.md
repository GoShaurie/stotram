[p. 709,3] priya : priyāla. See under √ 1. prī below.

[p. 710,1] priya/ : mf(ā)n. beloved, dear to (gen. loc. dat. or comp.), liked, favourite, wanted, own, RV. &c. &c. (with abl. ‘dearer than’, R.; Kathās.; Pañcat.; priyaṃ-√ kṛ Ā. kurute, either ‘to gain the affection of, win as a friend’, RV.; or ‘to feel affection for, love more and more’, MBh.)

[p. 710,1] : dear, expensive, high in price (cf. priya-dhānyaka, priyānna-tva)

[p. 710,1] : fond of attached or devoted to (loc.), RV. (id. in comp., either ibc., e.g. priya-devana, ‘fond of playing’, or ifc., e.g. akṣa-priya, ‘fond of dice’, cf. Pāṇ. ii, 2, 35, Vārtt. 2; ifc. also = pleasant, agreeable, e.g. gamana-priya, ‘pleasant to go’, vi, 2, 15Sch.)

[p. 710,1] priya/ : m. a friend, Gaut.

[p. 710,1] : a lover, husband, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 710,1] : a son-in-law, Mn. iii, 119 (Kull.)

[p. 710,1] : a kind of deer, L.

[p. 710,1] priya/ : m. N. of 2 medicinal plants, L.

[p. 710,1] priya/ : n. love, kindness, favour, pleasure, MBh.; Kāv. &c.
