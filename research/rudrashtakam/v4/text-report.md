## Chosen text

चलत्कुण्डलं भ्रूसुनेत्रं विशालं
प्रसन्नाननं नीलकण्ठं दयालम् ।
मृगाधीशचर्माम्बरं मुण्डमालं
प्रियं शङ्करं सर्वनाथं भजामि ॥ ४ ॥

## Variants

- a: भ्रूसुनेत्रं (K S E; भ्रू सुनेत्रं G W, S-2007). B: **सुभ्रनेत्रं** (worn print, possibly सुभ्रूनेत्रं). That gives "fine-browed eyes" instead of "brows, fine eyes": a minor difference, single witness, and सुभ्रू- would be unmetrical. Not adopted.
- b: नीलकण्ठं (G K B S E). W नीलकंठ is unmetrical, a typo. G's OCR drops the anusvāra, but the page image has नीलकंठं.
- c: मृगाधीशचर्माम्बरं (G S E; चर्मांबरं K; चर्म्माम्बरं B). Orthographic. G's Hindi renders मृगाधीश as "सिंह" (lion skin); S's Hindi has "बाघाम्बर" (tiger skin) and S's English "lion-skin". That is a difference of interpretation, not of text.
- d: प्रियं शङ्करं सर्वनाथं भजामि in all (G's OCR has शंकर, the image शंकरं).
- No sense-changing variant in the text.
