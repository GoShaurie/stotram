# Text report: rudrashtakam v9

This file is the v9 slice of `fulltext/text-report.md`; witnesses, sigla and conventions are explained there. Sigla: G = `gitapress-ramcharitmanas` (Gita Press, Hindi tr.), K = `kashiraj-ramcharitmanas` (Kāśirāja critical ed.), B = `belvedere-1926-ramcharitmanas`, S = `sanskritdocuments-rudra8` (S-2007 = its 2007 encoding), E = `greenmesg-rudrashtakam` (F = `shlokam-rudrashtakam` votes with E), W = `wikisource-sa-rudrashtakam` (no vote). Independent votes: G, K, B, S, E.

Meter: anuṣṭubh (śloka); B labels it "अनुष्टुप-वृत्त". All four pādas are regular pathyā (5th syllable light, 6th heavy, 7th heavy in pādas 1 and 3 and light in 2 and 4; Vidyut weights GGLLLGGG GGLLLGLG / GLGLLGGG GGGGLGLL).

## Chosen text

रुद्राष्टकमिदं प्रोक्तं विप्रेण हरतोषये ।
ये पठन्ति नरा भक्त्या तेषां शम्भुः प्रसीदति ॥ ९ ॥

## Variants

- The wording is **identical in every witness**: रुद्राष्टकमिदं प्रोक्तं विप्रेण हरतोषये । ये पठन्ति नरा भक्त्या तेषां शम्भुः प्रसीदति. K prints पठंति, which is orthographic.
- Numbering and heading: G heads it "श्लोक—" and numbers it ९. E and F number it ९. S leaves it unnumbered in the text but numbers its Hindi and English "9". K, B and W leave it unnumbered. B heads it "अनुष्टुप-वृत्त".
- This is the **only phalaśruti**. No witness adds a second one or a ninth bhujaṅgaprayāta stanza. (In K's appendix a single manuscript, "अल", inserts 13 extra half-lines elsewhere in the hymn; none of them is a phalaśruti.)
- Interpretation, not text: विप्रेण means "by the brāhmaṇa", i.e. the guru in the narrative. G's Hindi has "ब्राह्मणद्वारा कहा गया" and S's English "uttered by the Brahmana". E and F translate it "by the Wise", which loses the narrative reference. हरतोषये (for हरतोषाय) is an ārṣa form (S's note).
