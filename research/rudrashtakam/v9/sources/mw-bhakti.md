[p. 743,1] bhakti/ : f. distribution, partition, separation, RV.; TāṇḍBr. &c. (cf. kṣetra-, bhaṅgī-bh˚)

[p. 743,1] : a division, portion, share, AitBr.

[p. 743,1] : a division of a Sāman (also called vidhi, of which 7 or 5 are enumerated), Lāṭy. Śaṃk.

[p. 743,2] : division by streaks or lines, Ragh.

[p. 743,2] : a streak, line, variegated decoration, Hariv.; Kāv.

[p. 743,2] : a row, series, succession, order (˚tyā ind. and ˚ti-tas ind. in succession), RPrāt.

[p. 743,2] : (ifc.) the being a part of (ajbhakteḥ, ‘on the part of the vowels’), belonging to, Siddh. &c.

[p. 743,2] : that which belongs to or is contained in anything else, an attribute, Nir.; Prāt.

[p. 743,2] : predisposition (of body to any disease), Car.

[p. 743,2] : attachment, devotion, fondness for, devotion to (with loc., gen. or ifc.), trust, homage, worship, piety, faith or love or devotion (as a religious principle or means of salvation, together with karman, ‘works’, and jñāna, ‘spiritual knowledge’; cf. IW. 326; RTL. 97), ŚvetUp.; Bhag.; Kāv.; Pur. &c.

[p. 743,2] : (ifc.) assumption of the form of Megh. 61

[p. 743,2] : often w.r. for bhaṅgi or bhukti
