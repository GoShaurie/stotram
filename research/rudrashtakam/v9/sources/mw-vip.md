[p. 972,3] 1. vip (or vep) : cl. 1. Ā. (Dhātup. x, 6) vepate (ep. also ˚ti; p. vipāna/, RV.; pf. vivepe Gr.; vivipre, RV.; aor. avepiṣṭa, Br.; fut. vepitā, vepiṣyate Gr.; inf. vepitum, ib.),
to tremble, shake, shiver, vibrate, quiver, be stirred, RV. &c. &c.;
to start back through fear, Pañcar.; Kathās. :
Caus. vipa/yati or vepayati (aor. avīvipat), to cause to tremble or move, shake, agitate, RV.; &c.

[p. 972,3] vip : [cf. Lat. vibrare; Goth. weiþan; Germ. wîfen, weifen, Wipfel Eng. whiffle.]

[p. 972,3] 2. vi/p : mfn. inwardly stirred or excited, inspired, RV.

[p. 972,3] vi/p : f. ‘easily moved or bent, flexible (?)’, a switch, rod &c., the shaft (of an arrow), the rods (which form the bottom of the Soma filter, and support the straining cloth), RV.

[p. 972,3] : a finger, Naigh. ii, 5.
