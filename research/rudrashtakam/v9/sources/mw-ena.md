[p. 231,2] eṇa : as, ī/, m. f. a species of deer or antelope (described as being of a black colour with beautiful eyes and short legs), AV. v, 14, 11; VS. xxiv, 36; Mn. iii, 269; MBh. &c.

[p. 231,2] eṇa : m. (in astron.) Capricorn.
