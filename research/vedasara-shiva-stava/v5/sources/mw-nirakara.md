[p. 540,1] 1. nir—ākāra : mfn. formless, shapeless, incorporeal (brahman), making no appearance or show, insignificant, unimportant, MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 540,1] : having no object (cf. below)

[p. 540,1] nir—ākāra : m. N. of Śiva, Śivag.

[p. 540,1] : of Viṣṇu, L.

[p. 540,1] : heaven, L.

[p. 540,1] : the universal spirit, god, L.

[p. 553,2] 2. nir-ā-˚kāra : (for 1. See p. 540, col. 1) m. rebuke, reproach, censure, L.
