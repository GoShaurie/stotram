[p. 171,2] īh : cl. 1. Ā. īhate, īhāṃ-cakre, īhiṣyate, īhitum, rarely P. īhati, &c., to endeavour to obtain;
to aim at, attempt;
to long for, desire;
to take care of;
 to have in mind, think of (with acc.), MBh.; R.; BhP.; Bhag.; Pañcat. &c. :
Caus. īhayati, to impel.
