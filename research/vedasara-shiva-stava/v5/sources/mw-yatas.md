[p. 841,2] ya/tas : ind. (fr. 3. ya, correlative of ta/tas, and often used as abl. or instr. of the relative pron.) from which or what, whence, whereof, wherefrom, RV. &c. &c. (ya/to yataḥ, ‘from whichever’, ‘from whatever’, ‘whencesoever’; yatas tataḥ, ‘from any one soever’, ‘from any quarter whatever’; yata eva kutaś ca, ‘from this or that place’, ‘whencesoever’)

[p. 841,2] : where, in what place, AV. &c. &c.

[p. 841,2] : whither, Kāv.; Var.; Kathās. (yato yataḥ, ‘whithersoever’; yatas tataḥ, ‘any whither’, ‘to any place whatever’)

[p. 841,2] : wherefore, for which reason, in consequence where of R.; BhP.

[p. 841,2] : as, because, for, since, AV. &c. &c. (often connecting with a previous statement)

[p. 841,2] : from which time forward, since when (also with prabhṛti; yato jātā, ‘ever since birth’), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 841,2] : as soon as, RV. iii, 10, 6

[p. 841,2] : that (= ὅτι, also to introduce an oratio recta), Kāv.; Pur.

[p. 841,2] : in order that (with Pot.), BhP.
