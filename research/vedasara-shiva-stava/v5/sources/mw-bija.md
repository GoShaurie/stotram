[p. 732,2] bī/ja : n. (also written vīja, of doubtful origin; ifc. f(ā). ) seed (of plants), semen (of men and animals), seed-corn, grain, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 732,2] : a runner (of the Indian fig-tree), Vcar.

[p. 732,2] : any germ, element, primary cause or principle, source, origin (ifc. = caused or produced by, sprung from), ChUp.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 732,2] : the germ or origin of any composition (as of a poem, of the plot of a drama, of a magical formula &c.), R.; BhP.; Daśar.; Pratāp.

[p. 732,2] : calculation of original or primary germs, analysis, algebra, Col.

[p. 732,2] : truth (as the seed or cause of being), L.

[p. 732,2] : anything serving as a receptacle or support (= ālambana), Yogaś.

[p. 732,2] : the mystical letter or syllable which forms the essential part of the Mantra of any deity, RTL. 197 &c.

[p. 732,2] : the position of the arms of a child at birth, BhP.

[p. 732,2] : quicksilver (?), Sūryas.

[p. 732,2] : marrow, L.

[p. 732,2] bī/ja : m. = bijaka, the citron tree, Āryabh.
