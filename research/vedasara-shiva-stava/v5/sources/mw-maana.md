[p. 804,3] māna : See p. 809, col. 3.

[p. 809,1] 1. mā/na : m. (√ man) opinion, notion, conception, idea, Tattvas. (cf. ātma-m˚)

[p. 809,1] : purpose, wish, design, AitBr.

[p. 809,1] : self-conceit, arrogance, pride, KauṣUp.; Mn. &c. (with Buddhists one of the 6 evil feelings, Dharmas. 67; or one of the 10 fetters to be got rid of MWB. 127)

[p. 809,1] : (also n.) consideration, regard, respect, honour, Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 809,1] : a wounded sense of honour, anger or indignation excited by jealousy (esp. in women), caprice, sulking, Kāv.; Daśar.; Sāh.

[p. 809,1] : N. of the father of Agastya (perhaps also of A˚ himself, Pāṇ. the family of Māna), RV.

[p. 809,1] : (in astron.) N. of the tenth house, VarBṛS. (W. also a ‘blockhead ; an agent; a barbarian’).

[p. 809,3] 2. mā/na : m. (√ 3. mā) a building, house, dwelling, RV.

[p. 809,3] : an altar, Āpast.

[p. 809,3] : (māna/) a preparation, decoction (?), RV. x, 144, 5

[p. 1331,2] : (accord. to some in RV. i, 39, 1 = ‘light, sheen’).

[p. 809,3] mā/na : n. measuring, meting out, KātyŚr.; Hariv. &c.

[p. 809,3] : measure, measuring-cord, standard, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 809,3] : dimension, size, height, length (in space and time), weight, ib. (ifc. = fold See śata/-m˚)

[p. 809,3] : a partic. measure or weight (= kṛṣṇala or raktikā; accord. to Sch. on TS. & KātyŚr. 100 Mānas = 5 Palas or Paṇas)

[p. 809,3] : form, appearance, RV.

[p. 809,3] : likeness, resemblance, Śiś.

[p. 809,3] : (in phil.) proof, demonstration, means of proof (= pra-māṇa. q.v.)
