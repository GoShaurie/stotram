[p. 374,2] grīṣma/ : m. (√ gras, Uṇ.) the summer, hot season (the months Śuci and Śukra, VS. xiv, 6; Suśr.; or Jyeṣṭha and Āṣāḍha, from the middle of May to the middle of July), RV. x, 90, 6; AV. &c.

[p. 374,2] : summer heat, heat, Pañcat.

[p. 374,2] : N. of a man g. aśvādi

[p. 374,2] grīṣma : [cf. Hib. gris, ‘fire’; griosgaim, ‘I fry, boil’; griosach, ‘burning embers’.]
