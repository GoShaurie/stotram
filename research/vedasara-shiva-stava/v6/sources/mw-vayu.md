[p. 942,2] 1. vāyu/ : m. (fr. √ 2. vā) wind, air (as one of the 5 elements; in MBh. 7 winds are reckoned), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 942,2] : the god of the wind (often associated with Indra in the Ṛg-veda, as Vāta [q.v.] with Parjanya, but although of equal rank with Indra, not occupying so prominent a position;
in the Puruṣasūkta he is said to have sprung form the breath of Puruṣa, and elsewhere is described as the son-in-law of Tvaṣṭṛ;
be is said to move in a shining car drawn by a pair of red or purple horses or by several teams consisting of ninety-nine or a hundred or even a thousand horses [cf. ni-yu/t];
he is often made to occupy the same chariot with Indra, and in conjunction with him honoured with the first draught of the Soma libation;
he is rarely connected with the Maruts, although in i, 134, 4, he is said to have begotten them from the rivers of heaven;
he is regent of the Nakṣatra Svāti and north-west quarter See loka-pāla), ib.

[p. 942,2] : breathing, breath, VPrāt.; ĪśUp.

[p. 942,2] : the wind of the body, a vital air (of which 5 are reckoned, viz. prāṇa, apāna, samāna, udāna, and vyāna; or nāga, kūrma, kṛkara, devadatta, and dhanaṃ-jaya), Hariv.; Sāṃkhyak.; Vedāntas.

[p. 942,2] : (in medicine) the windy humour or any morbid affection of it, Suśr.

[p. 942,2] : the wind as a kind of demon producing madness, Kād.; Vcar. (cf. -grasta)

[p. 942,2] : (in astron.) N. of the fourth Muhūrta

[p. 942,2] : a mystical N. of the letter ya, Up.

[p. 942,2] : N. of a Vasu, Hariv.

[p. 942,2] : of a Daitya, ib.

[p. 942,2] : of a king of the Gandharvas, VP.

[p. 942,2] : of a Marut, R.

[p. 942,2] : pl. the Maruts, Kathās.; MārkP.

[p. 943,1] 2. vāyu/ : mfn. (fr. √ vai) tired, languid, RV. vii, 91, 1.

[p. 943,1] 3. vāyu/ : mfn. (fr. √ vī) desirous, covetous, greedy (for food, applied to calves), TS.

[p. 943,1] : desirable, desired by the appetite, RV.
