[p. 47,3] 1. a/p : n. (gen. apa/s), work (according to NBD.), RV. i, 151, 4.

[p. 47,3] 2. a/p : f. (in Ved. used in sing. and pl., but in the classical language only in pl., ā/pas) water

[p. 47,3] : air, the intermediate region, Naigh.

[p. 47,3] : the star δ Virginis

[p. 47,3] : the Waters considered as divinities. ifc. ap may become apa or īpa, ūpa after i- and u- stems respectively.

[p. 47,3] ap : [cf. Lat. aqua; Goth. ahva, ‘a river’; Old Germ. aha, and affa at the end of compounds; Lith. uppê, ‘a river’; perhaps Lat. amnis, ‘a river’, for apnis cf. also ἀϕρός]
