[p. 548,2] ni-˚drā/ : f. sleep, slumber, sleepiness, sloth, RV.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 548,2] : the budding state of a flower (hence ˚drāṃ-√ tyaj, to bloom), ŚārṅgP.
