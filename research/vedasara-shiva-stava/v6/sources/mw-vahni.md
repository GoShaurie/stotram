[p. 933,3] va/hni : m. any animal that draws or bears along, a draught animal, horse, team, RV.; AV.; VS.; TBr.

[p. 933,3] : any one who conveys or is borne along (applied to a charioteer or rider, or to various gods, esp. to Agni, Indra, Savitṛ, the Maruts &c.), RV.; AV.

[p. 933,3] : N. of Soma (as ‘the flowing or streaming one’), RV. ix, 9, 6 &c.

[p. 933,3] : the conveyer or bearer of oblations to the gods (esp. said of Agni, ‘fire’, or of the three sacrificial fires, see agni), RV.

[p. 933,3] : partic. fire, Gṛhyās.

[p. 933,3] : fire (in general or ‘the god of fire’), Mn.; MBh. &c. (vahninā saṃ-√ skṛ, to hallow by fire, burn solemnly)

[p. 933,3] : the fire of digestion, VarBṛS.

[p. 933,3] : N. of the number ‘three’ (fr. the three sacred fires), L.

[p. 933,3] : N. of various plants (accord. to L. Plumbago Ceylanica; Semecarpus Anacardium; Poa Cynosuroides; and the citron tree), Suśr.

[p. 933,3] : a mystical N. of the letter r, Up.

[p. 934,1] : N. of the 8th Kalpa (q.v.), Cat.

[p. 934,1] : of a Daitya, MBh.

[p. 934,1] : of a son of Kṛṣṇa, BhP.

[p. 934,1] : of a son of Turvasu, ib.

[p. 934,1] : of a son of Kukura, ib.
