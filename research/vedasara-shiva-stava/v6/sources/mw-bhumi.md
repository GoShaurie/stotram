[p. 763,1] : (pl. divisions of the world; cf. bhūmi-traya)

[p. 763,1] : a territory, country, district, ib.

[p. 763,1] : a place, situation, ŚBr. &c. &c.

[p. 763,1] : position, posture, attitude, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 763,1] : the part or personification (played by an actor), Kathās.

[p. 763,1] : the floor of a house, story, Megh.; Kathās.

[p. 763,1] : the area, Śulbas.

[p. 763,1] : the base of any geometrical figure, Col.

[p. 763,1] : (metaph.) a step, degree, stage, Yogas. (with Buddhists there are 10 or 13 stages of existence or perfection, Dharmas. 45; 46)

[p. 763,1] : extent, limit, Kir.
