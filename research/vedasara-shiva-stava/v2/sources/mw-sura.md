[p. 1234,2] sura : m. (prob. fr. asura as if fr. a-sura and as sita fr. a-sita; thought by some to be connected with 2. svar) a god, divinity, deity (surāṇāṃ hantṛ m. ‘slayer of the gods’, N. of a partic. form of fire, son of Tapas), MaitrUp.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1234,2] : the image of a god, an idol, Viṣṇ.

[p. 1234,2] : a symbolical N. for the number ‘thirty-three’ (from the 33 gods; see deva), Gaṇit.

[p. 1234,2] : a sage, learned man, L.

[p. 1234,2] : the sun, L.

[p. 1234,2] : (said to be) = kṣura, MBh.

[p. 1234,2] : w.r. for svara, ib.

[p. 1234,2] sura : (am), n., see surā.
