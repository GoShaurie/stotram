[p. 170,2] 1. īḍ : cl. 2. Ā. ī/ṭṭe (2. sg. pres. īḍiṣe, Ved. īḻiṣe, pf. īḍe/, fut. īḍiṣyate, aor. aiḍiṣṭa, inf. īḍitum, Ved. īḻe, &c.) to implore, request, ask for (with two acc.);
to praise, RV.; AV.; VS.; R.; BhP. &c.:
Caus. P. īḍayati, to ask;
to praise, BhP.

[p. 170,2] 2. īḍ : ṭ, f. praise, extolling, RV. viii, 39, 1.
