[p. 952,1] vi—rūpa : See s.v.

[p. 984,2] vi/-rūpa : mf(ā)n. many-coloured, variegated, multiform, manifold, various, RV.; TS.; Br.; Kauś.

[p. 984,2] : varied, altered, changed, AitBr.

[p. 984,2] : different, Pāṇ., Vārtt. (with ekārtha, ‘different in form but the same in meaning’; ifc. ‘different from’, Sāṃkhyak.)

[p. 984,2] : deformed, misshapen, ugly, monstrous, unnatural, ChUp.; MBh. &c.

[p. 984,2] : less by one, minus one, VarBṛS.

[p. 984,2] vi/-rūpa : m. jaundice, Gal.

[p. 984,2] : N. of Śiva, MBh. (cf. virūpa-cakṣus and virūpākṣa)

[p. 984,2] : N. of an Asura, MBh.; Hariv.

[p. 984,2] : of a son of the demon Parivarta, MārkP.

[p. 984,2] : of an Āṅgirasa (author of RV. viii, 43; 44; 75; father of Pṛṣad-aśva and son of Ambarīṣa; pl. the family of the Virūpas), RV.; MBh.; Pur.

[p. 984,2] : of a son of Kṛṣṇa, BhP.

[p. 984,2] : of one of the descendants of Manu Vaivasvata, MW.

[p. 984,2] : of a prince, W.

[p. 984,2] : of two teachers, Buddh.

[p. 984,2] vi/-rūpa : n. deformity, irregular or monstrous shape, W.

[p. 984,2] : difference of form, variety of nature or character, ib.

[p. 984,2] : the root of Piper Longum, L.
