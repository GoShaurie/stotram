Devanagari:
महेशं सुरेशं सुरारातिनाशं
विभुं विश्वनाथं विभूत्यङ्गभूषम् ।
विरूपाक्षमिन्द्वर्कवह्नित्रिनेत्रं
सदानन्दमीडे प्रभुं पञ्चवक्त्रम् ॥ २ ॥

Transliteration:
maheśaṃ sureśaṃ surārātināśaṃ
vibhuṃ viśvanāthaṃ vibhūtyaṅgabhūṣam .
virūpākṣamindvarkavahnitrinetraṃ
sadānandamīḍe prabhuṃ pañcavaktram .. 2 ..

Translation:
(I meditate on him) who is the great lord (Mahesha), who is the lord of the devas (suresha) and who removes the afflictions of the devas,
who is the all-pervading lord of the universe (Vishwanatha) and whose body is adorned with sacred ashes (Vibhuti),
whose unique eyes consists of the triad of the moon (Indu), sun (Arka) and fire (Vahni),
I extol that ever-blissful lord who has five faces (Pancha Vaktra).

Word-meanings:
महेशं (maheśam) – great lord (mahā:great | īśa:lord); सुरेशं (sureśam) – lord of the devas (sura:deva | īśa:lord); सुरारातिनाशं (surārātināśam) – remover of devas' afflictions (sura:deva | arāti:affliction | nāśa:remover); विभुं (vibhum) – all-pervading; विश्वनाथं (viśvanātham) – lord of the universe (viśva:universe | nātha:lord); विभूत्यङ्गभूषम् (vibhūtyaṅgabhūṣam) – adorned with sacred ashes (vibhūti:sacred ashes | aṅga:body | bhūṣa:adorned); विरूपाक्षम् (virūpākṣam) – unique-eyed (virūpa:unique | akṣa:eye); इन्द्वर्कवह्नित्रिनेत्रं (indvarkavahnitrinetram) – triad of moon, sun, fire (indu:moon | arka:sun | vahni:fire | tri:three | netra:eye); सदानन्दम् (sadānandam) – ever-blissful (sadā:ever | ānanda:blissful); ईडे (īḍe) – I extol; प्रभुं (prabhum) – lord; पञ्चवक्त्रम् (pañcavaktram) – five-faced (pañca:five | vaktra:face);
