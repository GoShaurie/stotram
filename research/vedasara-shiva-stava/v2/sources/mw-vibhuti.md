[p. 978,3] vi/-bhūti : mfn. penetrating, pervading, Nir.

[p. 978,3] : abundant, plentiful, RV.

[p. 978,3] : mighty, powerful, ib.

[p. 978,3] : presiding over (gen.), ib. viii, 50, 6

[p. 978,3] vi/-bhūti : m. N. of a Sādhya, Hariv.

[p. 978,3] : of a son of Viśvāmitra, MBh.

[p. 978,3] : of a king, VP.

[p. 978,3] vi/-bhūti : f. development, multiplication, expansion, plenty, abundance, Kāv.; Kathās. &c.

[p. 978,3] : manifestation of might, great power, superhuman power (consisting of eight faculties, especially attributed to Śiva, but supposed also to be attainable by human beings through worship of that deity, viz. aṇiman, the power of becoming as minute as an atom; laghiman, extreme lightness; prāpti, attaining or reaching anything [e.g. the moon with the tip of the finger] ; prākāmya, irresistible will; mahiman, illimitable bulk; īśitā, supreme dominion; vaśitā, subjugating by magic; and kāmāvasāyitā, the suppressing all desires), ib.

[p. 979,1] : a partic. Śakti, Hcat.

[p. 979,1] : the might of a king or great lord, sovereign power, greatness, Kālid.; Pañcat.; Kathās. &c.

[p. 979,1] : successful issue (of a sacrifice), MBh.; R.

[p. 979,1] : splendour, glory, magnificence, Hariv.; Ragh.; VarBṛS.

[p. 979,1] : fortune, welfare, prosperity, PraśnUp.; MBh. &c.

[p. 979,1] : (also pl.) riches, wealth, opulence, Kām.; Kāv.; Kathās.

[p. 979,1] : N. of Lakṣmi (the goddess of fortune and welfare), BhP.

[p. 979,1] : the ashes of cow-dung &c. (with which Śiva is said to smear his body, and hence used in imitation of him by devotees), Pañcar.; Sāh.

[p. 979,1] : (in music) a partic. Śruti, Saṃgīt.
