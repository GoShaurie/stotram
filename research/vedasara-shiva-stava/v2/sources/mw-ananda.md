[p. 139,3] ā-nanda/ : as, m. happiness, joy, enjoyment, sensual pleasure, RV.; AV.; VS.; R.; Ragh. &c.

[p. 139,3] ā-nanda/ : (as), m. and (am), n. ‘pure happiness’, one of the three attributes of Ātman or Brahman in the Vedānta philosophy, Vedāntas. &c.

[p. 139,3] ā-nanda/ : (as), m. (in dram.) the thing wished for, the end of the drama [e.g. the VIth Act in the Veṇīs.], Sāh. 399

[p. 139,3] : a kind of flute

[p. 139,3] : the sixteenth Muhūrta

[p. 139,3] : N. of Śiva

[p. 139,3] : of a Lokeśvara (Buddh.)

[p. 139,3] : of a Bala (Jain.), L.

[p. 139,3] : of several men

[p. 1319,2] ānanda : (also) N. of one of the chief disciples of Gautama Buddha, MWB. 47 &c.

[p. 1319,3] ānanda : N. of various authors &c., Cat.

[p. 139,3] : of a country

[p. 139,3] ā-nanda/ : m. and (am), n. N. of the forty-eighth year of the cycle of Jupiter

[p. 139,3] ā-nanda/ : (am), n. a kind of house

[p. 139,3] ānanda : (often at the beginning and end of proper names.)
