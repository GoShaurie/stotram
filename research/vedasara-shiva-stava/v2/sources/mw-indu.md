[p. 166,1] i/ndu : m. (√ und, Uṇ. i, 13; probably fr. ind = √ und, ‘to drop’ [see p. 165, col. 3, and cf. i/ndra]; perhaps connected with bindu, which last is unknown in the Ṛg-veda, BRD.), Ved. a drop (especially of Soma), Soma, RV.; AV.; VS.

[p. 166,1] : a bright drop, a spark, TS.

[p. 166,1] : the moon

[p. 166,1] : m. pl. (avas) the moons i.e. the periodic changes of the moon

[p. 166,1] : time of moonlight, night, RV.; MBh.; Śak.; Megh. &c.

[p. 166,1] : camphor, Bhpr.

[p. 166,1] : the point on a die, AV. vii, 109, 6

[p. 166,1] : N. of Vāstoṣpati, RV. vii, 54, 2

[p. 166,1] : a symbolic expression for the number ‘one’

[p. 166,1] : designation of the Anusvāra

[p. 166,1] : a coin, L. (In the Brāhmaṇas, indu is used only for the moon; but the connexion between the meanings ‘Soma juice’ and ‘moon’ in the word indu has led to the same two ideas being transferred in classical Sanskṛt to the word soma, although the latter has properly only the sense ‘Soma juice’.)

[p. 1320,3] : the weight of a silver Pala, L.
