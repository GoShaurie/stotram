[p. 534,3] nāth : cl. 1. Ā. (Dhātup. ii, 6) nā/thate, TS.; Kāṭh. &c. (˚ti, MBh.; Kathās.; pf. nanātha; aor. anāthīt; fut. nāthiṣyati, or nāthitā Gr.; inf. nāthitum, Kathās.; ind.p. -nāthya, Prab.)
to seek aid, approach with prayers or requests (loc.), TS.; Kāṭh.;
to ask, solicit, beg for (gen. of thing, MBh. iii, 126, 30; cf. Pāṇ. ii, 3, 55; dat. of th˚, Vop.; with 2 acc. Naiṣ. iii, 25);
 to have power, be master, Dhātup. :
Caus. nāthayati, to cause a person to ask for anything i.e. to grant a request (acc.), BhP. (B.), ii, 9, 25.

[p. 1329,2] : to harass, destroy, Dhātup.

[p. 534,3] nāth : [cf. √ nādh; Goth. nithan, Old Sax. ginātha, nātha; HGerm. genāde, gnade.]
