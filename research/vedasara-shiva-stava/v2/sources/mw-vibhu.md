[p. 978,2] vi-bhu : &c. See col. 3.

[p. 978,3] vi-bhu/ : or (Ved.) vibhu, mf(ū/ or vī/)n. (Ved.) being everywhere, far-extending, all-pervading, omnipresent, eternal, RV.; VS.; Up.; MBh. &c.

[p. 978,3] : abundant, plentiful, RV.; VS.; Br.

[p. 978,3] : mighty, powerful, excellent, great, strong, effective, able to or capable of (inf.), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 978,3] : firm, solid, hard, L.

[p. 978,3] vi-bhū/ : m. a lord, ruler, sovereign, king (also applied to Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Śiva), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 978,3] : (ifc.) chief of or among, VarBṛS.

[p. 978,3] : a servant, L.

[p. 978,3] : the sun, L.

[p. 978,3] : the moon, L.

[p. 978,3] : N. of Kubera, L. (W. also ‘ether; space; time; the soul’)

[p. 978,3] : N. of a god (son of Veda-śiras and Tuṣitā), BhP.

[p. 978,3] : of a class of gods under Manu Sāvarṇi, MārkP.

[p. 978,3] : of Indra under Manu Raivata and under the 7th Manu, ib.; BhP.

[p. 978,3] : of a son of Viṣṇu and Dakṣiṇā, BhP.

[p. 978,3] : of a son of Bhaga and Siddhi, ib.

[p. 978,3] : of Buddha, L.

[p. 978,3] : of a brother of Śakuni, MBh.

[p. 978,3] : of a son of Śambara, Hariv.

[p. 978,3] : of a son of Satya-ketu and father of Su-vibhu, VP.

[p. 978,3] : of a son of Dharma-ketu and father of Su-kumāra, ib.

[p. 978,3] : of a son of Varṣa-ketu or Satya-ketu and father of Ānarta, Hariv.

[p. 978,3] : of a son of Prastāva and Niyutsā, BhP.

[p. 978,3] : of a son of Bhṛgu, MW.

[p. 978,3] vi-bhu/ : m. pl. N. of the Ṛbhus, RV.
