[p. 1234,2] sur : (rather Nom. fr. sura below) cl. 6. P. surati, to rule, possess supreme or superhuman power, Dhātup. xxviii, 50;
to shine, ib.;
 cl. 10. P. surayati, to find fault (v.l. for svar), xxxv, 11.
