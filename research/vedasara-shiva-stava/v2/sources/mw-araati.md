[p. 87,2] a/-rāti : f. ‘non liberality’, enviousness, malignity, RV.

[p. 87,2] : failure, adversity, RV.; AV.; ŚBr.; TBr.

[p. 87,2] : particular evil spirits (who frustrate the good intentions and disturb the happiness of man), ib.

[p. 87,2] a/-rāti : (is), m. an enemy, Ragh. xii, 89

[p. 87,2] : (in arithm.) the number six (there being six sins or internal enemies, cf. ṣaḍ-varga).
