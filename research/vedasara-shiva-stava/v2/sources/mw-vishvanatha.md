[p. 993,1] viśva—nātha : m. ‘lord of the universe’, N. of Śiva (esp. as the object of adoration at Benares, cf. vśveśa), Inscr.

[p. 993,1] : of various authors and other men (also with kavi, cakravartin, dīkṣita, daivājña, paṇḍita, miśtra, -rāja, vājapeyin &c.), Kṣitīś.; Cat.; Col.
