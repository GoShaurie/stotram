[p. 389,2] 1. carācara : mfn. movable and immovable, locomotive and stationary, moving and fixed (as animals and plants), Mn. i, iii; Bhag. x f.; R.; BhP.

[p. 389,2] carācara : n. the aggregate of all created things whether animate or inanimate, world, Mn.; Yājñ.; Bhag.; R.; BhP.

[p. 389,3] 2. carācara/ : mfn. (√ car redupl. Pāṇ. vi, 1, 12, Vārtt. 6; vii, 4, 58, Pat.) moving, locomotive, running, RV. x, 85, 11; VS. xxii; ŚBr.

[p. 389,3] carācara/ : n. Cypraea moneta, L.
