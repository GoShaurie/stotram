[p. 901,3] liṅga : n. (once m. in NṛsUp.; ifc. f(ā). , f(ī). only in viṣṇu-liṅgī; prob. fr. √ lag; cf. lakṣa, lakṣaṇa) a mark, spot, sign, token, badge, emblem, characteristic (ifc. = tal-liṅga, ‘having anything for a mark or sign’), Up.; MBh. &c.

[p. 901,3] : any assumed or false badge or mark, guise, disguise, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 901,3] : a proof, evidence, Kaṇ.; KātyŚr.; Sarvad.

[p. 901,3] : a sign of guilt, corpus delicti, Yājñ., Sch.

[p. 901,3] : the sign of gender or sex, organ of generation, Mn.; Hariv.; Pur. &c.

[p. 901,3] : the male organ or Phallus (esp. that of Śiva worshipped in the form of a stone or marble column which generally rises out of a yoni, q.v., and is set up in temples dedicated to Śiva; formerly 12 principal Śiva-liṅgas existed, of which the best known are Soma-nātha in Gujarāt, Mahā-kāla at Ujjayinī, Viśveśvara at Benares &c.; but the number of Liṅgas in India is estimated at 30 millions, IW. 322 n.; RTL. 78, 1; 90), MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 901,3] : gender (in gram.; cf. puṃ-l˚), Prāt.; Pāṇ.

[p. 901,3] : the image of a god, an idol, VarBṛS.

[p. 901,3] : (in logic) = vyāpya, the invariable mark which proves the existence of anything in an object (as in the proposition ‘there is fire because there is smoke’, smoke is the liṅga; cf. IW. 62)

[p. 901,3] : inference, conclusion, reason (cf. kāvya-l˚)

[p. 901,3] : = liṅgaśarīra (in Vedānta)

[p. 901,3] : anything having an origin and therefore liable to be destroyed again, Kap.

[p. 901,3] : = ākāśa, Kāraṇḍ.

[p. 901,3] : (in Sāṃkhya) = prakṛti or pradhāna, ‘the eternal procreative germ’, L.

[p. 902,1] : = vyakta, L.

[p. 902,1] : cf. RTL. 30

[p. 902,1] : = prātipadika, the crude base or uninflected stem of a noun (shortened into li), Vop., Sch.

[p. 902,1] : (in rhet.) an indication (word that serves to fix the meaning of another word; e.g. in the passage kupito makara-dhvajaḥ the word kupita restricts the meaning of makara-dhvaja to ‘Kāma’) = liṅga-purāṇa, BhP.

[p. 902,1] : the order of the religious student, W.

[p. 902,1] : a symptom, mark of disease, W.
