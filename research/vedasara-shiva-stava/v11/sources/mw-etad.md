[p. 231,2] eta/d : mfn. (Gr. 223; g. sarvādi, Pāṇ. i, 1, 27) this, this here, here (especially as pointing to what is nearest to the speaker, e.g. eṣa bāṇaḥ, this arrow here in my hand; eṣa yāti panthāḥ, here passes the way; eṣa kālaḥ, here i.e. now, is the time; etad, this here i.e. this world here below)

[p. 231,2] : sometimes used to give emphasis to the personal pronouns (e.g. eṣo'ham, I, this very person here) or with omission of those pronouns (e.g. eṣa tvāṃ svargaṃ nayāmi, I standing here will convey thee to heaven; etau praviṣṭau svaḥ, we two here have entered)

[p. 231,2] : as the subject of a sentence it agrees in gender and number with the predicate without reference to the noun to be supplied (e.g. etad eva hi me dhanam, for this [scil. cow] is my only wealth, MBh.)

[p. 231,2] : but sometimes the neuter sing. remains (e.g. etad guruṣu vṛttiḥ, this is the custom among Gurus, Mn. ii, 206)

[p. 231,2] : etad generally refers to what precedes, esp. when connected with idam, the latter then referring to what follows (e.g. eṣa vai prathamaḥ kalpaḥ . anukalpas tv ayaṃ jñeyaḥ, this before-mentioned is the principal rule, but this following may be considered a secondary rule, Mn. iii, 147)

[p. 231,2] : it refers also to that which follows, esp. when connected with a relative clause (e.g. eṣa caiva gurur dharmo yam pravakṣyāmy ahaṃ tava, this is the important law, which I will proclaim to you, MBh.), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 231,2] eta/d : ind. in this manner, thus, so, here, at this time, now (e.g. na/ vā/ u eta/n mriyase, thou dost not die in this manner or by that, RV. i, 162, 21), AV.; VS.; &c.;

[p. 231,2] etad : [cf. Zd. aêta; Old Pers. aita; Armen. aid; Osk. eiso.]
