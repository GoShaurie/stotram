[p. 389,2] cara : mfn. (g. pacādi) moving, locomotive (as animals opposed to plants, or as the Karaṇas in astrol.), VPrāt.; ŚvetUp. iii, 18; Mn. vii, 15; MBh. &c.

[p. 389,2] : (= saṃcārin) forming the retinue of any one, BhP. iv, 29, 23

[p. 389,2] : movable, shaking, unsteady, W.

[p. 389,2] : ifc. going, walking, wandering, being, living, practising (e.g. adhaś-, anta-, antarikṣa-, ap-, ādāya-, udake-, &c.; cf. Pāṇ. iii, 2, 16)

[p. 389,2] cara : mf(ī)n. ifc. (Pāṇ. v, 3, 53 f.; vi, 3, 35 f.) having been formerly (e.g. āḍhya-, devadatta-, qq.vv.; a-dṛṣṭa- or nadṛṣṭa-, ‘not seen before’, Kathās. [once f. irr. ā, lx, 58] Sarvad. iii, 16; vii, 19; an-ālokita- id., Bālar. iv, 54/55)

[p. 389,2] cara : m. a spy, secret emissary or agent, Mn. vii, 122; Hariv. 10316; R. &c.

[p. 389,2] : = caraṭa, L.

[p. 389,2] : the small shell Cypraea moneta, L.

[p. 389,2] : the wind, air, BhP. x, 14, 11

[p. 389,2] : the planet Mars, L.

[p. 389,2] : a game played with dice (similar to backgammon), L.

[p. 389,2] : a cowrie, W.

[p. 389,2] : ‘passage’, see a-, duś-

[p. 389,2] cara : n. (in astron.) ascensional difference, Gol. vii

[p. 1327,1] : (in astron., read, ‘the difference of time between the rising of a heavenly body at Laṅkā or Ceylon, over which the first meridian passes, and that of its rising at any partic. place’).
