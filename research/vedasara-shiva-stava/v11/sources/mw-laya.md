[p. 897,2] laya , : layana &c. See p. 903, col. 2.

[p. 903,2] laya : m. the act of sticking or clinging to (loc.), Śiś. (layaṃ √ gā with loc., ‘to become attached to any one’, Kuval.)

[p. 903,2] : lying down, cowering, MBh.

[p. 903,2] : melting, dissolution, disappearance or absorption in (loc. or comp.), Up.; Kap. &c. (layaṃ √ gam or yā ‘to disappear, be dissolved or absorbed’; layaṃ saṃ-√ gam, ‘to hide or conceal one's self’)

[p. 903,2] : extinction, destruction, death, MBh.; Kāv. &c. (layaṃ √ yā, ‘to be destroyed, perish’)

[p. 903,2] : rest, repose, Śiś.; BhP. (cf. a-laya)

[p. 903,2] : place of rest, residence, house, dwelling, W.

[p. 903,2] : mental inactivity, spiritual indifference, Kap.; Vedāntas.

[p. 903,2] : sport, diversion, merriness, Vās.

[p. 903,2] : delight in anything, Harav.

[p. 903,2] : an embrace, L.

[p. 903,2] : (in music) time (regarded as of 3 kinds, viz. druta, ‘quick’, madhya, ‘mean or moderate’, and vilambita, ‘slow’), Kālid.; Daśar.; Pañcat. &c.

[p. 903,2] : a kind of measure, Saṃgīt.

[p. 903,2] : the union of song, dance and instrumental music, L.

[p. 903,2] : a pause, MW.

[p. 903,2] : a partic. agricultural implement (perhaps a sort of harrow or hoe), VS.

[p. 903,2] : a swoon, L.

[p. 903,2] : the quick (downward) movement of an arrow, L.

[p. 903,2] laya : n. the root of Andropogon Muricatus, Bhpr.

[p. 903,2] laya : mfn. making the mind inactive or indifferent, BhP.
