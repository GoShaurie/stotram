[p. 341,2] ga/ṅgā : f. (√ gam, Uṇ.) ‘swift-goer’, the river Ganges (personified and considered as the eldest daughter of Himavat and Menā, R. i, 36, 15; as the wife of Śāntanu and mother of Bhīṣma, MBh. i, 3800; Hariv. 2967 ff.; or as one of the wives of Dharma, PadmaP. ; there is also a Gaṅgā in the sky [ākāśa- or vyoma-g˚, qq.vv.; cf. khāpagā, gaganāpagā &c.] and one below the earth, Hariv. 12782; Bhagī-ratha is said to have conducted the heavenly Gaṅgā down to the earth, 810 ff.; R. i, ch. 44), RV. x, 75, 5; ŚBr. xiii; TĀr. &c.

[p. 341,3] : N. of the wife of Nīla-kaṇṭha and mother of Śaṃkara

[p. 341,3] : ifc. See dviveda-gaṅga.
