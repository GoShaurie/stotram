[p. 611,3] paśu—pa/ti : m. ‘lord of animals’ (or ‘l˚ of a servant named Paśu’ or ‘l˚ of the soul’, RTL. 89), N. of the later Rudra-Śiva or of a similar deity (often associated in the Veda with Bhava, Śarva, Ugra, Rudra, Mahā-deva, Īśāna and others who together with Bhīma are in later times regarded as manifestations of Rudra), AV. &c. &c.

[p. 611,3] : of Agni.; TS.; ŚBr.

[p. 611,3] : of Śiva, MBh. &c. (according to one legend every deity acknowledged himself to be a mere paśu or animal when entreating Śiva to destroy the Asura Tri-pura)

[p. 611,3] : of a lexicographer

[p. 611,3] : of a Scholiast &c.
