Devanagari:
पशूनां पतिं पापनाशं परेशं
गजेन्द्रस्य कृत्तिं वसानं वरेण्यम् ।
जटाजूटमध्ये स्फुरद्गाङ्गवारिं
महादेवमेकं स्मरामि स्मरारिम् ॥ १ ॥

Transliteration:
paśūnāṃ patiṃ pāpanāśaṃ pareśaṃ
gajendrasya kṛttiṃ vasānaṃ vareṇyam .
jaṭājūṭamadhye sphuradgāṅgavāriṃ
mahādevamekaṃ smarāmi smarārim .. 1 ..

Translation:
(I meditate on him) who is the lord of the living beings (Pashus), who destroys our sins, and who is the transcendental god,
who wears the hide of the best of elephants, and who is himself the best,
From whose matted hairs is spurting out the holy waters of the great river ganga,
on that mahadeva (with one-pointed mind) I meditate, the one who is the enemy of smara (Kamadeva, representing desires).

Word-meanings:
पशूनां (paśūnāṃ) – of the living beings; पतिं (patiṃ) – lord; पापनाशं (pāpanāśaṃ) – destroyer of sins (pāpa:sins | nāśaṃ:destroyer); परेशं (pareśaṃ) – transcendental god (para:transcendental | īśaṃ:god); गजेन्द्रस्य (gajendrasya) – of the best elephant (gaja:elephant | indra:best); कृत्तिं (kṛttiṃ) – hide; वसानं (vasānaṃ) – wearing; वरेण्यम् (vareṇyam) – the best; जटाजूटमध्ये (jaṭājūṭamadhye) – in the midst of matted hair (jaṭā:matted hair | jūṭa:hair | madhye:in the midst); स्फुरद्गाङ्गवारिं (sphuradgāṅgavāriṃ) – spurting Ganga's water (sphurat:spurting | gāṅga:Ganga's | vāriṃ:water); महादेवम् (mahādevam) – Mahadeva; एकं (ekaṃ) – one; स्मरामि (smarāmi) – I meditate; स्मरारिम् (smarārim) – enemy of Smara (smara:Smara (Kamadeva) | arim:enemy);
