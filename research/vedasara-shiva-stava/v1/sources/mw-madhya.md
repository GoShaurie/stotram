[p. 781,3] : middlemost, intermediate, central, Var.; Megh.

[p. 781,3] : standing between two, impartial, neutral, Kām.

[p. 781,3] : middle i.e. being of a middle kind or size or quality, middling, moderate (with vṛtti f. ‘a middle course’), Lāṭy.; Mn.; Suśr. &c.

[p. 781,3] : (in astron.) mean i.e. theoretical (opp. to spaṣṭa, or sphuṭa), Sūryas. (-tva n.)

[p. 781,3] : lowest, worst, L.

[p. 781,3] ma/dhya : m. n. (ifc. f(ā). ) the middle of the body, (esp.) a woman's waist, ŚBr. &c. &c.

[p. 781,3] ma/dhya : m. (in alg.) the middle term or the mean of progression, Col.
