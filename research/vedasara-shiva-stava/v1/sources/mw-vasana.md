[p. 932,1] 1. va/sana : n. (for 2. see col. 3) cloth, clothes, dress, garment, apparel, attire (du. an upper and lower garment), RV. &c. &c. (ifc. f(ā). = clothed in, surrounded by, engrossed by i.e. wholly devoted or attached to, e.g. to a doctrine, Kāv.; Pur.)

[p. 932,1] : investment, siege, L.

[p. 932,1] : a leaf of the cinnamon tree, L.

[p. 932,1] va/sana : f(ā)n. an ornament worn by women round the loins, L.

[p. 932,3] 2. vasana : n. (for 1. See col. 1) dwelling, abiding, sojourn, residence in (comp.), MBh.
