[p. 781,3] ma/dhye : ind., See s.v.

[p. 782,3] madhye : ind. in the middle, in the midst, within, between, among, in the presence of (with gen. or ifc.; sometimes also ibc.; cf. Pāṇ. ii, 1, 18 and comp. below), Mn.; MBh. &c. (with √ kṛ [ind.p. -kṛtya or -kṛtvā, Pāṇ. i, 4, 76], to place in the middle, make an intermediary of Kull. on Mn. iv, 80; to count among, Kād.)
