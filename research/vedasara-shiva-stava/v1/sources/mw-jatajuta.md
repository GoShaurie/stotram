[p. 409,1] jaṭā—jūṭa : m. the long tresses of hair twisted on the top of the head, quantity of twisted hair (also applied to that of Śiva, Kathās. i, 18), BhP. v, 17, 3; Mahān.; Kathās. &c. (ifc. f(ā). , Hcat.)
