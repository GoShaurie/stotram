[p. 301,3] 1. kṛt : mfn. only ifc. (Pāṇ. vi, 1, 182) making, doing, performing, accomplishing, effecting, manufacturing, acting, one who accomplishes or performs anything, author (see su-k˚, karma-k˚, pāpa-k˚, &c.)

[p. 301,3] kṛt : m. an affix used to form nouns from roots, VPrāt.; Pāṇ. iii, 1, 93; 4, 67; vi, 1, 71; vii, 2, 8 and 11; 3, 33; viii, 4, 29

[p. 301,3] : a noun formed with that affix, Nir.; PārGṛ.; Gobh.; Pāṇ.

[p. 304,2] 2. kṛt : cl. 6. P. kṛnta/ti, ep. also Ā. ˚te and cl. 1. P. kartati (perf. cakarta, 2nd fut. kartsyati or kartiṣyati, 1st fut. kartitā, Pāṇ. vii, 2, 57 ; Subj. kṛnta/t; aor. akartīt, Ved. 2. sg. akṛtas),
to cut, cut in pieces, cut off, divide, tear asunder, destroy, RV.; AV.; AitBr.; MBh. &c.:
Caus. kartayati id., Pañcat. :
Desid. cikartiṣati or cikṛtsati, Pāṇ. vii, 2, 57;

[p. 304,2] kṛt : [cf. κείρω, κάρσις; Lith. kertu inf. kirsti, ‘to cut’; Slav. korjū, ‘to split’; Lat. curtus, culter; Hib. ceartaighim, ‘I prune, trim, cut’; cuirc, ‘a knife’.]

[p. 304,3] 3. kṛt : cl. 7. P. kṛṇa/tti (impf. 3. pl. a/kṛntan), to twist threads, spin, AV.; TS. ii; ŚBr. iii; MaitrS. = TāṇḍyaBr. (Nir. iii, 21);
to wind (as a snake), AV. i, 27, 2 (pr. p. f. kṛntatī/);
to surround, encompass, attire, Dhātup. xxix, 10.
