## Gaps (not verified, skipped rather than guessed)
- Śiva Purāṇa Rudra Saṃhitā (Yuddha khaṇḍa 2.5.9 Paśupati; Pārvatī khaṇḍa Kāma-dahana): sanskritdocuments.org and wisdomlib.org returned Cloudflare 403. GRETIL has only Śiva Purāṇa books 1 and 7. Liṅga Purāṇa 1.72 has almost the same Paśupati verses and comes with a translation.
- Mahābhārata Paśupati lines: these sit only in the CE apparatus (App. I no. 2 after 8.24.84b). They are not in the constituted text and not in Ganguli. Don't cite them as "MBh 8.24.x".
- Gajāsura in the Śiva or Skanda Purāṇa: not fetched. The Kūrma Purāṇa 1.30 Kṛttivāseśvara story is the only Gajāsura source here.
- Bhāgavata 8.7: Vedabase and GRETIL verse numbers differ by one. Each file gives both.
- Taittirīya Āraṇyaka was not fetched separately. The five Brahma-mantras come from the Mahānārāyaṇa (Āndhra) numbering on Wikisource.
- The sacred-texts.com and Project Gutenberg main sites were blocked or under maintenance. The mirrors used (ibiblio SriPedia, gutenberg.pglaf.org) are named in each file.
