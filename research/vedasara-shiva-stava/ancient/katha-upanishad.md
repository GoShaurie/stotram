[1.2.15] सर्वे वेदा यत् पदमामनन्ति तपाँसि सर्वाणि च यद् वदन्ति ।
यदिच्छन्तो ब्रह्मचर्यं चरन्ति तत् ते पदँ संग्रहेण ब्रवीम्योमित्येतत्
Gambhirananda: "15 I tell you briefly of that goal, which all the Vedas with one voice propound, which all the austerities speak of, and wishing for which people practise Brahmacarya: it is this, viz Om."

[1.2.16] एतद्ध्येवाक्षरं ब्रह्म एतद्ध्येवाक्षरं परं ।
एतद्ध्येवाक्षरं ज्ञात्वा यो यदिच्छति तस्य तत्
Gambhirananda: "16 This letter (Om), indeed, is the (inferior) Brahman (Hiranyagarbha); and this letterr is, indeed, the supreme Brahman. Anybody, who, (while) meditating on this letter, wants any of the two, to him comes that."

[1.3.15] अशब्दमस्पर्शमरूपमव्ययं तथाऽरसं नित्यमगन्धवच्च यत् ।
अनाद्यनन्तं महतः परं ध्रुवं निचाय्य तन्मृत्युमुखात् प्रमुच्यते
Gambhirananda: "15 One becomes freed from the jaws of death by knowing That which is soundless, touchless, colourless, undiminishing, and also tasteless, eternal, odourless, without beginning, and without end, distinct from Mahat, and ever constant."

[2.2.15] न तत्र सूर्यो भाति न चन्द्रतारकम् नेमा विद्युतो भान्ति कुतोऽयमग्निः ।
तमेव भान्तमनुभाति सर्वं तस्य भासा सर्वमिदं विभाति
।। इति द्वितीयेऽध्याये द्वितीया वल्ली ।।
Gambhirananda: "15 There the sun does not shine, neither do the moon and the stars; nor do these flashes of lightning shine. How can this fire? He shining, all these shine; through his lustre all these are variously illumined."
