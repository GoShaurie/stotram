[p. 274,2] kāraṇā : f. pain, agony, Daś.

[p. 274,2] : an astronomical period, W. [kāraṇāt, from some cause or reason, RPrāt. iii, 13; Mn. viii, 355; kasmāt kāraṇāt, from what cause? mama-kāraṇāt, for my sake, R. &c.; a-kāraṇena, without a reason, Yājñ. ii, 234; yena kāraṇena, because; yasmin kāraṇe, from which motive, wherefore.]
