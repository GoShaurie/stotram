[p. 136,3] 1. ādi : m. beginning, commencement

[p. 136,3] : a firstling, first-fruits

[p. 136,3] : ifc. beginning with, {{et caetera->et cætera||20260515|Dr. Dhaval Patel|https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/pull/2845}}, and so on (e.g. indrādayaḥ surāḥ, the gods beginning with Indra i.e. Indra &c.; gṛhādiyukta, possessed of houses &c.; evamādīni vastūni, such things and others of the same kind: śayyā khaṭvādiḥ [Comm. on Pāṇ. iii, 3, 99], Śayyā means a bed &c.; often with -ka at the end, e.g. dānadharmādikam [Hit.], liberality, justice, &c.)

[p. 1319,2] : the third part in the 7-partite Sāman, ChUp.

[p. 137,1] 2. ādi : mfn. beginning with ā, RāmatUp.
