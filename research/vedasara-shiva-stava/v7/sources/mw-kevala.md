[p. 309,3] ke/vala : mf(ā, ī)n. (m. nom. pl. e, RV. x, 51, 9) (f. ī, RV. x, 73, 6; AV.; ŚBr.; ā, Mn. &c. See Pāṇ. iv, 1, 30) (n. in comp. Pāṇ. ii, 1, 49) exclusively one's own (not common to others), RV.; AV.

[p. 309,3] : alone, only, mere, sole, one, excluding others, RV.; AV.; TS. &c.

[p. 309,3] : not connected with anything else, isolated, abstract, absolute

[p. 310,1] : simple, pure, uncompounded, unmingled, ŚBr. &c.

[p. 310,1] : entire, whole, all, Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 310,1] : selfish, envious, L.

[p. 310,1] ke/vala : m. (= kelaka) a dancer, tumbler, Gal.

[p. 310,1] : N. of a prince, BhP. ix, 2, 30

[p. 310,1] ke/vala : n. the doctrine of the absolute unity of spirit

[p. 310,1] : the highest possible knowledge (= kevala-jñāna), Jain.

[p. 310,1] : N. of a country (v.l. kerala), MBh. vi, 9, 34.
