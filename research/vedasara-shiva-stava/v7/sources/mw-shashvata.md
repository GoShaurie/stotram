[p. 1068,3] śāśvata/ : mf(ī)n. (fr. śaśvat) eternal, constant, perpetual, all (śā/śvatībhyaḥ sa/mābhyaḥ, śāśvatīḥ samāḥ, or śāśvatam, for evermore, incessantly, eternally), VS. &c. &c.

[p. 1068,3] : about to happen, future, MW.

[p. 1068,3] śāśvata/ : m. N. of Śiva, L.

[p. 1068,3] : of Vyāsa, L.

[p. 1068,3] : of a son of Śruta (and father of Su-dhanvan), VP.

[p. 1068,3] : of a poet and various other writers (esp. of a lexicographer, author of the Anekārtha-samuccaya)

[p. 1068,3] śāśvata/ : n. continuity, eternity, MBh.

[p. 1068,3] : heaven, ether, W.
