[p. 584,1] pa/dya : mf(ā)n. (fr. 3. pad and pada) relating or belonging to a foot, RV.; Kāṭh.

[p. 584,1] : hurting or coming in contact with the feet, Pāṇ. iv, 4, 83; vi, 3, 53. Sch.

[p. 584,1] : marked with footsteps, ib. iv, 4, 87, Sch.

[p. 584,1] : measuring a Pada in length or breadth, KātyŚr. (also in comp. with numerals; cf. ardha-, daśa-)

[p. 584,1] : consisting of Padas or parts of verses, Br.; ĀśvGṛ.; RPrāt.

[p. 584,1] : consisting of one Pada, KātyŚr., Sch.

[p. 584,1] : forming the end, final, APrāt.

[p. 584,1] pa/dya : m. a Śūdra, L. (cf. paj-ja)

[p. 584,1] : a part of a word, verbal element, RPrāt.

[p. 584,1] pa/dya : n. a verse, metre, poetry (opp. to gadya, prose), Vām.; Kāvyād.; Sāh. &c.

[p. 584,1] : N. of sev. hymns.
