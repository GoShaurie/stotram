[p. 1296,2] hīna/ : mfn. left, abandoned, forsaken, RV.

[p. 1296,2] : left behind, excluded or shut out from, lower or weaker than, inferior to (abl.), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1296,3] : left out, wanting, omitted, MBh.

[p. 1296,3] : defeated or worsted (in a lawsuit), Yājñ.

[p. 1296,3] : deficient, defective, faulty, insufficient, short, incomplete, poor, little, low, vile, bad, base, mean, ŚBr. &c. &c.

[p. 1296,3] : bereft or deprived of, free from, devoid or destitute of, without (instr., abl., loc., acc., or comp.; prāṇair hīnaḥ, ‘bereft of breath or life’; mantrād or mantrato h˚, ‘devoid of sacred knowledge’), MuṇḍUp.; KātyŚr.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1296,3] : lost or strayed from (a caravan), Pāṇ. i, 4, 23, Kāś.

[p. 1296,3] : brought low, broken down in circumstances, ŚrS.

[p. 1296,3] hīna/ : m. a faulty or defective witness (of five kinds, viz. anya-vādin, kriyā-dveṣin, nopasthāyin, nir-uttara, āhūta-prapalāyin), Yājñ., Sch.

[p. 1296,3] : subtraction (= = vyavakalana), MW.

[p. 1296,3] : Mesua Ferrea, L.

[p. 1296,3] hīna/ : (am), n. deficiency, want, absence (velā-hīne ‘before the right time’, ‘unseasonably’), VarBṛS.; Yājñ.
