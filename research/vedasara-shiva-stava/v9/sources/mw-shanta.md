[p. 1064,2] 1. śānta : mfn. (perhaps always w.r. for 1. śāta, q.v.) = śānita, L.

[p. 1064,2] : thin, slender, Hariv.; R. (Sch.)

[p. 1064,2] 2. śānta/ : mfn. (fr. √ 1. śam) appeased, pacified, tranquil, calm, free from passions, undisturbed, Up.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1064,2] : soft, pliant, Hariv.

[p. 1064,2] : gentle, mild, friendly, kind, auspicious (in augury; opp. to dīpta), AV. &c. &c.

[p. 1064,2] : abated, subsided, ceased, stopped, extinguished, averted (śāntam or dhik śāntam or śāntam pāpam, may evil or sin be averted! may God forfend! Heaven forbid! not so!), ŚBr.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1064,2] : rendered ineffective, innoxious, harmless (said of weapons), MBh.; R.

[p. 1064,2] : come to an end, gone to rest, deceased, departed, dead, died out, ib.; Ragh.; Rājat.

[p. 1064,2] : purified, cleansed, W.

[p. 1064,2] śānta/ : m. an ascetic whose passions are subdued, W.

[p. 1064,2] : tranquillity, contentment (as one of the Rasas q.v.)

[p. 1064,2] : N. of a son of Day, MBh.

[p. 1064,2] : of a son of Manu Tāmasa, MārkP.

[p. 1064,2] : of a son of Śambara, Hariv.

[p. 1064,2] : of a son of Idhma-jihva, BhP.

[p. 1064,2] : of a son of Āpa, VP.

[p. 1064,2] : of a Devaputra, Lalit.

[p. 1064,2] śānta/ : n. tranquillity, peace of mind, BhP.

[p. 1064,2] : N. of a Varṣa in Jambu-dvīpa, ib.

[p. 1064,2] : N. of a Tīrtha, W.
