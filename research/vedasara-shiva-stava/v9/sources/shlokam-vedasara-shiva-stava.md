Devanagari:
प्रभो शूलपाणे विभो विश्वनाथ
महादेव शंभो महेश त्रिनेत्र ।
शिवाकान्त शान्त स्मरारे पुरारे
त्वदन्यो वरेण्यो न मान्यो न गण्यः ॥ ९ ॥

Transliteration:
prabho śūlapāṇe vibho viśvanātha
mahādeva śaṃbho maheśa trinetra .
śivākānta śānta smarāre purāre
tvadanyo vareṇyo na mānyo na gaṇyaḥ .. 9 ..

Translation:
O lord (Prabhu), O the one with trident in his hand (Shulapani), O the all-pervading one (Vibhu), O the lord of the universe (Vishwanatha),
O the great god (mahadeva), O the one who causes happiness to all (shambhu), O the great lord (Mahesha), the one with three eyes (Trinetra),
the beloved of devi Parvati (Shiva Kanta), an embodiment of peace (Shanta), the one who was an enemy of Kama (smarari) and tripura (Purari),
O lord, apart from you there is no Other (for me) to be wished for, venerated and considered (for worship).

Word-meanings:
प्रभो (prabho) – O lord; शूलपाणे (śūlapāṇe) – O the one with trident (śūla:trident | pāṇe:in hand); विभो (vibho) – O the all-pervading one; विश्वनाथ (viśvanātha) – O the lord of the universe (viśva:universe | nātha:lord); महादेव (mahādeva) – O the great god (mahā:great | deva:god); शंभो (śambho) – O the one who causes happiness; महेश (maheśa) – O the great lord (mahā:great | īśa:lord); त्रिनेत्र (trinetra) – the one with three eyes (tri:three | netra:eyes); शिवाकान्त (śivākānta) – the beloved of devi Parvati (śiva:Shiva | kānta:beloved); शान्त (śānta) – an embodiment of peace; स्मरारे (smarāre) – enemy of Kama (smara:Kama | ari:enemy); पुरारे (purāre) – enemy of tripura (pura:tripura | ari:enemy); त्वदन्यो (tvadanyo) – apart from you (tvad:you | anyaḥ:other); वरेण्यो (vareṇyo) – to be wished for; न (na) – not; मान्यो (mānyo) – venerated; न (na) – not; गण्यः (gaṇyaḥ) – considered;
