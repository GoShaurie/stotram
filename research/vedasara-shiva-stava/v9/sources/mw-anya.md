[p. 45,2] 1. a/nya : (3), am, n. inexhaustibleness (as of the milk of cows), AV. xii, 1, 4 (cf. a/nyā.).

[p. 45,2] 2. anya/ : as, ā, at other, different

[p. 45,2] : other than, different from, opposed to (abl. or in comp.)

[p. 45,2] : another

[p. 45,2] : another person

[p. 45,2] : one of a number

[p. 45,2] : anya anya or eka anya, the one, the other

[p. 45,2] : anyac ca, and another, besides, moreover

[p. 45,2] anya : [cf. Zend anya; Armen. ail; Lat. alius; Goth. aljis, Theme alja; Gk. ἄλλος for ἄλϳο-ς; cf. also ἔνιοι].
