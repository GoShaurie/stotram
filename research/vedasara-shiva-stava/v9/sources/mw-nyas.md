[p. 572,1] ny-√ as : P. -asyati, or -asati, to throw or cast or lay or put down (with loc., e.g. bhuvi, on the earth), AV. &c. &c.;
to take off, MBh.; R.;
to give up, resign (as life), R.;
to set in the ground, plant (prarohān, shoots), Bālar.;
 (with loc.) to throw or hurl upon, pour or shed on or in, put or place or fix or insert in, turn or direct to, deposit with, intrust or commit to;
to settle arrange, MBh.; Kāv. &c. (with citre, ‘to place in a picture’, paint, depict; with śirasi, ‘to place on the head’, receive with reverence; with manasi, ‘to call to mind’, reflect, ponder; with pathi, ‘to lay on the road’, give up);
to bring forward, mention, Mall. :
Caus. (pf. nyāsayām āsa or ˚sayāṃ cakre) to cause to put or lay down, MBh.
