[p. 1074,2] śivā/ : f. Śiva's wife (also śivī) See śivā below

[p. 1075,3] śivā : f. the energy of Śiva personified as his wife (known as Durgā, Pārvatī &c.), Inscr.; Kāv.; Kathās.; Pur.

[p. 1075,3] : final emancipation (= mukti), Pur.

[p. 1075,3] : a euphemistic N. of a jackal (generally regarded as an animal of bad omen), GṛS.; Baudh.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1076,1] : N. of various plants (accord. to L. ‘Prosopis Spicigera or Mimosa Suma; Terminalia Chebula or Citrina, Emblica Officinalis; Jasminum Auriculatum; turmeric; Dūrvā grass &c.’)

[p. 1076,1] : the root of Piper longum, L.

[p. 1076,1] : a kind of yellow pigment (= ˚go-rocanā), L.

[p. 1076,1] : a kind of metre, L.

[p. 1076,1] : (in music) a partic. Śruti, Saṃgīt.

[p. 1076,1] : N. of the wife of Anila, MBh.

[p. 1076,1] : of the wife of Aṅgiras, ib.

[p. 1076,1] : of a Brāhman woman, ib.

[p. 1076,1] : of the mother of Nemi (the 22nd Arhat of the present Avasarpiṇī), L.

[p. 1076,1] : of the mother of Rudra-bhaṭṭa, Cat.

[p. 1076,1] : of a river, MBh.; Hariv. (In the following comp. not always, distinguishable from śiva m. or n.)
