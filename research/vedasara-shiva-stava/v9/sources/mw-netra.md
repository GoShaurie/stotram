[p. 568,3] netra : m. a leader, guide (with gen. R. [B.] iii, 66, 10; mostly ifc., e.g. tvaṃ-netra, ‘having you for guide’, MBh. ii, 2486 [f. ā, ib. ix, 222]; cf. Pāṇ. v, 4, 116, Vārtt. 2, 3, Pat.), AitUp.; MBh.; BhP.

[p. 568,3] : N. of a son of Dharma and father of Kuntī, BhP.

[p. 568,3] : of a son of Su-mati, MatsyaP.

[p. 568,3] netra/ : n. (and m., Siddh.) leading, guiding, conducting, AV. x, 10, 22

[p. 568,3] : (ifc. f(ā). ) the eye (as the guiding organ, also -ka, Hcat.; cf. nayana)

[p. 568,3] : the numeral 2 Sūryas. (cf. netṛ)

[p. 568,3] : the string by which a churning-stick is whirled round, MBh.

[p. 568,3] : a pipe-tube, Car.

[p. 568,3] : an injection pipe, Suśr.

[p. 568,3] : the root of a tree, Kād.

[p. 568,3] : a kind of cloth, Hcar.

[p. 568,3] : a veil, R.; Ragh.

[p. 568,3] : a carriage, L.

[p. 568,3] : a river, L.
