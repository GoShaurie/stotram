[p. 755,3] 1. bhās : cl. 1. Ā. (Dhātup. xvi, 23) bhāsate (in older language also P. bhā/sati, AV. &c.; p. bhā/sat, RV.; pf. babhāse, MBh.; aor. abhāsiṣṭa Gr.; fut. bhāsiṣyate, bhāsitā, ib.),
to shine, be bright, RV. &c. &c.;
to appear (‘as’ or ‘like’ nom. or instr. of an abstract noun), occur to the mind, be conceived or imagined, become clear or evident, Sāh.; Vedāntas. &c.:
Caus. bhāsayati, ˚te (aor. ababhāsat and abībhasat, Pāṇ. vii, 4, 3), to make shine, illuminate, Up.; MBh. &c. ;
to show, make evident, cause to appear (‘by way of’, instr. of an abstract noun), Bhaṭṭ.; Cat. Desid. bibhāsiṣate Gr.:
Intens. bābhāsyate, bābhāsti, ib. (cf. √ bhā, of which √ bhās is a secondary form).

[p. 756,1] 2. bhā/s : nf. (cf. 2. bhā) light or ray of light, lustre, brightness, RV. &c. &c. (bhāsāṃ nidhi [Prasaṅg.] and bhāsām pati [Hcat.] m. ‘receptacle or lord of rays of light’, the sun)

[p. 756,1] : an image, reflection shadow, MW.

[p. 756,1] : glory, splendour, majesty, L.

[p. 756,1] : wish, desire, L.
