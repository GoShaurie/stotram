[p. 343,1] gaṇa/ : as, m. a flock, troop, multitude, number, tribe, series, class (of animate or inanimate beings), body of followers or attendants, RV.; AV. &c.

[p. 343,1] : troops or classes of inferior deities (especially certain troops of demi-gods considered as Śiva's attendants and under the special superintendence of the god Gaṇeśa; cf. -devatā), Mn.; Yājñ.; Lalit. &c.

[p. 343,1] : a single attendant of Śiva, VarBṛS.; Kathās.; Rājat. iii, 270

[p. 343,1] : N. of Gaṇeśa, W.

[p. 343,1] : a company, any assemblage or association of men formed for the attainment of the same aims, Mn.; Yājñ.; Hit.

[p. 343,1] : the 9 assemblies of Ṛṣis under the Arhat Mahā-vīra, Jain.

[p. 343,1] : a sect in philosophy or religion, W.

[p. 343,1] : a small body of troops (= 3 Gulmas or 27 chariots and as many elephants, 81 horses, and 135 foot), MBh. i, 291

[p. 343,1] : a series or group of asterisms or lunar mansions classed under three heads (that of the gods, that of the men, and that of the Rākṣasas), W.

[p. 343,1] : (in arithm.) a number, L.

[p. 343,1] : (in metre) a foot or four instants (cf. -cchandas)

[p. 343,1] : (in Gr.) a series of roots or words following the same rule and called after the first word of the series (e.g. ad-ādi, the g. ad &c. or the whole series of roots of the 2nd class; gargādi, the g. garga &c. or the series of words commencing with garga)

[p. 343,1] : a particular group of Sāmans, Lāṭy. i, 6, 5; VarYogay. viii, 7

[p. 343,1] : a kind of perfume, L.

[p. 343,1] : = vāc (i.e. ‘a series of verses’), Naigh. i, 11

[p. 343,1] : N. of an author
