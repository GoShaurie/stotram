[p. 260,3] kalatra : n. a wife, consort, MBh.; Bhartṛ.; Hit. &c.

[p. 260,3] : the female of an animal, Vikr.

[p. 260,3] : the hip and loins, L.

[p. 260,3] : pudendum muliebre, L.

[p. 260,3] : a royal citadel, a stronghold or fastness, W.

[p. 260,3] : (in astron.) the seventh lunar mansion, VarBṛ.
