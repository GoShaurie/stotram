[p. 764,2] bhūṣ : cl. 1. P. bhū/ṣati (pf. bubhūṣa Gr.; aor. abhūṣīt, ib.; fut. bhūṣiṣyati, bhūṣitā, ib.; inf. bhūṣitum, ib.),
to strive after, use efforts for, be intent upon (dat.), RV. iii, 25, 2; 34, 2 &c.;
to seek to procure (acc.) for (dat.), ib. ix, 94, 3;
to adorn, Dhātup. xvii, 30 :
Caus. bhūṣayati (Dhātup. xxxiii, 56, ep. also ˚te; aor. abubhūṣat; inf. bhūṣayitum),
to adorn, embellish, attire (Ā. also, ‘one's self’, Pāṇ. iii, 1, 87, Vārtt. 18, Pat.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.
