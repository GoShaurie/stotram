[p. 912,2] vaktra : n. ‘organ of speech’, the mouth, face, muzzle, snout, proboscis, jaws, beak &c., Mn.; MBh. &c. (vaktraṃ √ kṛ, to open the mouth, gape)

[p. 912,2] : the point (of an arrow), MBh.

[p. 912,2] : the spout (of a jug or vessel See a-vaktra)

[p. 912,2] : beginning, commencement, Gaṇit.

[p. 912,2] : (in alg.) the initial quantity or first term of a progression, Col.

[p. 912,2] : a metre containing 4 × 8 syllables, Kāvyād.

[p. 912,2] : a sort of garment, L.

[p. 912,2] : the root of Tabernaemontana Coronaria, L.

[p. 912,2] vaktra : m. N. of a king of the Karūṣas, MW. (v.l. vakra).
