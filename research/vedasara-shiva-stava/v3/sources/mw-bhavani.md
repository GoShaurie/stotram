[p. 749,3] bhavānī : f. (fr. bhava; cf. indrāṇī, rudrāṇī, śarvāṇī and Pāṇ. iv, 1, 49) N. of a goddess identified in later times with Pārvatī (she is the wife of Śiva in her pacific and amiable form; cf. RTL. 79), GṛŚrS.; Hariv. &c.

[p. 749,3] : of various women, Cat.

[p. 749,3] : of a river, L.
