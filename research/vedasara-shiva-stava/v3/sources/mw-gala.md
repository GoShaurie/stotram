[p. 350,3] 1. gala : m. ‘oozing’, resin (especially that of the plant Shorea robusta), L.

[p. 350,3] : = galaka, L.

[p. 350,3] : a kind of musical instrument, L.

[p. 350,3] : a reed (large kind of Saccharum cylindricum), L.

[p. 350,3] : a rope (made of that reed), L.

[p. 350,3] 2. gala : m. (Pāṇ. viii, 2, 21, Kāś.) ‘swallower’, the throat, neck, MBh.; Mṛcch. &c. (ifc. f(ā). [g. kroḍādi] Hcat. i, 7, 334; f(ī). g. bahv-adi);

[p. 350,3] gala : [cf. Lat. gula.]
