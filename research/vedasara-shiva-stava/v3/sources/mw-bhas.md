[p. 750,1] 1. bhas : cl. 3. P. (Dhātup. xxv, 18) ba/bhasti or (3. sg. and pl.) ba/psati (2. du. bhasathas, RV.; Subj. babhasat or bha/sat, ib.; babdhām, Pāṇ. vi, 4, 100; fut. bhasitā, vii, 2, 8 Vārtt. 1, Pat.; inf. bhasitum, ib.),
to chew, masticate, devour, consume, RV.; AV.; ŚBr. (cf. √ psā);
to blame, abuse, Dhātup. xxv, 18 (cf. √ bharts);
to shine, ib.
