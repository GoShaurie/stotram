[p. 885,2] rūḍha/ : mfn. mounted, risen, ascended, AV. &c. &c.

[p. 885,2] : lifted up, imposed on, laden (see -paricchada)

[p. 885,2] : grown together, healed, R.; Suśr.

[p. 885,2] : sprung up, grown, increased, developed, produced from (comp.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 885,2] : budded, blown, W.

[p. 885,2] : large, great, MW.

[p. 885,2] : high, noble (see -vaṃśa)

[p. 885,2] : diffused, spread about, widely known, current, notorious, famous, Kāv.; Sāh.

[p. 885,2] : traditional, conventional, popular (opp. to yaugika and said of words which have a meaning not directly connected with their etymology; esp. in pl. applied to names of warrior tribes, which also denote the country inhabited by them), Śiś.; Pāṇ., Sch. &c.

[p. 885,2] : acquainted or conversant with (loc.), Gaṇit.

[p. 885,2] : certain, ascertained, W.

[p. 885,2] : obscure, MW.

[p. 885,2] rūḍha/ : m. a scar (also n. and f(ā). )

[p. 885,2] rūḍha/ : m. barley, L.
