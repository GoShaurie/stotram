[p. 615,2] 1. pāṇi : m. a place of sale, shop, market, W.

[p. 615,2] 2. pāṇi/ : m. (said to be fr. √ paṇ) the hand, RV. &c. &c. (often ifc. = holding in the h˚, e.g. asi-p˚, holding a sword in the h˚, s˚ in h˚; pāṇiṃ-√ grah or ˚ṇau-√ kṛ, to take the h˚ of a bride, marry; ˚niṃ√ dā, to give the h˚ in marriage)

[p. 615,2] : a hoof, RV. ii, 31, 2

[p. 615,2] : N. of Sch. on the Daśa-rūpaka, Cat.

[p. 615,2] pāṇi : [Orig. palni; cf. Gk. παλάμη; Lat. palma; Angl.Sax. folm; Germ. fühlen; Eng. to feel.]
