Devanagari:
शंभो महेश करुणामय शूलपाणे
गौरीपते पशुपते पशुपाशनाशिन् ।
काशीपते करुणया जगदेतदेक-
स्त्वंहंसि पासि विदधासि महेश्वरोऽसि ॥ १० ॥

Transliteration:
śaṃbho maheśa karuṇāmaya śūlapāṇe
gaurīpate paśupate paśupāśanāśin .
kāśīpate karuṇayā jagadetadeka
tvaṃhaṃsi pāsi vidadhāsi maheśvaro'si .. 10 ..

Translation:
O Shambhu, O Mahesha, O the compassionate one (Karunamaya) with trident in hand (Shulapani),
O the consort of Gauri (Gauripati), the lord of all beings (Pashupati) who severe their bondages (Pashupasha nashi),
O the lord of Kashi (Kashipati); By your compassion (and will), this universe, you alone ...
destroy, protect and create, O you are the maheshwara (the great god).

Word-meanings:
शंभो (śaṃbho) – O Shambhu; महेश (maheśa) – O Mahesha; करुणामय (karuṇāmaya) – O compassionate one; शूलपाणे (śūlapāṇe) – O trident in hand (śūla:trident | pāṇe:in hand); गौरीपते (gaurīpate) – O consort of Gauri (gaurī:Gauri | pate:consort of); पशुपते (paśupate) – O lord of beings (paśu:beings | pate:lord of); पशुपाशनाशिन् (paśupāśanāśin) – who severs bondages (paśu:beings | pāśa:bondages | nāśin:destroyer); काशीपते (kāśīpate) – O lord of Kashi (kāśī:Kashi | pate:lord of); करुणया (karuṇayā) – by compassion; जगदेतदेक (jagadetadeka) – this universe, you alone (jagat:universe | etat:this | eka:alone); त्वंहंसि (tvaṃhaṃsi) – you destroy (tvam:you | haṃsi:destroy); पासि (pāsi) – protect; विदधासि (vidadhāsi) – create; महेश्वरः (maheśvaraḥ) – great god; असि (asi) – are;
