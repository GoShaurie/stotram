[p. 611,2] 1. paśu : ind. see, behold! L.

[p. 611,2] 2. paśu/ or pa/śu, : m. (instr. paśu/nā or ˚śva; dat. pa/śve or paśa/ve; gen. paśva/s or ˚śos; du. paśvā/; acc. pl. paśva/s or ˚śū/n) cattle, kine (orig. ‘any tethered animal’; singly or collect. ‘a herd’), a domestic or sacrificial animal (as opp. to mṛga, ‘wild animal’; 5 kinds are enumerated, ‘men, kine, horses, goats and sheep’ [AV. xi, 2, 9 &c.], to which are sometimes added mules and asses [MBh. vi, 155 &c.] or camels and dogs [AV. iii, 10, 6 Comm.]), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 611,3] : any animal or brute or beast (also applied contemptuously to a man; cf. nara-p˚ and nṛ-p˚)

[p. 611,3] : a mere animal in sacred things i.e. an uninitiated person, Cat.

[p. 611,3] : an animal sacrifice, AitĀr.; BhP.

[p. 611,3] : flesh, RV. i, 166, 6 an ass, L.

[p. 611,3] : a goat, L.

[p. 611,3] : a subordinate deity and one of Śiva's followers, L.

[p. 611,3] : (with Māheśvaras and Pāśupatas) the individual soul as distinct from the divine Soul of the universe, RTL. 89

[p. 611,3] : Ficus Glomerata, L.

[p. 611,3] : (pl.) N. of a people, MBh. (v.l. patti)

[p. 611,3] pa/śu : cattle (only as acc. before manyate [VS. xxiii, 30] and manyamāna [RV. iii, 53, 23]; and pl. paśūni, R.; Kathās.)

[p. 611,3] paśu : [cf. Zd. pasu; Lat. pecu; Old Pruss. pecku; Goth. faíhu; Germ. fihu, vihe, Vieh; Angl.Sax. feoh; Eng. fee.]
