[p. 802,2] maheśvara : m. a great lord, sovereign, chief, ŚvetUp.; MBh. &c. (with tridaśānām, ‘chief of the gods’ i.e. Indra)

[p. 802,2] : a god (opp. to prakṛti), ŚvetUp.

[p. 802,2] : N. of various gods (esp. of Śiva and of Kṛṣṇa; pl. of the Loka-pālas or guardians of the world, viz. Indra, Agni, Yama and Varuṇa), MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 802,2] : of a Deva-putra, Lalit.

[p. 802,2] : of various authors and other men, Inscr.; Siddhāntaś.; Cat.

[p. 802,2] : bdellium, L.
