[p. 280,2] kāśi—pati : m. id., MBh. i, 4083; Bhag.

[p. 280,2] : N. of Divodāsa Dhanvantari (a king of Benares, author of certain medical works and teacher of the Āyur-veda; he is often confounded with the celestial namesake, the physician of the gods), Suśr.
