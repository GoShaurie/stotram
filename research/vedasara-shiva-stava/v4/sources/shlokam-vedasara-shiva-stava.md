Devanagari:
शिवाकान्त शंभो शशाङ्कार्धमौले
महेशान शूलिञ्जटाजूटधारिन् ।
त्वमेको जगद्व्यापको विश्वरूपः
प्रसीद प्रसीद प्रभो पूर्णरूप ॥ ४ ॥

Transliteration:
śivākānta śaṃbho śaśāṅkārdhamaule
maheśāna śūliñ jaṭājūṭadhārin .
tvameko jagadvyāpako viśvarūpaḥ
prasīda prasīda prabho pūrṇarūpa .. 4 ..

Translation:
O Shambhu (giver of happiness), the beloved of Shiva (devi Parvati); O the one with the half-moon on his head,
O the great master (Maheshana) holding the trident (in his hand) and bearing the matted hairs (on his head),
you are the one who pervades the entire universe; and whose form is the universe itself (Vishwarupa),
Be pleased with us, be pleased with us, O lord, whose form is the fullness (of consciousness); (be pleased with us and make us merge in your fullness).

Word-meanings:
शिवाकान्त (śivākānta) – beloved of Shiva (śiva:Shiva | kānta:beloved); शंभो (śambho) – O Shambhu; शशाङ्कार्धमौले (śaśāṅkārdhamaule) – with half-moon on head (śaśāṅka:moon | ardha:half | maule:on head); महेशान (maheśāna) – great master; शूलिञ्जटाजूटधारिन् (śūliñjaṭājūṭadhārin) – holding trident, bearing matted hair (śūlin:holding trident | jaṭā:matted hair | jūṭa:hair | dhārin:bearing); त्वमेकः (tvamekaḥ) – you are one; जगद्व्यापकः (jagadvyāpakaḥ) – pervades the universe (jagat:universe | vyāpakaḥ:pervades); विश्वरूपः (viśvarūpaḥ) – whose form is universe (viśva:universe | rūpaḥ:form); प्रसीद (prasīda) – be pleased; प्रसीद (prasīda) – be pleased; प्रभो (prabho) – O lord; पूर्णरूप (pūrṇarūpa) – form of fullness (pūrṇa:fullness | rūpa:form);
