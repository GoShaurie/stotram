[p. 1037,2] vy-āpaka : mf(ikā)n. pervading, diffusive, comprehensive, widely spreading or extending, spreading everywhere (vyāpakaṃ ny-√ as or nyāsaṃ√ kṛ, to put or place or fix or make applicable everywhere, AgP.), KaṭhUp.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1037,2] : (in logic) invariably pervading or inherent or concomitant (as an attribute which is always found [as smoke] where some other [as fire] is found), Bhāṣāp.; IW. 62

[p. 1037,2] : (in law) comprehending all the points of an argument, pervading the whole plea, W.
