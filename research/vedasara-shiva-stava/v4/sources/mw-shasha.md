[p. 1060,1] śaśa/ : m. a hare, rabbit, or antelope (the markings on the moon are supposed to resemble a hare or rabbit), RV. &c. &c. (for śaśasya vrata See under karṣū, p. 260)

[p. 1060,1] : a kind of meteor, AV. v, 17, 4

[p. 1060,1] : N. of a man born under a partic. constellation, VarBṛS.

[p. 1060,1] : a man of mild character and easily led (one of the four classes into which men are divided by erotic writers, the other three being aśva, mṛga and vṛṣan), L.

[p. 1060,1] : the Lodhra tree, Symplocos Racemosa, Kād.

[p. 1060,1] : gum-myrrh, L.

[p. 1060,1] : N. of a part of Jambu-dvīpa, MW.

[p. 1060,1] śaśa : [cf. accord. to some, Gk. κεκήν; accord. to others, śaśa is for śasa and is connected with Germ. haso, Hase; Eng. hare.]
