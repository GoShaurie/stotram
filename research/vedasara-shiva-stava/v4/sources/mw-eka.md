[p. 227,3] e/ka : mfn. (√ i, Uṇ. iii, 43, probably fr. a base e; cf. Zd. ae-va; Gk. οἰ-ν-ός, οἶος; Goth. ai-n-s; also Lat. aequu-s; g. sarvādi, Pāṇ. i, 1, 27; see Gr. 200), one (eko'pi, or ekaś-cana, with na preceding or following, no one, nobody; the words ekayā na or ekān na are used before decade numerals to lessen them by one, e.g. ekān na triṃśat, twenty-nine), RV. &c.

[p. 227,3] : (with and without eva) alone, solitary, single, happening only once, that one only (frequently ifc.; cf. dharmaika-rakṣa, &c.), RV. &c.

[p. 227,3] : the same, one and the same, identical, ŚBr. v; KātyŚr.; Mn. &c.

[p. 227,3] : one of two or many (eka — eka, eka — dvitīya, the one — the other; esp. pl. eke, some, eke — apare some — others, &c.), ŚBr.; KātyŚr.; MBh.; Hit. &c.

[p. 227,3] : (eka repeated twice, either as a compound [cf. ekaika] or uncompounded, may have the sense ‘one and one’, ‘one by one’, RV. i, 20, 7; 123, 8; v, 52, 17; R.; BhP. &c.)

[p. 227,3] : single of its kind, unique, singular, chief, pre-eminent, excellent, Ragh.; Kathās.; Kum. &c.

[p. 227,3] : sincere, truthful, MW.

[p. 227,3] : little, small, L.

[p. 227,3] : (sometimes used as an indefinite article), a, an, R.; Śak.; Vet. &c. (the fem. of eka before a Taddhita suffix and as first member of a compound is eka not ekā, Pāṇ. vi, 3, 62)

[p. 227,3] e/ka : m. N. of a teacher, Āp.

[p. 227,3] : of a son of Raya, BhP.

[p. 227,3] e/ka : n. unity, a unit (ifc.), Hcat.
