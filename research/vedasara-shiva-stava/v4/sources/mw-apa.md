[p. 142,2] 1. āpa : m. obtaining

[p. 142,2] āpa : mfn. ifc. to be obtained (cf. dur˚).

[p. 142,2] 2. āpa : m. N. of one of the eight demigods called Vasus, VP.; Hariv.; MBh.

[p. 142,2] 3. āpa : n. (fr. 2. ap, Pāṇ. iv, 2, 37), a quantity of water, Mallinātha on Śiś. iii, 72.
