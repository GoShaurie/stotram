[p. 1037,2] vy-√ āp : P. -āpnoti (rarely Ā. -āpnute), to reach or spread through, pervade, permeate, cover, fill, AV. &c. &c.;
to reach as far as, extend to, L. (cf. Pāṇ. v, 2, 8) :
Pass. vy-āpyate (see vyāpyamāna) :
Caus. vy-āpayati (see vy-āpita) :
Desid. vīpsati (see vīpsa).
