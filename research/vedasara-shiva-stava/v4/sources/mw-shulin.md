[p. 1087,1] : one who suffers from sharp internal pain or from colic, Kauś.; Vcar.; Hcat.

[p. 1087,1] śūlin : m. a spearman, lancer, L.

[p. 1087,1] : N. of Rudra Śiva (as holding a trident), MBh.

[p. 1087,1] : a hare, Bhpr.
