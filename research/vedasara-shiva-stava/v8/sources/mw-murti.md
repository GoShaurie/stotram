[p. 824,1] mū/rti : f. any solid body or material form, (pl. material elements, solid particles; ifc. = consisting or formed of), Up.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 824,1] : embodiment, manifestation, incarnation, personification, TBr.; Mn.; MBh. &c. (esp. of Śiva, Hcat.)

[p. 824,1] : anything which has definite shape or limits (in phil. as mind and the 4 elements earth, air, fire, water, but not ākāśa, ether, IW. 52 n. 1), a person, form, figure, appearance, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 824,1] : an image, idol, statue, Kāv.

[p. 824,1] : beauty, Pañcat.

[p. 824,1] : N. of the first astrological house, VarBṛS.

[p. 824,1] : of a daughter of Dakṣa and wife of Dharma, BhP.

[p. 824,1] mū/rti : m. N. of a Ṛṣi under the 10th Manu, ib.

[p. 824,1] : of a son of Vasiṣṭha, VP.
