[p. 348,1] gamya : mfn. to be gone or gone to, approachable, accessible, passable, attainable (often a- neg.), MBh. &c.

[p. 348,1] : to be fixed (as to the number, saṃkhyayā), countable, RPrāt. xiv, 28

[p. 348,1] : accessible to men (a woman), fit for cohabitation, Yājñ. ii, 290; MBh. i; BhP. i, &c.

[p. 348,1] : (a man) with whom a woman may have intercourse, v

[p. 348,1] : libidinous, dissolute, Daś. vii, 32

[p. 348,1] : ‘easily brought under the influence of (a drug)’, curable by (gen.), Bhartṛ. i, 88

[p. 348,1] : approaching, impending, Gaṇit.; Gol.

[p. 348,1] : to be perceived or understood, intelligible, perceptible, Mn. xii, 122; Megh. &c.

[p. 348,1] : intended, meant, L.
