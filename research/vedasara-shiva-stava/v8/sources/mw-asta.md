[p. 122,1] 1. asta : mfn. (perf. Pass. p. √ 2. as), thrown, cast, Ragh. xii, 91

[p. 122,1] : (a/n- neg.), ŚBr. iii

[p. 122,1] : (only in comp.) thrown off, left off, set aside, given up (as grief, anger, a vow, &c.), VP.; Kathās. &c.

[p. 122,1] 2. a/sta : n. home, RV.; AV.; ŚBr.

[p. 122,1] a/sta : m. setting (as of the sun or of luminaries), VarBṛS.; Sūryas.

[p. 122,1] : ‘end, death’, see asta-samaya below

[p. 122,1] : the western mountain (behind which the sun is supposed to set), MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 122,1] : (in astron.) the seventh lunar mansion, VarBṛ.

[p. 122,1] a/sta : m. astam-√ i, astaṃ-√ gam (also Caus. See astaṃ-gamita below), or √ prāp [Kathās.], to go to one's eternal home, cease, vanish, perish, die, ŚBr. xiv; MBh. &c.

[p. 122,1] : astaṃ-√ nī [-nayati], to lead to setting, cause to set, MBh. iii, 17330
