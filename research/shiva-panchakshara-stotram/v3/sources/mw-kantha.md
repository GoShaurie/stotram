[p. 245,3] kaṇṭha/ : as, m. (√ kaṇ, Uṇ. i, 105), the throat, the neck (cf. ā-kaṇṭha-tṛpta; kaṇṭhe √ grah, to embrace, Kathās.)

[p. 245,3] : the voice (cf. sanna-kaṇṭha), MBh.; BhP. &c.

[p. 245,3] : sound, especially guttural sound, W.

[p. 245,3] : the neck (of a pitcher or jar), the narrowest part (e.g. of the womb; of a hole in which sacrificial fire is deposited; of a stalk &c.), Suśr.; Hcat.; Kathās. &c.

[p. 245,3] : immediate proximity, Pañcat.

[p. 245,3] : Vangueria Spinosa, L.

[p. 245,3] : N. of a Maharṣi, R.
