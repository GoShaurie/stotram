[p. 1324,2] : accord. to some in RV. also, ‘gain, prize, booty’; ‘contest, war’

[p. 274,2] : the term used in designating a letter or sound or indeclinable word (e.g. a-k˚, ka-k˚, qq.vv.; eva-k˚, the word eva; phūt-k˚, q.v.), Prāt.; Mn. &c.

[p. 274,2] : effort, exertion, L.
