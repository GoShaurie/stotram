[p. 1011,2] vṛnda/ : n. (fr. √ 1. vṛ ?) a heap, multitude, host, flock, swarm, number, quantity, aggregation (vṛndaṃ vṛndam, vṛndais, or vṛndavṛndais, in separate groups, in flocks or crowds), Naigh.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1011,2] : a bunch, cluster (of flowers or berries &c.), BhP.

[p. 1011,2] : a chorus of singers and musicians, Saṃgīt.

[p. 1011,2] : a partic. high number (100,000 millions), L.

[p. 1011,2] vṛnda/ : m. a tumour in the throat, Suśr.

[p. 1011,2] : a partic. high number (1,000 millions), Āryabh.

[p. 1011,2] : (with Jainas) a partic. Śakti, L. (prob. vṛndā)
