[p. 916,2] va/dana : n. (ifc. f(ā). ) the act of speaking, talking, sounding, ŚBr.; ŚrS.

[p. 916,2] : the mouth, face, countenance, MBh.; Kāv. &c. (˚naṃ √ kṛ, to make a face or grimace, ˚nī-√ bhū, to become a face)

[p. 916,2] : the front, point, R.; Suśr.

[p. 916,2] : (in alg.) the first term, initial quantity or term of a progression, Col.

[p. 916,2] : (in geom.) the side opposite to the base, the summit or apex of a triangle, Āryabh.
