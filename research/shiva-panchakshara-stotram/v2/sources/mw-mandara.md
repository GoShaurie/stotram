[p. 788,2] mandāra : m. (in some meanings also written mandara) the coral tree, Erythrina Indica (also regarded as one of the 5 trees of paradise or Svarga), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 788,2] : a white variety of Calotropis Gigantea, L.

[p. 788,2] : the thorn-apple, L.

[p. 788,2] : heaven, L.

[p. 788,2] : N. of a son of Hiraṇya-kaśipu, MBh. (C. mandara)

[p. 788,2] : of a Vidyā-dhara, MārkP.

[p. 788,2] : of a hermitage and desert spot on the right bank of the Ganges where there are said to be 11 sacred pools, Cat.

[p. 788,2] : of a mountain (v.l. mandara), R.

[p. 788,2] mandāra : n. = -puṣpa, Kālid.
