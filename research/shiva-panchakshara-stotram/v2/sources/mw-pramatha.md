[p. 685,1] pra-˚matha : m. ‘Tormentor’, N. of a class of demons attending on Śiva, MBh.; Kāv. &c. (cf. RTL. 238)

[p. 685,1] : of a son of Dhṛtarāṣṭra, MBh.

[p. 685,1] : a horse, L.
