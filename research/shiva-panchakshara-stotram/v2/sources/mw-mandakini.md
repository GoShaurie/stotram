[p. 788,2] mandākinī : f. (fr. manda + 2. añc) ‘going or streaming slowly’, N. of an arm of the Ganges (flowing down through the valley of Kedāra-nātha in the Himālayas) and of other rivers, MBh.; Pur.

[p. 788,2] : (esp.) the heavenly Ganges, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 788,2] : another river in heaven, BhP.

[p. 788,2] : N. of a metre, Chandom.

[p. 788,2] : (in astron.) N. of a partic. conjunction.
