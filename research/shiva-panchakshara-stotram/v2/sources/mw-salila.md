[p. 1189,3] salila/ : mf(ā)n. (cf. sarira/) flowing, surging, fluctuating, unsteady, RV.; AV.; MaitrS.

[p. 1189,3] salila : (am), n. (ifc. f(ā). ) flood, surge, waves, RV.; AV.; Br.

[p. 1189,3] : (also pl.) water (acc. with √ kṛ, ‘to offer a libation of water to [gen.]’), Kauś.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1189,3] : rainwater, rain, VarBṛS.

[p. 1189,3] : eye-water, tears, Megh.

[p. 1189,3] : a kind of wind (see -vāta)

[p. 1189,3] : a partic. high number, ŚāṅkhŚr.

[p. 1189,3] : a kind of metre, Nidānas.
