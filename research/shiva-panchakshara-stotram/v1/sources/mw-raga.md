[p. 872,1] rāga : m. (fr. √ rañj; ifc. ā, or ī) the act of colouring or dyeing (cf. mūrdhaja-r˚)

[p. 872,1] : colour, hue, tint, dye, (esp.) red colour, redness, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 872,1] : inflammation, Car.

[p. 872,1] : any feeling or passion, (esp.) love, affection or sympathy for, vehement desire of, interest or joy or delight in (loc. or comp.), Up.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 872,1] : loveliness, beauty (esp. of voice or song), Śak.; Pañcat.

[p. 872,1] : a musical note, harmony, melody (in the later system a partic. musical mode or order of sound or formula; Bharata enumerates 6, viz. bhairava, kauśika, hindola, dīpaka, śrī-rāga, and megha, each mode exciting some affection; other writers give other names; sometimes 7 or 26 Rāgas are mentioned; they are personified, and each of the 6 chief Rāgas is wedded to 5 or 6 consorts called Rāgiṇīs; their union gives rise to many other musical modes), Bhar.; Saṃgīt.; Rājat.; Pañcat. &c.

[p. 872,1] : nasalization, RPrāt.

[p. 872,1] : a partic. process in the preparation of quicksilver, Sarvad.

[p. 872,1] : seasoning, condiment, Car.

[p. 872,1] : a prince, king, L.

[p. 872,1] : the sun, L.

[p. 872,1] : the moon, L.
