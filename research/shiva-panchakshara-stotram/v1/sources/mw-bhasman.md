[p. 750,3] bha/sman : mfn. chewing, devouring, consuming, pulverizing, RV. v, 19, 5; x, 115, 2

[p. 750,3] bha/sman : n. (also pl.) ‘what is pulverized a or calcined by fire’, ashes, AV. &c. &c. (yuṣmābhir bhasma bhakṣayitavyam, ‘you shall have ashes to eat’ i.e. ‘you shall get nothing’, Hit.; ˚mani-huta. mfn. ‘sacrificed in a˚’ i.e. ‘useless’, Pāṇ. ii, 1, 47, Sch.)

[p. 750,3] bha/sman : n. sacred ashes (smeared on the body; cf. bhasma-dhāraṇa).
