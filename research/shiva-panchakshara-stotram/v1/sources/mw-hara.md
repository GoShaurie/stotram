[p. 1289,2] : levying, raising (e.g. kara-h˚, ‘raising taxes’), BhP.

[p. 1289,2] : ravishing, charming, delightful (or ‘relating to Hari i.e. Viṣṇu’), ib.

[p. 1289,2] : relating to Hara or Śiva, Nalac.

[p. 1289,2] hāra : m. taking away, removal, ib.

[p. 1289,2] : confiscation, forfeiture (of land, money &c.), Mn.; MBh.; Kathās.

[p. 1289,2] : waste, loss (see kāla-h˚)

[p. 1289,2] : war, battle, W.

[p. 1289,2] : a carrier, porter, ib.

[p. 1289,2] : (in arithm.) a divisor or division, Col.

[p. 1289,2] : (in prosody) a long syllable, ib.

[p. 1289,2] : a garland of pearls, necklace (accord. to some, one of 108 or 64 strings), MBh.; Kāv. &c.
