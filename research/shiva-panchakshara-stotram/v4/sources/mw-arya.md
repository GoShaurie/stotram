[p. 152,2] ā/rya : m. (fr. arya/, √ ṛ), a respectable or honourable or faithful man, an inhabitant of Āryāvarta

[p. 152,2] : one who is faithful to the religion of his country

[p. 152,2] : N. of the race which immigrated from Central Asia into Āryāvarta (opposed to an-ārya, dasyu, dāsa)

[p. 152,2] : in later times N. of the first three castes (opposed to śūdra), RV.; AV.; VS.; MBh.; Yājñ.; Pañcat. &c.

[p. 152,2] : a man highly esteemed, a respectable, honourable man, Pañcat.; Śak. &c.

[p. 152,2] : a master, an owner, L.

[p. 152,2] : a friend, L.

[p. 152,2] : a Vaiśya, L.

[p. 152,2] : Buddha

[p. 152,2] : (with Buddhists [Pāli ayyo, or ariyo]) a man who has thought on the four chief truths of Buddhism (see next col.) and lives accordingly, a Buddhist priest

[p. 152,2] : a son of Manu Sāvarṇa, Hariv.

[p. 152,2] ā/rya : mf(ā and ā/rī)n. Āryan, favourable to the Āryan people, RV. &c.

[p. 152,2] : behaving like an Āryan, worthy of one, honourable, respectable, noble, R.; Mn.; Śak. &c.

[p. 152,2] : of a good family

[p. 152,2] : excellent

[p. 152,2] : wise

[p. 152,2] : suitable

[p. 152,2] ārya : [cf. Old Germ. êra; Mod. Germ. Ehre; Irish Erin.]
