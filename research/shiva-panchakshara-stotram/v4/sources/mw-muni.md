[p. 823,1] mu/ni : m. (accord. to Uṇ. iv, 122 fr. √ man) impulse, eagerness (?), RV. vii, 56, 8

[p. 823,1] : (prob.) any one who is moved by inward impulse, an inspired or ecstatic person, enthusiast, RV.; AV.; Br.

[p. 823,1] : a saint, sage, seer, ascetic, monk, devotee, hermit (esp. one who has taken the vow of silence), ŚBr. &c. &c. (with hṛdayeṣu sthitaḥ, the internal monitor or conscience, Mn. viii, 91)

[p. 823,1] : a Brāhman of the highest (eighth) order, Hcat.

[p. 823,1] : N. of a son of Kuru, MBh.

[p. 823,1] : of a son of Dyuti-mat, MārkP.

[p. 823,1] : of Vyāsa, Kir.

[p. 823,1] : of Bharata, Sāh.

[p. 823,1] : of Agastya, L.

[p. 823,1] : of a Buddha or Arhat, Lalit.

[p. 823,1] : of Pāṇini &c. (cf. -traya)

[p. 823,1] : of other men, VP.

[p. 823,1] : of various authors, Cat.

[p. 823,1] : of various plants (Agati Grandiflora, Buchanania Latifolia, Butea Frondosa, Terminalia Catappa, the mango-tree and Artemisia Indica), L.

[p. 823,1] : pl. ‘the celestial Munis’, N. of the seven stars of Ursa Major (and there fore a symbolical N. for the number ‘seven’), Var.; Sūryas.; Śrutab.

[p. 823,1] mu/ni : f. a female Muni (also ī), Uṇ. iv, 122, Sch.

[p. 823,1] : N. of a daughter of Dakṣa (and wife of Kaśyapa), mother of a class of Gandharvas and Apsaras (cf. mauneya), MBh.; Hariv.; Pur.

[p. 823,1] mu/ni : n. N. of a Varṣa (called after a royal Muni), VP.
