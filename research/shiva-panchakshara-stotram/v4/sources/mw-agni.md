[p. 5,1] agni/ : m. (√ ag, Uṇ.) fire, sacrificial fire (of three kinds, Gārhapatya, Āhavanīya, and Dakṣiṇa)

[p. 5,1] : the number three, Sūryas.

[p. 5,1] : the god of fire, the fire of the stomach, digestive faculty, gastric fluid

[p. 5,1] : bile, L.

[p. 5,1] : gold, L.

[p. 5,1] : N. of various plants Semecarpus Anacardium, Suśr., Plumbago Zeylanica and Rosea, Citrus Acida

[p. 5,1] : mystical substitute for the letter r

[p. 5,1] : in the Kātantra grammar N. of noun-stems ending in i and u

[p. 1309,1] : (also) = next [agni-kṣetra the place for the fire-altar], ĀpŚr.

[p. 5,1] agni : [cf. Lat. ignì-s; Lith. ugni-s; Slav. ognj].
