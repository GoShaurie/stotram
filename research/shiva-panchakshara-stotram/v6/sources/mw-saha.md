[p. 1193,1] 1. saha/ : mf(ā)n. powerful, mighty, RV.

[p. 1193,1] : (ifc.) overcoming, vanquishing, MBh.

[p. 1193,1] : bearing, enduring, withstanding, defying, equal to, a match for (gen. or comp.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1193,1] : causing, effecting, stimulating, exerting, Śiś.

[p. 1193,1] : able to, capable of (inf. or comp.), Kālid.; Śiś.; Kathās.

[p. 1193,1] saha/ : m. the month Mārgaśīrṣa (see sahas), VS.; ŚBr.; Car.

[p. 1193,1] : a partic. Agni, MBh.

[p. 1193,1] : a species of plant, AV.

[p. 1193,1] : N. of a son of Manu, Hariv.

[p. 1193,1] : of a son of Prāṇa and Ūrjasvatī, BhP.

[p. 1193,1] : of a son of Dhṛtarāṣṭra, MBh.

[p. 1193,1] : of a son of Kṛṣṇa and Mādrī, BhP.

[p. 1193,1] saha/ : n. = bala, L.

[p. 1193,1] : kind of salt, L.

[p. 1193,2] 2. saha/ : ind. (prob. fr. 7. sa + dhā, which in Veda may become dha; cf. 1. sadha) together with, along with, with (with √ grah and ā-√ dā, ‘to take with one’; with √ dā, ‘to give to take away with one’; with kṛtvā and acc., ‘taking with one’, ‘in the company of’; often as a prepos. governing instr. case, but generally placed after the governed word, e.g. tena saha, ‘along with him’ ; exceptionally with abl., e.g. aiśvaryāt saha, ‘with sovereignty’, Cāṇ. 104)

[p. 1193,3] : in common, in company, jointly, conjointly, in concert (often used as a prefix in comp., expressing ‘community of action’, e.g. sahādhyayana, q.v.; or forming adjectives expressing ‘the companion of an action’, e.g. saha-cara, q.v.)

[p. 1193,3] : at the same time or simultaneously with (prefixed to adverbs of time, e.g. saha-pūrvāhṇam, q.v.; rarely ifc., e.g. vainateya-s˚, ‘with Vainateya’, Hariv.), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 1193,3] saha/ : m. a companion, L.
