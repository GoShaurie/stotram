[p. 107,1] avāp : (√ āp), -āpnoti (Imper. 2. sg. āpnuhi) to reach, attain, obtain, gain, get, Up.; Mn.; MBh. &c.;
to get by division (as a quotient), Sūryas.;
to suffer (e.g. blame or unpleasantness or pain), Mn.; Ragh. xviii, 34; Pañcat. :
Caus. to cause to obtain anything (acc.), Naiṣ. viii, 89.
