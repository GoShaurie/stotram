[p. 632,1] pu/ṇya : mf(ā)n. (perhaps fr. √ 2. puṣ, according to Uṇ. v, 15 from √ pū; see also √ puṇ) auspicious, propitious, fair, pleasant, good, right, virtuous, meritorious, pure, holy, sacred, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 632,1] pu/ṇya : m. N. of a poet, Cat.

[p. 632,1] : of another man, Buddh.

[p. 632,1] pu/ṇya : m. or n. N. of a lake, MBh.

[p. 632,1] pu/ṇya : n. (ifc. f(ā). ) the good or right, virtue, purity, good work, meritorious act, moral or religious merit, MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 632,1] pu/ṇya : n. a religious ceremony (esp. one performed by a wife in order to retain her husband's affections and to obtain a son; also -ka), MBh.; Hariv.

[p. 632,1] : a brick trough for watering cattle, W.
