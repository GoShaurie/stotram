[p. 409,1] jaṭā : f. the hair twisted together (as worn by ascetics, by Śiva, and persons in mourning), PārGṛ. ii, 6; Mn. vi, 6; MBh. (ifc. f(ā). , iii, 16137) &c.

[p. 409,1] : a fibrous root, root (in general), Bhpr. v, 111; ŚārṅgS. i, 46 and 58

[p. 409,1] : N. of several plants (= ṭā-vatī, L.; Mucuna pruritus, L.; Flacourtia cataphracta, L.; = ˚ṭā-mūlā, L.; = rudra-jaṭā, L.), Suśr. v f.

[p. 409,1] : N. of a Pāṭha or arrangement of the Vedic text (still more artificial than the Krama, each pair of words being repeated thrice and one repetition being in inverted order), Caraṇ.
