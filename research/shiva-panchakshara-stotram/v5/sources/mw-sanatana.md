[p. 1141,1] sanāta/na : mf(ī, m.c. also ā)n. eternal, perpetual, permanent, everlasting, primeval, ancient, ŚBr. &c. &c.

[p. 1141,1] sanāta/na : m. N. of Brahmā, L.

[p. 1141,1] : of Viṣṇu, Bhaṭṭ.

[p. 1141,1] : of Śiva, L.

[p. 1141,1] : a guest of deceased ancestors, one who must always be fed whenever he attends Śrāddhas, L.

[p. 1141,1] : N. of a Ṛṣi (in MBh. and later ‘a mind-born son of Brahmā’), TS. &c. &c.

[p. 1141,1] : of a king, Buddh.

[p. 1141,1] : (with śarman and go-svāmin) of two authors, Cat.

[p. 1141,1] sanāta/na : m. pl. N. of partic. worlds, Hariv.
