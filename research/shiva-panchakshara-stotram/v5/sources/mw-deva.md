[p. 492,2] deva/ : mf(ī)n. (fr. 3. div) heavenly, divine (also said of terrestrial things of high excellence), RV.; AV.; VS.; ŚBr. (superl. m. deva/-tama, RV. iv, 22, 3 &c.; f. devi-tamā, ii, 41, 16)

[p. 492,2] deva/ : m. (according to Pāṇ. iii, 3, 120 de/va) a deity, god, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 492,2] : (rarely applied to) evil demons, AV. iii, 15, 5; TS. iii, 5, 4, 1

[p. 492,2] : (pl. the gods as the heavenly or shining ones; vi/śve devā/s, all the gods, RV. ii, 3, 4 &c., or a partic. class of deities [see under vi/śva], often reckoned as 33, either 11 for each of the 3 worlds, RV. i, 139, 11 &c. [cf. tri-daśa], or 8 Vasus, 11 Rudras, and 12 Ādityas [to which the 2 Aśvins must be added] Br.; cf. also, Divyāv. 68; with Jainas 4 classes, viz. bhavanādhīśa, vyantara, jyotiṣka, and vaimānika; devā/nām pa/tnyas, the wives of the gods, RV.; VS.; Br. [cf. deva-patnī below])

[p. 492,3] : N. of the number 33 (see above), Gaṇit.

[p. 492,3] : N. of Indra as the god of the sky and giver of rain, MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 492,3] : a cloud, L.

[p. 492,3] : (with Jainas) the 22nd Arhat of the future Ut-sarpiṇī

[p. 492,3] : the image of a god, an idol, Viṣṇ.

[p. 492,3] : a god on earth or among men, either Brāhman, priest, RV.; AV. (cf. bhū-d˚), or king, prince (as a title of honour, esp. in the voc. ‘your majesty’ or ‘your honour’; also ifc., e.g. śrī-harṣa-d˚, vikramāṅka-d˚, king Śrī-h˚ or Vikr˚, and in names as puruṣottama-d˚ [lit. having Viṣṇu as one's deity; cf. atithi-d˚, ācārya-d˚, pitṛ-d˚, mātṛ-d˚]; rarely preceding the name, e.g. deva-caṇḍamahāsena, Kathās. xiii, 48), Kāv.; Pañc. &c. (cf. kṣiti-, nara-, &c.)

[p. 492,3] : a husband's brother (cf. devṛ and devara), W.

[p. 492,3] : a fool, dolt, L.

[p. 492,3] : a child, L.

[p. 492,3] : a man following any partic. line or business, L.

[p. 492,3] : a spearman, lancer, L.

[p. 492,3] : emulation, wish to excel or overcome, L.

[p. 492,3] : sport, play, L.

[p. 492,3] : a sword, Gal.

[p. 492,3] : N. of men, VP.

[p. 492,3] : of a disciple of Nāgārjuna, MWB. 192

[p. 492,3] : dimin. for devadatta, Pāṇ. v, 3, 83, Vārtt. 4, Sch.

[p. 492,3] deva/ : n. (L.) an organ of sense, MuṇḍUp. iii, 1, 8; 2, 7

[p. 492,3] deva : [cf. Lat. dīvus, deus; Lit. dë́vas; Old Pruss. deiwas.]
