[p. 1294,3] ha/sta : m. (ifc. f(ā). , of unknown derivation) the hand (ifc. = ‘holding in or by the hand’; haste √ kṛ [as two words], ‘to take into the hand’, ‘get possession of’; haste-√ kṛ [as a comp.], ‘to take by the hand, marry’; śatru-hastaṃ √ gam, ‘to fall into the hand of the enemy’), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 1294,3] : an elephant's trunk (ifc. = ‘holding with the trunk’), AitBr.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1294,3] : the forearm (a measure of length from the elbow to the tip of the middle finger, = 24 Aṅgulas or about 18 inches), VarBṛS.; Rājat. &c.

[p. 1294,3] : the position of the hand (= hasta-vinyāsa), VPrāt.

[p. 1294,3] : hand-writing, Yājñ.; Vikr.

[p. 1294,3] : the 11th (13th) lunar asterism (represented by a hand and containing five stars, identified by some with part of the constellation Corvus), AV. &c. &c.

[p. 1294,3] : a species of tree, L.

[p. 1294,3] : (in prosody) an anapest, Col.

[p. 1294,3] : quantity, abundance, mass (ifc. after words signifying ‘hair’; cf. keśa-h˚)

[p. 1294,3] : N. of a guardian of the Soma, Sāy.

[p. 1294,3] : of a son of Vasudeva, BhP.

[p. 1294,3] : of another man, Rājat.

[p. 1294,3] ha/sta : (am), n. a pair of leather bellows, L.

[p. 1294,3] ha/sta : mfn. born under the Nakṣatra Hasta, Pāṇ. iv, 3, 34 [cf., accord. to some Gk. ἀγοστός]
