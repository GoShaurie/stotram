[p. 627,2] pi/nāka : m. n. a staff or bow, (esp.) the staff or bow of Rudra-Śiva, AV.; VS.; TS.; MBh. &c.

[p. 627,2] : Śiva's trident or three-pronged spear (= śūla and tri-śūla), L.

[p. 627,2] : falling dust, L.

[p. 627,2] pi/nāka : n. a species of talc, Bhpr. (Perhaps fr. pi-api-√ nam; cf. nāka.)
