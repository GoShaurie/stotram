[p. 623,3] pā/śa : m. (once n. ifc. f(ā). ; fr. √ 3. paś) a snare, trap, noose, tie, bond, cord, chain, fetter (lit. and fig.), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 623,3] : (esp.) the noose as attribute of Śiva or Yama, RTL. 81; 290

[p. 623,3] : (with Jainas) anything that binds or fetters the soul i.e. the outer world, nature, Sarvad. (cf. also, RTL. 89)

[p. 623,3] : selvage, edge, border (of anything woven), ŚrGṛS.

[p. 623,3] : a die, dice, MBh.

[p. 623,3] : (in astrol.) a partic. constellation

[p. 1330,2] : a partic. land-measure, Inscr.

[p. 623,3] : (ifc. it expresses either contempt, e.g. chattra-p˚, ‘a shabby umbrella’, or admiration, e.g. karṇa-p˚, ‘a beautiful ear’; after a word signifying, ‘hair’ abundance, quantity, e.g. keśa-p˚, ‘a mass of hair’)
