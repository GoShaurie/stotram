[p. 510,3] 1. dha/rma : m. (rarely n. g. ardharcādi; the older form of the RV. is dha/rman, q.v.) that which is established or firm, steadfast decree, statute, ordinance, law

[p. 510,3] : usage, practice, customary observance or prescribed conduct, duty

[p. 510,3] : right, justice (often as a synonym of punishment)

[p. 510,3] : virtue, morality, religion, religious merit, good works (dha/rmeṇa ind. or ˚māt ind. according to right or rule, rightly, justly, according to the nature of anything; cf. below; ˚mesthita mfn. holding to the law, doing one's duty), AV. &c. &c.

[p. 510,3] : Law or Justice personified (as Indra, ŚBr. &c.; as Yama, MBh.; as born from the right breast of Yama and father of Śama, Kāma and Harṣa, ib.; as Viṣṇu, Hariv.; as Prajā-pati and son-in-law of Dakṣa, Hariv.; Mn. &c.; as one of the attendants of the Sun, L.; as a Bull, Mn. viii, 16; as a Dove, Kathās. vii, 89, &c.)

[p. 510,3] : the law or doctrine of Buddhism (as distinguished from the saṅgha or monastic order, MWB. 70)

[p. 510,3] : the ethical precepts of Buddhism (or the principal dharma called sūtra, as distinguished from the abhi-dharma or ‘further dharma’ and from the vinaya or ‘discipline’, these three constituting the canon of Southern B˚, MWB. 61)

[p. 510,3] : the law of Northern B˚ (in 9 canonical scriptures, viz. Prajñā-pāramitā, Gaṇḍa-vyūha, Daśa-bhūmīśvara, Samādhirāja, Laṅkāvatāra, Saddharma-puṇḍarīka, Tathāgata-guhyaka, Lalita-vistara, Suvarṇa-prabhāsa,ib. 69)

[p. 510,3] : nature, character, peculiar condition or essential quality, property, mark, peculiarity (= sva-bhāva, L.; cf. daśa-dh˚arma-gata, ŚBr. &c. &c.; upamānopameyayor dh˚, the tertium comparationis, Pāṇ. ii, 1, 55, Sch.)

[p. 510,3] : a partic. ceremony, MBh. xiv, 2623

[p. 510,3] : sacrifice, L.

[p. 510,3] : the ninth mansion, Var.

[p. 510,3] : an Upaniṣad, L.

[p. 510,3] : associating with the virtuous, L.

[p. 510,3] : religious abstraction, devotion, L.

[p. 510,3] : = upamā, L. (cf. above)

[p. 510,3] : a bow, Dharmaś.

[p. 1329,2] : a thing, Sukh. i

[p. 510,3] : a Soma-drinker, L.

[p. 510,3] : N. of the 15th Arhat of the present Ava-sarpiṇī, L.

[p. 510,3] : of a son of Anu and father of Ghṛta, Hariv.

[p. 510,3] : of a s˚ of Gāndhāra and f˚ of Dhṛta, Pur.

[p. 510,3] : of a s˚ of Haihaya and f˚ of Netra, BhP.

[p. 510,3] : of a s˚ of Pṛthu-śravas and of Uśanas, ib.

[p. 510,3] : of a s˚ of Su-vrata, VP. (cf. dharma-sūtra)

[p. 510,3] : of a s˚ of Dīrgha-tapas, VāyuP.

[p. 510,3] : of a king of Kaśmīra, Rāj. iv, 678

[p. 510,3] : of another man, ib. vii, 85

[p. 510,3] : of a lexicographer &c. (also -paṇḍita, -bhaṭṭa and -śāstrin), Cat.
