[p. 449,3] 1. tu : cl. 2. (tauti, Dhātup.; fut. 2nd totā or tavitā, Vop.) to have authority, be strong, RV. i, 94, 2 (pf. tūtāva cf. Naigh. iv, 1; Pāṇ. vi, 1, 7, Kāś.);
to go, Dhātup.;
to injure, ib. :
Caus. (aor. tūtot, 2. sg. ˚tos) to make strong or efficient, RV. ii, 20, 5; vi, 26, 4;
 cf. ut-, saṃ-;
 tava/s, &c., tīvra/;

[p. 449,3] tu : [Zend tav, ‘to be able’; Lat. tumour, tueri, totus.]

[p. 449,3] 2. tu/ : ind. (never found at the beginning of a sentence or verse; metrically also tū/, RV.; cf. Pāṇ. vi, 3, 133) pray! I beg, do, now, then, Lat.dum used (esp. with the Imper.), RV.

[p. 449,3] : but (also with eva/ or vai/ following), AV. iv, 18, 6; TS.; ŚBr. &c.
