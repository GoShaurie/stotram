[p. 812,2] mārga : m. (in most meanings fr. mṛga, of which it is also the Vṛddhi form in comp.) seeking, search, tracing out, hunting, L.

[p. 812,2] : (exceptionally also n.; ifc. f(ā). ) the track of a wild animal, any track, road, path, way to (loc. or comp.) or through (comp.), course (also of the wind and the stars), Mn.; MBh. &c. (mārgaṃ-√ dā or yam, with gen. of pers., to give up the way to, allow to pass; māgeṇa ifc. = by way of i.e. through, across or along; with √ yā, to go the way of i.e. suffer the same fate as; mārgais ifc., through; mārgāya, with gen., in order to make way for any one; mārge, by the wayside or on the way; with pra-√ cal, to set out on one's way; nijamārgaṃ-√ gam, to go one's way)

[p. 812,2] : a walk, journey, VarBṛS.

[p. 812,2] : reach, range, Kir.

[p. 812,2] : a scar, mark (left by a wound &c.), Ragh.

[p. 812,2] : (in medic.) a way, passage, channel (in any part of the body, esp. the intestinal canal, anus)

[p. 812,2] : a way, expedient, means, Kām.; Kathās. (mārgeṇa, by means of VarBṛS.)

[p. 812,2] : a way, manner method, custom, usage, Up.; Yājñ.; MBh. &c.

[p. 812,2] : the right way, proper course, MBh.; Hariv. (cf. āmārga)

[p. 812,2] : (with Buddhists) the way or path pointed out by Buddha for escape from the misery of existence (one of the 4 noble truths), MWB. 44 (cf. āryāṣṭāṅga-m˚)

[p. 812,2] : a title or head in law, ground for litigation, Mn. viii, 3, 9 &c.

[p. 812,2] : a way of speaking or writing, diction, style, Kāvyād.; Sāh.

[p. 812,2] : a high (opp. to ‘vulgar’) style of acting or dancing or singing, Inscr.; Daśar.

[p. 812,2] : (in dram.) pointing out the way, indicating how anything is to take place, Daśar.; Sāh.

[p. 812,2] : (in astrol.) the 7th mansion, VarYogay.

[p. 812,2] : (in geom.) a section, W.

[p. 812,2] : musk, L. (cf. mṛga-mada)

[p. 812,2] : the month Mārgaśīrṣa (November-December), Rājat.

[p. 812,2] : the constellation Mṛga-śiras, L.

[p. 812,2] : N. of Viṣṇu (as ‘the way’, scil. to final emancipation), MBh.

[p. 812,2] mārga : mf(ī)n. belonging to or coming from game or deer, R.; Var.; Suśr.
