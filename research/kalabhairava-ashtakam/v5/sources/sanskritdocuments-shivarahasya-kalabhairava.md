धर्मसेतुपालनमधर्ममार्गनाशनं
कर्मपाशमोचनं सुकर्मदायकं प्रभुम् ।
सुवर्णवर्णकेशपाशशोभिताङ्गनिर्मलं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे । ६ ॥ ५८॥

Notes: separate recension (Īśvara speaking to Pārvatī). Its stanza 6 corresponds to our verse 5. Order differs from the Śaṅkara text: its stanzas run 1, 4, 2, 3, 6, 5, 7, 8 in our numbering. The source itself says: “This text is slightly different from the more popularly known Aśṭakam that is attributed to Shankaracharya.”
