धर्मसेतुपालकं त्वधर्ममार्गनाशकं
कर्मपाशमोचकं सुशर्मदायकं विभुम् ।
स्वर्णवर्णशेषपाशशोभिताङ्गमण्डलं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे ॥५॥

Transliteration:
dharma setu pālakaṃ tvadharma mārga nāśakaṃ
karma pāśa mocakaṃ su śarma dāyakaṃ vibhum .
svarṇa varṇa śeṣa pāśa śobhitāṅga maṇḍalaṃ
kāśikā purādhinātha kālabhairavaṃ bhaje ..5..

Simplified transliteration:
dharma setu paalakam tvadharma maarga naashakam
karma paasha mochakam su sharma daayakam vibhum .
svarna varna shesha paasha shobhita anga mandalam
kaashikaa pura adhinaatha kaala bhairavam bhaje ..5..

Translation:
The protector of the dam of dharma, the destroyer of the path of unrighteousness, the one who releases the bonds of karma, the giver of true happiness, the all-pervading Lord — whose bodily frame is adorned with a serpent-noose golden in hue: I worship that Kālabhairava, the sovereign Lord of the city of Kāśī.

Word meanings:
धर्मसेतुपालकं (dharmasetupālakaṃ) – maintainer of righteousness (dharma:righteousness | setu:bridge | pālakaṃ:maintainer); त्वधर्ममार्गनाशकं (tvadharmamārganāśakaṃ) – destroyer of unrighteous paths (tvadharma:unrighteousness | mārga:path | nāśakaṃ:destroyer); कर्मपाशमोचकं (karmapāśamocakaṃ) – liberator from ties of karma (karma:deeds | pāśa:ties | mocakaṃ:liberator); सुशर्मदायकं (suśarmadāyakaṃ) – bestower of happiness (suśarma:happiness | dāyakaṃ:bestower); विभुम् (vibhum) – all-pervading lord; स्वर्णवर्णशेषपाशशोभिताङ्गमण्डलं (svarṇavarṇaśeṣapāśaśobhitāṅgamaṇḍalaṃ) – with golden cord decorated body (svarṇa:golden | varṇa:color | śeṣa:cord | pāśa:cord | śobhita:decorated | aṅga:body | maṇḍalaṃ:circle); काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं (kāśikāpurādhināthakālabhairavaṃ) – Kalabhairava, ruler of Kashi (kāśikā:Kashi | pura:city | adhinātha:ruler | kālabhairavaṃ:Kalabhairava); भजे (bhaje) – I sing praise;
