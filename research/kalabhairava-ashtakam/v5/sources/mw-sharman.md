[p. 1058,2] śa/rman : n. (prob. fr. √ śri and connected with 1. śaraṇa, śarīra) shelter, protection, refuge, safety, RV. &c. &c.;

[p. 1058,2] : a house, Naigh. iii, 4

[p. 1058,2] : Joy, bliss, comfort, delight, happiness (often at the end of names of Brāhmans, just as varman is added to the names of Kṣatriyas, and gupta to those of Vaiśyas), Yājñ.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1058,2] : N. of partic. formulas, VarYogay.

[p. 1058,3] : identified with śarva (Kauś.) and with vāc (AitBr.)

[p. 1058,3] śa/rman : mfn. happy, prosperous, W.
