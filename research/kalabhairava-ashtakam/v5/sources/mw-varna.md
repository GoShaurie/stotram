[p. 924,2] va/rṇa : m. (or n. g. ardharcādi, prob. fr. √ 1. vṛ; ifc. f(ā). ) a covering, cloak, mantle, L.

[p. 924,2] : a cover, lid, Yājñ. iii, 99

[p. 924,2] : outward appearance, exterior, form, figure, shape, colour, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 924,2] : colour of the face, (esp.) good colour or complexion, lustre, beauty, Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 924,2] : colour, tint, dye, pigment (for painting or writing), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 924,2] : colour = race, species, kind, sort, character, nature, quality, property (applied to persons and things), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 924,2] : class of men, tribe, order, caste (prob. from contrast of colour between the dark aboriginal tribes and their fair conquerors; in RV. esp. applied to the Āryas and the Dāsas; but more properly applicable to the four principal classes described in Manu's code, viz. Brāhmans, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, and Śūdras; the more modern word for ‘caste’ being jāti; cf. IW. 210 n. 1), ib.

[p. 924,2] : a letter, sound, vowel, syllable, word, Br.; Prāt. &c.

[p. 924,2] : a musical sound or note (also applied to the voice of animals), MBh.; R.; Pañcat.

[p. 924,2] : the order or arrangement of a song or poem, W.

[p. 924,2] : praise, commendation, renown, glory, Mṛcch.; Kum.; Rājat.

[p. 924,2] : (in alg.) an unknown magnitude or quantity

[p. 924,2] : (in arithm.) the figure, ‘one’

[p. 924,2] : (accord. to some) a co-efficient

[p. 924,2] : a kind of measure, L. (cf. -tāla)

[p. 924,2] : gold, L.

[p. 924,2] : a religious observance, L.

[p. 924,2] : one who wards off, expeller, Sāy. on RV. i, 104, 2

[p. 924,2] va/rṇa : n. saffron, L.
