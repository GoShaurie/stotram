[p. 7,2] 1. aṅga/ : ind. a particle implying attention, assent or desire, and sometimes impatience; it may be rendered by well;

[p. 7,2] : indeed, true; please; rather; quick

[p. 7,2] : kim aṅga, how much rather!

[p. 7,3] 2. a/ṅga : n. (√ am, Uṇ.), a limb of the body

[p. 7,3] : a limb, member

[p. 7,3] : the body

[p. 7,3] : a subordinate division or department, especially of a science, as the six Vedāṅgas

[p. 7,3] : hence the number six

[p. 7,3] : N. of the chief sacred texts of the Jainas

[p. 7,3] : a limb or subdivision of Mantra or counsel (said to be five, viz. 1. karmaṇām ārambhopāyaḥ, means of commencing operations; 2. puruṣa-dravya-sampad, providing men and materials; 3. deśa-kāla-vibhāga, distribution of place and time; 4. vipatti-pratīkāra, counter-action of disaster; 5. kārya-siddhi, successful accomplishment; whence mantra is said to be pañcāṅga)

[p. 7,3] : any subdivision, a supplement

[p. 7,3] : (in Gr.) the base of a word, but in the strong cases only, Pāṇ. i, 4, 13 seqq.

[p. 7,3] : anything inferior or secondary, anything immaterial or unessential, See aṅga-tā

[p. 7,3] : (in rhetoric) an illustration

[p. 7,3] : (in the drama) the whole of the subordinate characters

[p. 7,3] : an expedient

[p. 7,3] : a mental organ, the mind, L.

[p. 7,3] a/ṅga : m. sg. or pl. N. of Bengal proper or its inhabitants

[p. 7,3] a/ṅga : m. (sg.), N. of a king of Aṅga

[p. 7,3] a/ṅga : mfn. having members or divisions, L.

[p. 7,3] a/ṅga : m. contiguous, L.
