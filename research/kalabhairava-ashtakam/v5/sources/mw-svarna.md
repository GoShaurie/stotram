[p. 1282,1] svarṇa : m. (contracted from su-varṇa) a partic. Agni, Hariv.

[p. 1282,1] svarṇa : n. gold (as a weight = one Karṣa of gold), Yājñ.; Kāv.; Kathās. &c.

[p. 1282,1] : a kind of red chalk, R.

[p. 1282,1] : a kind of plant (accord. to L., ‘a kind of herb’, = gaura-suvarṇa ‘the thorn-apple’, ‘a kind of cocoa palm’, and ‘the flower of Mesua Roxburghii’), BhP.
