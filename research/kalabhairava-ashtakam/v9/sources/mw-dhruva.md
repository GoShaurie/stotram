[p. 521,2] dhruva/ : mf(ā/)n. (prob. fr. √ dhṛ, but cf. √ dhru and dhruv) fixed, firm, immovable, unchangeable, constant, lasting, permanent, eternal, RV. &c. &c. (e.g. the earth, a mountain, a pillar, a vow &c.; with svāṅga n. an inseparable member of the body, Pāṇ. vi, 2, 177; with dhenu f. a cow which stands quiet when milked, AV. xii, 1, 45; with diś f. the point of the heavens directly under the feet [reckoned among the quarters of the sky, cf. 2. diś] AV.; Br.; with smṛti f. a strong or retentive memory, ChUp. vii, 26, 2; cf. also under karaṇa and nakṣatra)

[p. 521,3] : staying with (loc.), RV. ix, 101, 12

[p. 521,3] : settled, certain, sure, Mn.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 521,3] : ifc. = pāpa, L.

[p. 521,3] dhruva/ : m. the polar star (personified as son of Uttāna-pāda and grandson of Manu), GṛS.; MBh. &c.

[p. 521,3] : celestial pole, Sūryas.

[p. 521,3] : the unchangeable longitude of fixed stars, a constant arc, ib.

[p. 521,3] : a knot, VS. v, 21; 30

[p. 521,3] : a post, stake, L.

[p. 521,3] : the Indian fig-tree, L.

[p. 521,3] : tip of the nose (?), L.

[p. 521,3] : a partic. water-bird, ib.

[p. 521,3] : the remaining (i.e. preserved) Graha which having been drawn in the morning is not offered till evening, ŚBr.; Vait.

[p. 521,3] : (in music) the introductory verse of a song (recurring as a kind of burthen) or a partic. time or measure (tāla-viśeṣa)

[p. 521,3] : any epoch to which a computation of dates is referred, W.

[p. 521,3] : N. of an astrol. Yoga

[p. 521,3] : of the syllable Om, RāmatUp.

[p. 521,3] : of a serpent supporting the earth, GṛS.; TĀr.
