[p. 313,1] kopa : m. (√ kup) morbid irritation or disorder of the humors of the body, Suśr.

[p. 313,2] : fury (of fire, arms, war, &c.), VarBṛS.; VarBṛ.

[p. 313,2] : passion, wrath, anger, rage (with loc. gen., prati, or upari, or ifc.), Mn. iii, 230 & viii, 280; MBh. &c. (rarely pl. Hit.; ifc. f(ā). , Mālav.; sa-kopa, ‘enraged’, Pañcat.; sa-kopam, ‘angrily’, Pañcat.; Hit.)

[p. 313,2] : the state of being in contradiction with, incompatibleness with, Bādar. ii, 1, 26.
