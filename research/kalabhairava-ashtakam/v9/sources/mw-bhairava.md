[p. 767,1] bhairava : mf(ā and ī)n. (fr. bhīru) frightful, terrible, horrible, formidable (am ind.), MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 767,1] : relating to Bhairava, Cat.

[p. 767,1] bhairava : m. N. of a form of Śiva (cf. RTL. 85), Prab.; Rājat.; Pur. (in the latter 8 Bh˚s are enumerated, viz. mahā-, saṃhāra-, asitāṅga-, ruru-, kāla-, krodha-, tāmracūḍa-, or kapāla-, candracūḍa- or rudra-bh˚; sometimes other names are given, e.g. vidyā-rāja, kāma-r˚, nāga-r˚, svacchanda-r˚, lambita-r˚, deva-r˚, ugra-r˚, vighna-r˚)

[p. 767,1] : a man representing Bh˚, W.

[p. 767,1] : a jackal, L.

[p. 767,1] : a mountain, L.

[p. 767,1] : (in music) N. of a Rāga

[p. 767,1] : N. of a chief of Śiva's host, KālP.

[p. 767,1] : of a son of Śiva by Tārā-vatī (wife of Candra-śekhara, king of Karavīra-pura), ib.

[p. 767,1] : of a Nāga, MBh.

[p. 767,1] : of a Yakṣa, Cat.

[p. 767,1] : of a hunter, Hit.

[p. 767,1] : of 2 kings and various teachers and authors (also with tripāṭhin, daivajña, tilaka, dīkṣita, ācārya, bhaṭṭa and miśra), ib.

[p. 767,1] : of a river, L.

[p. 767,1] bhairava : m. pl. N. of a partic. sect, VP.

[p. 767,1] bhairava : n. terror or the property of exciting terror, W.

[p. 767,1] : = bhairava-tantra below.
