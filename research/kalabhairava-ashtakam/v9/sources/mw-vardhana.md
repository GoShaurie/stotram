[p. 926,1] 1. vardhana : n. the act of cutting or cutting off (see nābhi-v˚)

[p. 926,1] vardhana : mfn. ifc. after a proper N. prob. = ‘town’ (cf. puṇḍra-v˚, and Old Persian vardana).

[p. 926,1] 2. va/rdhana : mf(ī)n. increasing, growing, thriving, MBh.

[p. 926,1] : (often ifc.) causing to increase, strengthening, granting prosperity, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 926,1] : (mostly ifc.) animating, gladdening, exhilarating, MBh.; R.; BhP.

[p. 926,1] va/rdhana : m. a granter of increase, bestower of prosperity, ib.

[p. 926,1] : a tooth growing over another tooth, Suśr.

[p. 926,1] : (in music) a kind of measure, Saṃgīt.

[p. 926,1] : N. of Śiva, MBh.

[p. 926,1] : of one of Skanda's attendants, ib.

[p. 926,1] : of a son of Kṛṣṇa and Mitra-vindā, BhP.

[p. 926,1] va/rdhana : (am), n. the act of increasing, increase, growth, prosperity, success, MBh.; R.

[p. 926,1] : enlarging, magnifying, strengthening, promoting, KātyŚr.; Hit.

[p. 926,1] : a means of strengthening, restorative, comfort, RV.

[p. 926,1] : educating, rearing, Kathās.

[p. 1010,2] vardhana : &c. See p. 926, col. 1.
