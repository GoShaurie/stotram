कालभैरवाष्टकं पठंति ये मनोहरं
ज्ञानमुक्तिसाधनं विचित्रपुण्यवर्धनम् ।
शोकमोहदैन्यलोभकोपतापनाशनं
ते प्रयान्ति कालभैरवांघ्रिसन्निधिं ध्रुवम् ॥९॥

Transliteration:
kālabhairavāṣṭakaṃ paṭhanti ye manoharaṃ
jñāna mukti sādhanaṃ vicitra puṇya vardhanam .
śoka moha dainya lobha kopa tāpa nāśanaṃ
te prayānti kālabhairavāṅghri sannidhiṃ dhruvam ..9..

Simplified transliteration:
kaalabhairava ashtakam pathanti ye manoharam
jnaana mukti saadhanam vichitra punya vardhanam .
shoka moha dainya lobha kopa taapa naashanam
te prayaanti kaalabhairava anghri sannidhim dhruvam ..9..

Translation:
Those who recite this enchanting Kālabhairava Aṣṭaka — the means to knowledge and liberation, the increaser of wondrous merit, the destroyer of grief, delusion, wretchedness, greed, anger, and torment — they surely proceed to the presence of Kālabhairava's feet.

Word meanings:
कालभैरवाष्टकं (kālabhairavāṣṭakam) – eight verses on Kalabhairava (kāla:time | bhairava:Bhairava | aṣṭakam:eight verses); पठंति (paṭhanti) – recite; ये (ye) – those who; मनोहरं (manoharam) – enticing; ज्ञानमुक्तिसाधनं (jñānamuktisādhanam) – source of knowledge and liberation (jñāna:knowledge | mukti:liberation | sādhanam:means); विचित्रपुण्यवर्धनम् (vicitrapuṇyavardhanam) – increases righteousness (vicitra:varied | puṇya:righteousness | vardhanam:increasing); शोकमोहदैन्यलोभकोपतापनाशनं (śokamohadainyalobhakopatāpanāśanam) – destroys grief, attachment, etc. (śoka:grief | moha:attachment | dainya:depression | lobha:greed | kopa:anger | tāpa:heat | nāśanam:destroying); ते (te) – they (those who recite); प्रयान्ति (prayānti) – move towards; कालभैरवांघ्रिसन्निधिं (kālabhairavāṅghrisannidhim) – proximity of Kalabhairava's feet (kāla:time | bhairava:Bhairava | aṅghri:feet | sannidhim:proximity); ध्रुवम् (dhruvam) – certainly;

Colophon:
इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ श्रीकालभैरवाष्टकं सम्पूर्णम् ॥
iti śrīmat paramahaṃsa parivrājakācāryasya śrī govinda bhagavat pūjyapāda śiṣyasya śrīmacchaṅkara bhagavataḥ kṛtau śrī kālabhairavāṣṭakaṃ sampūrṇam ..
iti shriimat paramahamsa parivraajakaachaaryasya shrii govinda bhagavat puujyapaada shishyasya shriimacchankara bhagavatah kritau shrii kaalabhairavaashtakam sampuurnam ..
Thus concludes the Sri Kalabhairava Ashtakam, composed by the venerable Sri Shankara Bhagavan — the supreme paramahamsa, a wandering renunciant and teacher, and disciple of the revered Sri Govinda Bhagavatpada.
इति (iti) – thus (here it ends); श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य (śrīmatparamahaṃsaparivrājakācāryasya) – of the revered paramahamsa wandering-ascetic teacher (śrīmat:revered | paramahaṃsa:supreme renunciant | parivrājaka:wandering monk | ācāryasya:of the teacher); श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य (śrīgovindabhagavatpūjyapādaśiṣyasya) – disciple of the venerable Sri Govinda Bhagavatpada (govinda-bhagavat-pūjyapāda:the worshipful Govinda Bhagavatpada | śiṣyasya:of the disciple); श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः (śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ) – of the revered Shankara Bhagavan; कृतौ (kṛtau) – in the work / composed by; श्रीकालभैरवाष्टकं (śrīkālabhairavāṣṭakaṃ) – the Sri Kalabhairava Ashtakam; सम्पूर्णम् (sampūrṇam) – is complete;
