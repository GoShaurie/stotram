[p. 117,1] a/ṣṭaka : mf(ā or ikā)n. (Śulb.; cf. Pāṇ. vii, 3, 45 Comm.) consisting of eight parts, ŚBr.; RPrāt. &c.

[p. 117,1] : one who is acquainted with the eight books of Pāṇini's grammar, Pāṇ. iv, 2, 65, Sch.

[p. 117,1] a/ṣṭaka : m. N. of a son of Viśvāmitra (author of the hymn, RV. x, 104), AitBr.; ĀśvŚr.; MBh. &c.

[p. 117,1] a/ṣṭaka : n. a whole consisting of eight parts (as each of the eight Aṣṭakas of the RV., or as, TS. i, or as Pāṇini's grammar &c.)
