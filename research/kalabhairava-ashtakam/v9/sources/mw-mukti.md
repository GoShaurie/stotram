[p. 821,2] mukti : f. setting or becoming free, release, liberation, deliverance from (comp.), ŚBr. &c., &c.

[p. 821,2] : final liberation or emancipation, final beatitude (= mokṣa, q.v.), Kāv.; Kathās.

[p. 821,2] : abandonment, putting off, giving up (comp.), ib.

[p. 821,2] : throwing, casting, hurling, shooting, sending, Hariv.

[p. 821,2] : discharge (of a debt; cf. ṛṇa-m˚)

[p. 821,2] : N. of a divine being (the wife of Satya), Cat.
