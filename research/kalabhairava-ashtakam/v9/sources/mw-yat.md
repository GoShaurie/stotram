[p. 849,2] 1. yā/t : mfn. (nom. yān, yātī, or yāntī, yāt; for 2. yāt See p. 851, col. 1) going, moving &c. (in RV. i, 32, 15, ‘travelling’, as opp. to a/va-sita, ‘resting’).

[p. 851,1] 2. yā/t : ind. (obs. abl. of 3. ya, cf. tā/t; for 1. yāt See p. 849, col. 2) inasmuch as, so far as, as long as, since, RV.; AV. [cf. Gk. ὡς.]

[p. 851,1] 3. yāt : (√ 2. yat). See riṇa-yā/t.
