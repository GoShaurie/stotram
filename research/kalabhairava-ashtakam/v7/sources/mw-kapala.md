[p. 250,2] kapā/la : m. n. (√ kamp, Uṇ. i, 117), a cup, jar, dish (used especially for the Puroḍāśa offering), TS.; ŚBr.; Suśr. &c. (cf. trikapāla, pañca-kapāla, &c.)

[p. 250,2] : the alms-bowl of a beggar, Mn. vi, 44; viii, 93; R. &c.

[p. 250,2] : a fragment of brick (on which the oblation is placed), ŚBr. vi, xii; KātyŚr.; Suśr. &c.

[p. 250,2] : a cover, lid, ĀśvGṛ. iv, 5, 8; Bhāṣāp. &c.

[p. 250,2] : the skull, cranium, skull-bone, AV. ix, 8, 22; x, 2, 8; ŚBr. i; Yājñ. &c.

[p. 250,2] : the shell of an egg, ŚBr. vi, 1, 3; Kathās. &c.

[p. 250,2] : the shell of a tortoise, ŚBr. vii, 5, 1, 2

[p. 250,2] : the cotyla of the leg of an animal, any flat bone, AitBr.; Suśr.

[p. 250,2] : a kind of leprosy, Suśr. i, 268, 1; 13

[p. 250,2] : multitude, assemblage, collection, L.

[p. 250,2] kapā/la : m. a treaty of peace on equal terms, Kām. ix, 2 (cf. kapāla-saṃdhi below)

[p. 250,2] : N. of an intermediate caste

[p. 250,2] : N. of several men

[p. 250,2] kapā/la : m. pl. N. of a school

[p. 250,2] kapā/la : n. N. of a Tantra

[p. 250,2] kapāla : [Gk., κωπή ‘handle’; Lat. capere; Hib. gabhaim; Goth. hafyan; Angl.Sax. haban, haefene, hafoc; Eng. haven, hawk; cf. Gk. κεϕαλή Lat. caput; Goth. haubith; Angl.Sax. heafud.]
