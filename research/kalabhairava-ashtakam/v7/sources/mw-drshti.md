[p. 492,1] : sight, the faculty of seeing, ŚBr.; Mn.; Suśr. &c.

[p. 492,1] : the mind's eye, wisdom, intelligence, BhP.; L.

[p. 492,1] : regard, consideration, L.

[p. 492,1] : view, notion, Bhag.; Kap.

[p. 492,1] : (with Buddhists) a wrong view

[p. 492,1] : theory, doctrine, system, Jātakam.

[p. 492,1] : eye, look, glance, Mn.; MBh.; Kāv. &c. (˚ṭiṃ dā with loc. turn the eye to, look at, Śṛṅgār. 15)

[p. 492,1] : the pupil of the eye, Suśr.
