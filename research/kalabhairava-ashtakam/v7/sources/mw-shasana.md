[p. 1068,3] śā/sana : mf(ī)n. punishing, a punisher, chastiser (see pāka-, pura-, rukmi-, smara-ś˚)

[p. 1069,1] : teaching, instructing, an instructor, BhP.

[p. 1069,1] śā/sana : (am), n. (ifc. f(ā). ) punishment, chastisement, correction (śāsanaṃ-√ kṛ, to inflict punishment), Baudh.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1069,1] : government, dominion, rule over (comp.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1069,1] : an order, command, edict, enactment, decree, direction (śāsanaṃ-√ kṛ [kāṅkṣ, Baudh.] or śāsane-√ vṛt or sthā, ‘to obey orders’; śāsanāt with gen., ‘by command of’; f(śāsanā). Sch. on Śiś. xiv, 36), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 1069,1] : a royal edict, grant, charter (usually a grant of land or of partic. privileges, and often inscribed on stone or copper), Yājñ.; Kāv.; Rājat. &c.

[p. 1069,1] : a writing, deed, written contract or agreement, W.

[p. 1069,1] : any written book or work of authority, scripture (= śāstra), ib.

[p. 1069,1] : teaching, instruction, discipline, doctrine (also = ‘faith’, ‘religion’), MBh.; Kām.; Kathās.

[p. 1069,1] : a message (see comp.)

[p. 1069,1] : self-control, W.
