[p. 618,2] pāpa/ : mf(ī older than ā; cf. Pāṇ. iv, 1, 30) (ŚBr. xiv, also pā/pa) n. bad, vicious, wicked, evil, wretched, vile, low, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 618,2] : (in astrol.) boding evil, inauspicious, Var.

[p. 618,2] pāpa/ : m. a wicked man, wretch, villain, RV. &c., &c.

[p. 618,2] : N. of the profligate in a drama, Cat.

[p. 618,2] : of a hell, VP.

[p. 618,2] pāpa/ : n. (ifc. f(ā). ) evil, misfortune, ill-luck, trouble, mischief, harm, AV. &c. &c. (often śāntam pāpam, ‘heaven forefend that evil’, R.; Mṛcch.; Kālid. &c.)

[p. 618,2] pāpa/ : n. sin, vice, crime, guilt, Br.; Mn.; MBh. &c.
