[p. 1294,2] 1. has : cl. 1. P. (Dhātup. xvii, 72) hasati (mc. also ˚te; pf. jahāsa, jahase, MBh. &c.; aor. ahasīt Gr.; fut. hasitā, ib.; hasiṣyati, MBh. &c.; inf. hasitum, ib.; ind.p. hasitvā, -hasya, ib.),
to laugh, smile, laugh at (instr.), ṢaḍvBr.; Gobh.; MBh. &c.;
to deride, mock, ridicule (acc.), MBh.; R. &c.;
to surpass, excel, Kāvyād.;
to expand, open (as a blossom), Kuval. :
Pass. hasyate (aor. ahāsi), to be laughed or smiled at, MBh.; Kāv. &c.:
Caus. hāsayati (aor. ajīhasat), to cause to laugh, Hariv.; Kum. :
Desid. jihasiṣati (cf. √ 1. jakṣ) Gr.:
Intens. jāhasyate (p. ˚yamāna, MBh.), jāhasti, to laugh continuously or immoderately, ib.

[p. 1294,2] 2. has : ind. an exclamation of laughter or loud merriment (also employed as a Nidhana in the Sāman).
