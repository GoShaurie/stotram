[p. 1294,2] hāsa : m. (ifc. f(ā). ) laughing, laughter, mirth (often in pl.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1294,2] : mocking, derision of (gen.), R.

[p. 1294,2] : a jest, joke, fun (ākhyāna-h˚, ‘a funny story’), Kathās.

[p. 1294,2] : dazzling whiteness (regarded as laughter in which the teeth are shown), R.; Kathās.; Sāh.

[p. 1294,2] : pride, arrogance, BhP.
