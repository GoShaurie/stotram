[p. 532,1] : escaped (also -vat mfn., MBh.), run away from (abl.), fled (impers. with instr. of subj. Ratn. ii, 3), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 532,1] : spoiled, damaged, corrupted, wasted, unsuccessful, fruitless, in vain, Mn.; Yājñ.; MBh. &c.

[p. 532,1] : deprived of (instr.), R. i, 14, 18 (in comp. = ‘without’, ‘-less’, ‘un-’; see below)

[p. 532,1] : one who has lost a lawsuit, Mṛcch. ix, 4.
