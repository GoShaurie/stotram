[p. 613,2] 1. pāta : mfn. (for 2. See p. 616, col. 3) watched, protected, preserved, L.

[p. 616,3] 2. pāta : m. (for 1. See under √ 3. pā) flying, mode of flying, flight, MBh.

[p. 616,3] : throwing one's self or falling into (loc.) or from (abl.), fall, downfall (also ifc. after what would be a gen. or abl. &c. e.g., gṛha-, fall of a house; parvata-, fall from a mountain; bhū-, fall on the earth), Mn.; MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 616,3] : alighting, descending or causing to descend, casting or throwing upon, cast, fall (of a thunderbolt), throw, shot, MBh.; R.; Pañcat.

[p. 616,3] : a stroke (of a sword &c.), Kathās.

[p. 616,3] : application (of ointment, of a knife &c.), Kāvyād.

[p. 616,3] : casting or directing (a look or glance of the eyes), Ragh.

[p. 616,3] : decay of the body (deha-pāta), death, Kathās.; Bādar.

[p. 616,3] : (with garbhasya) fall of the fetus, miscarriage, Suśr.

[p. 616,3] : an attack, incursion, Var.

[p. 616,3] : a case, possibility, ŚāṅkhBr.

[p. 616,3] : happening, occurrence, appearance, Prab.; Kathās.; Daśar.

[p. 616,3] : a fault, error, mistake, Sūryas.

[p. 616,3] : the node in a planet's orbit, ib. (cf. IW. 179)

[p. 616,3] : a malignant aspect, ib.

[p. 616,3] : N. of Rāhu, L.

[p. 616,3] : pl. N. of a school of the Yajur-veda, ib.
