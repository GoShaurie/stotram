## Chosen text

अट्टहासभिन्नपद्मजाण्डकोशसन्ततिं
दृष्टिपातनष्टपापजालमुग्रशासनम् ।
अष्टसिद्धिदायकं कपालमालिकाधरं

## Variants

- c: **मालिकाधरं** in A B E F and S vs **मालिकन्धरं** in C D. The independent votes tie 2–2. मालिकाधरं ("wearing a garland of skulls") is chosen: it is in the oldest print (B), it is regular, and S agrees. मालिकन्धरं ("whose neck, कन्धर, bears the skull-garland") would need मालिका shortened before कन्धर, which is irregular. C's own Hindi still glosses it "कपालकी माला धारण करनेवाले". Both readings are metrical. **Slight change in sense. Human to confirm.**
- a: S reads पद्द्मजाण्डकोटिसन्ततिं ("a series of crores of cosmic eggs"); this is a recension reading only. सन्ततिं (C D S) vs संततिं (A E F) is spelling only. B prints कोशसतति (anusvāra lost).
- S is its stanza 7 (śl. 59).

Sense-changing: c (slight).
