[p. 574,2] paṅka—ja : n. (ifc. f(ā). ) ‘mud-born’, a species of lotus, Nelumbium Speciosum (whose flower closes in the evening), MBh.; Kāv. &c. (in Kathās. once f(ā). )

[p. 574,2] paṅka—ja : m. N. of Brahmā, Cat. (for paṅkaja-ja)

[p. 574,2] paṅka—ja : mfn. lotus-eyed, Jātakam.
