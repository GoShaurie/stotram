[p. 480,1] dig—ambara : mfn. ‘sky clothed’ i.e. quite naked, Bhartṛ. iii, 90; Pañc. v, 14

[p. 480,1] dig—ambara : m. (also -ka) a naked mendicant (esp. of the Jaina or Bauddha sect, cf. 1. kṣapaṇa), Prab.; Vet. &c., MWB. 530 &c.

[p. 480,1] : N. of Śiva or Skanda, L.

[p. 480,1] : of a grammarian, Gaṇar.

[p. 480,1] : darkness, L.
