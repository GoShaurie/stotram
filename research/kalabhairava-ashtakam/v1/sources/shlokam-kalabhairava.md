देवराजसेव्यमानपावनांघ्रिपङ्कजं
व्यालयज्ञसूत्रमिन्दुशेखरं कृपाकरम् ।
नारदादियोगिवृन्दवन्दितं दिगंबरं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे ॥१॥

Transliteration:
deva rāja sevyamāna pāvanāṅghri paṅkajaṃ
vyāla yajña sūtramindu śekharaṃ kṛpākaram .
nāradādi yogi vṛnda vanditaṃ digambaraṃ
kāśikā purādhinātha kālabhairavaṃ bhaje ..1..

Simplified transliteration:
deva raaja sevya maana paavana anghri pankajam
vyaala yajna suutram indu shekharam kripaakaram .
naarada aadi yogi vrinda vanditam digambaram
kaashikaa pura adhinaatha kaala bhairavam bhaje ..1..

Translation:
His pure lotus feet are served by the king of the gods; a serpent is his sacred thread, the moon adorns his crest, and he is the fount of compassion. Worshipped by throngs of yogis led by Nārada, clad in the directions themselves, I worship Kālabhairava, the sovereign lord of the city of Kāśī.

Word meanings:
देवराजसेव्यमान (devarājasevyamāna) – served by Indra (deva:god | rāja:king | sevyamāna:served by); पावनांघ्रिपङ्कजं (pāvanāṅghripaṅkajaṃ) – pure lotus feet (pāvana:pure | aṅghri:feet | paṅkajaṃ:lotus); व्यालयज्ञसूत्रम् (vyālayajñasūtram) – snake-sacred thread (vyāla:snake | yajña:sacred | sūtram:thread); इन्दुशेखरं (induśekharaṃ) – moon-crested (indu:moon | śekharaṃ:crest); कृपाकरम् (kṛpākaram) – abode of mercy (kṛpā:mercy | karam:abode); नारदादियोगिवृन्दवन्दितं (nāradādiyogivṛndavanditaṃ) – praised by Narada and yogis (nārada:Narada | ādi:and others | yogi:yogis | vṛnda:group | vanditaṃ:praised); दिगंबरं (digambaraṃ) – clothed in directions (dik:directions | ambaraṃ:clothed); काशिकापुराधिनाथ (kāśikāpurādhinātha) – lord of Kashi city (kāśikā:Kashi | pura:city | adhinātha:lord); कालभैरवं (kālabhairavaṃ) – Kalabhairava; भजे (bhaje) – I sing praise;
