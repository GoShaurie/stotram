[p. 1088,2] śekhara : m. (fr. or connected with śikhara) the top or crown of the head, Kathās.

[p. 1088,2] : a chaplet or wreath of flowers worn on the top of the head, crown, diadem, crest, Hariv.; Kām.; Pur. &c.

[p. 1088,2] : a peak, summit, crest (of a mountain), ib.; Rājat.

[p. 1088,2] : (mostly ifc.) the highest part, chief or head or best or most beautiful of (-tā f.), Ṛt.; Caurap.; Dhūrtas.

[p. 1088,2] : (in music) a partic. Dhruva or introductory verse of a song (recurring as a kind of refrain)

[p. 1088,2] : N. of an author (with bhaṭṭa), Cat.

[p. 1088,2] : of a grammatical work, ib.

[p. 1088,2] śekhara : n. cloves, L.

[p. 1088,2] : the root of Moringa Pterygosperma, L.
