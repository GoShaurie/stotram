[p. 1038,2] : a lion, L.

[p. 1038,2] : a tiger, L.

[p. 1038,2] : a hunting leopard, L.

[p. 1038,2] : Plumbago Ceylanica, L.

[p. 1038,2] : the second dṛkāṇa (q.v.) in Cancer, the first in Scorpio, and the third in Pisces, VarBṛS.
