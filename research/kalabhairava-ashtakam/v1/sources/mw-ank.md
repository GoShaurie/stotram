[p. 7,1] aṅk : cl. 1. (connected, with, √ añc) Ā. aṅkate, ānaṅke, aṅkiṣyate, aṅkitum, to move in a curve, L.;
 cl. 10. P. aṅkayati, to move in a curve, L.;
to mark, stamp, brand.
