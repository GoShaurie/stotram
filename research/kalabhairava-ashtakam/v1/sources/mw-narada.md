[p. 537,2] nā/rada or nārada/ : m. N. of a Ṛṣi (a Kāṇva or Kāśyapa, author of RV. viii, 13; ix, 104; 105; Anukr.; as a Devarṣi often associated with Parvata and supposed to be a messenger between gods and men, MBh.; Hariv.; Kāv. &c.; among the 10 Prajā-patis as a son of Brahmā, Mn. i, 35; in later mythology he is a friend of Kṛṣṇa and is regarded as inventor of the Vinā or lute; in ep. poetry he is called a Deva-gandharva or a Gandharva-rāja or simply Gandharva)

[p. 537,2] : of a son of Viśvā-mitra, MBh.

[p. 537,2] : of one of the 24 mythic. Buddhas, MWB. 136

[p. 537,2] : of sev. men, Lalit.; HPariś.

[p. 537,2] : of sev. authors, Cat. (cf. below)

[p. 537,2] : of a mountain, BhP.

[p. 537,2] nā/rada : mf(ī)n. relating to Nārada, composed by him, Cat.
