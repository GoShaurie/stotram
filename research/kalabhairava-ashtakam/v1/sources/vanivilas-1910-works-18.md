देवराजसेव्यमानपावनाङ्घ्रिपङ्कज
व्यालयज्ञसूत्रबिन्दुशेखर कृपाकरम् ।
नारदादियोगिवृन्दवन्दित दिगम्बर
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरव भजे ॥ १ ॥

Transcriber's notes: This 1910 print systematically omits anusvāra (the dot is missing throughout, e.g. पावनाङ्घ्रिपङ्कज for -पङ्कजं, भजे lines end in भैरव) and sometimes loses reph and e-/o-mātrās; these are typesetting losses, not readings. Transcribed exactly as printed; the normalised reading is given in the note where it differs. Print reads सूत्रबिन्दु (म/ब glyph is unambiguous ब in the scan); every other witness has सूत्रमिन्दु. Treated as a misprint, not a reading.
