[p. 839,2] yajña/ : m. worship, devotion, prayer, praise

[p. 839,2] : act of worship or devotion, offering, oblation, sacrifice (the former meanings prevailing in Veda, the latter in post-Vedic literature; cf. mahā-y˚), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 839,2] : a worshipper, sacrificer, RV. iii, 30, 15; 32, 12

[p. 839,2] : fire, L.

[p. 839,2] : = ātman, L.

[p. 839,2] : Sacrifice personified, MBh.; Hariv.

[p. 839,2] : (with prājāpatya) N. of the reputed author of RV. x, 130, Anukr.

[p. 839,2] : N. of a form of Viṣṇu, Pur.

[p. 839,2] : of Indra under Manu Svāyambhuva, ib.

[p. 839,2] : of a son of Ruci and Ākūti, ib.
