[p. 1086,3] śūl : (rather Nom. fr. next) cl. 1. P. śūlati, to hurt, cause pain (Dhātup. xv, 19), (only occurring in Ā. śūlate and cl. 4. P. Ā. śūlyati, ˚te, Car.; accord. to Dhātup. also saṃghoṣe, or saṃghāte, ‘to sound’ or ‘to collect’).
