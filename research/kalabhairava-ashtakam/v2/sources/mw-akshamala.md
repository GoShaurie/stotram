[p. 3,2] akṣa—mālā : f. a string or rosary of beads, especially of Eleocarpus seeds

[p. 3,2] : N. of Arundhatī, wife of Vasiṣṭha (from her wearing a rosary), Mn. ix, 23

[p. 3,2] : N. of the mother of Vatsa.
