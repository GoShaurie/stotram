[p. 566,1] nīla—kaṇṭha : mfn. blue-necked, MBh.

[p. 566,1] nīla—kaṇṭha : m. a peacock, MBh.; Kāv.; Suśr. a species of gallinule or water-hen (= dātyūha), L.

[p. 566,1] : a wagtail, L.

[p. 566,2] : a sparrow, L.

[p. 566,2] : the blue-necked jay, L.

[p. 566,2] : Ardea Sibirica, L.

[p. 566,2] : a species of plant (= pīta-sāra), L.

[p. 566,2] : N. of Śiva (as having a black throat from swallowing the poison produced at the churning of the ocean), R.; Hit. &c.

[p. 566,2] : N. of the celebrated Sch. on MBh. and of other authors (also -dīkṣita, -nāga-nātha, -bhaṭṭa, -bhāratī, -miśra, -śarman, -śāstrin, -śivācārya, -sūnu, -sūri, ˚ṭhācārya), Cat.

[p. 566,2] nīla—kaṇṭha : n. a radish, L.

[p. 566,2] : N. of a Tīrtha, Cat.
