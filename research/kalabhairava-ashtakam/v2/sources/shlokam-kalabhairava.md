भानुकोटिभास्वरं भवाब्धितारकं परं
नीलकण्ठमीप्सितार्थदायकं त्रिलोचनम् ।
कालकालमंबुजाक्षमक्षशूलमक्षरं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे ॥२॥

Transliteration:
bhānu koṭi bhāsvaraṃ bhavābdhitārakaṃ paraṃ
nīla kaṇṭhamīpsitārtha dāyakaṃ trilocanam .
kālakālamambujākṣamakṣaśūlamakṣaraṃ
kāśikā purādhinātha kālabhairavaṃ bhaje ..2..

Simplified transliteration:
bhaanu koti bhaasvaram bhavaabdhi taarakam param
niila kantha miipsitaartha daayakam trilochanam .
kaala kaalam ambuja aksham aksha shuulam aksharam
kaashikaa puraadhinaatha kaala bhairavam bhaje ..2..

Translation:
Radiant as ten million suns, the one who ferries souls across the ocean of worldly existence, the Supreme; the blue-throated one, the granter of desired boons, the three-eyed; Death unto Death itself, the lotus-eyed, wielder of the imperishable trident, the imperishable — I worship Kālabhairava, the sovereign lord of the city of Kāśī.

Word meanings:
भानुकोटिभास्वरं (bhānukoṭibhāsvaraṃ) – resplendent like millions of suns (bhānu:sun | koṭi:millions | bhāsvaraṃ:resplendent); भवाब्धितारकं (bhavābdhitārakaṃ) – absolves the ocean of rebirth (bhava:rebirth | abdhi:ocean | tārakaṃ:absolves); परं (paraṃ) – supreme; नीलकण्ठम् (nīlakaṇṭham) – blue neck (nīla:blue | kaṇṭham:neck); ईप्सितार्थदायकं (īpsitārthadāyakaṃ) – bestows desired objects (īpsita:desired | artha:objects | dāyakaṃ:bestows); त्रिलोचनम् (trilocanam) – three-eyed (tri:three | locanam:eye); कालकालम् (kālakālam) – end of time (kāla:time | kālam:end); अंबुजाक्षम् (ambujākṣam) – lotus-eyed (ambu:lotus | akṣam:eye); अक्षशूलम् (akṣaśūlam) – immortal weapon (akṣa:immortal | śūlam:weapon); अक्षरं (akṣaraṃ) – immortal; काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं (kāśikāpurādhināthakālabhairavaṃ) – ruler of Kashi, Kalabhairava (kāśikā:Kashi | pura:city | adhinātha:ruler | kālabhairavaṃ:Kalabhairava); भजे (bhaje) – I sing praise;
