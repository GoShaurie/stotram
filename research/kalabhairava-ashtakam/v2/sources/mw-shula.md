[p. 1086,3] śū/la : m. n. (ifc. f. ā.) a sharp iron pin or stake, spike, spit (on which meat is roasted), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 1086,3] : any sharp instrument or pointed dart, lance, pike, spear (esp. the trident of Śiva), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1086,3] : a stake for impaling criminals (śūlam ā-√ ruh, ‘to be fixed on a stake, suffer impalement’; with Caus. of ā-√ ruh, ‘to fix on a stake, have any one [acc.] impaled’, cf. śūlādhiropita &c.), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1086,3] : any sharp or acute pain (esp. that of colic or gout), Kāv.; VarBṛS.; Suśr.

[p. 1086,3] : pain, grief, sorrow, MBh.; Hariv.

[p. 1086,3] : death, L.

[p. 1086,3] : a flag, banner, L.

[p. 1086,3] : = -yoga (q.v.), VarBṛS.
