[p. 1293,2] halāhala : m. n. a kind of deadly poison (produced at the churning of the ocean by gods and demons), Kāv.; Pañcat.

[p. 1293,2] halāhala : m. (only L.) a kind of lizard

[p. 1293,2] : a kind of snake

[p. 1293,2] : a Jaina or Bauddha sage.
