[p. 84,1] a/mbhas : n. (cf. abhra/, a/mbu), water, RV. &c., the celestial waters, AitUp.

[p. 84,1] : power, fruitfulness, VS. & AV.

[p. 84,1] : (āṃsi), pl. collective N. for gods, men, Manes, and Asuras, TBr. & VP., (hence) (as) sg. the number ‘four’

[p. 84,1] : mystical N. of the letter v

[p. 84,1] : N. of a metre (consisting of 82 syllables), RPrāt., (asa), instr. in comp. for ambhas (e.g. ambhasākṛta ‘done by water’), Pāṇ. vi, 3, 3

[p. 84,1] a/bhasī, du. (asī) : heaven and earth, Naigh. [Gk. ὄμβρος imber].
