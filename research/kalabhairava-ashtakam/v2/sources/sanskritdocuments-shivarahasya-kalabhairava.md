भानुकोटिभास्वरं भवाब्धितारकं परं
नीलकण्ठनीप्सितार्थदायकं त्रिलोचनम् ।
कालकालमम्बुजाक्षकन्धराहरं शिवं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे । ३ ॥ ५५॥

Notes: separate recension (Īśvara speaking to Pārvatī). Its stanza 3 corresponds to our verse 2. Order differs from the Śaṅkara text: its stanzas run 1, 4, 2, 3, 6, 5, 7, 8 in our numbering. The source itself says: “This text is slightly different from the more popularly known Aśṭakam that is attributed to Shankaracharya.”
