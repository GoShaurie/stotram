[p. 90,2] a/rtha : m. n. [in RV. i-ix only n.; in RV. x, six times n. and thrice m.; in later Sanskṛt only m.] aim, purpose (very often artham, arthena, arthāya, and arthe ifc. or with gen. ‘for the sake of, on account of, in behalf of, for’)

[p. 90,3] : cause, motive, reason, Mn. ii, 213, &c.

[p. 90,3] : advantage, use, utility (generally named with kāma and dharma See tri-varga; used in wishing well to another dat. or gen. Pāṇ. ii, 3, 73)

[p. 90,3] : thing, object (said of the membrum virile, ŚBr. xiv)

[p. 90,3] : object of the senses, VarBṛS.

[p. 90,3] : (hence) the number ‘five’, Sūryas.

[p. 90,3] : substance, wealth, property, opulence, money

[p. 90,3] : (hence in astron.) N. of the second mansion, the mansion of wealth (cf. dhana), VarBṛS.

[p. 90,3] : personified as the son of Dharma and Buddhi, BhP.

[p. 90,3] : affair, concern (Ved. often acc. a/rtham with √ i, or gam, to go to one's business, take up one's work, RV. &c.)

[p. 90,3] : (in law) lawsuit, action

[p. 90,3] : having to do with (instr.), wanting, needing anything (instr.), ŚBr., &c.

[p. 90,3] : sense, meaning, notion (cf. artha-śabdau and arthāt s.v. below and vedatattvārtha-vid)

[p. 90,3] : manner, kind, L., prohibition, prevention, L.

[p. 90,3] : price (for argha, q.v.), L.
