भूतसङ्घनायकं विशालकीर्तिदायकं
काशिवासकोकपुण्यपापशोधनप्रभुम् ।
नीतिमार्गकोविदं पुरातनं जगत्प्रभुं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे । ८ ॥ ६०॥

Notes: separate recension (Īśvara speaking to Pārvatī). Its stanza 8 corresponds to our verse 8. The source itself says: “This text is slightly different from the more popularly known Aśṭakam that is attributed to Shankaracharya.”
