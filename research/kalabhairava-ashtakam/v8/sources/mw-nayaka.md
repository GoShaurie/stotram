[p. 536,3] nāyaka : m. a guide, leader, chief, lord, principal, MBh.; Kāv. &c. (with or scil. sainyasya, a general, commander; ifc. f(akā). cf. a-nāyaka)

[p. 536,3] : a husband, BhP.

[p. 536,3] : (in dram.) the lover or hero

[p. 536,3] : the central gem of a necklace (implying also ‘a general’, cf. nāyakāya and mahā-nāyaka)

[p. 536,3] : a paradigm or example (in gram.)

[p. 536,3] : N. of Gautama Buddha, Buddh.

[p. 536,3] : of a Brāhman, Rājat.

[p. 536,3] : of an author (also bhaṭṭa-n˚), Cat.

[p. 536,3] nāyaka : m. n. a kind of musk, L. (cf. nāyikā-cūrṇa)
