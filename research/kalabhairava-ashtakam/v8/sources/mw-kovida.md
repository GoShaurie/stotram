[p. 314,1] ko-vida : mf(ā)n. (√ vid) experienced, skilled, learned in (loc. gen., or ifc., e.g. aśveṣu, or aśvānām or aśva-kovida, ‘skilled in horses’), Mn. vii, 26; MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 314,1] ko-vida : m. pl. N. of the Kṣatriyas in Kuśa-dvīpa, BhP. v, 20, 16.
