[p. 947,2] 1. vāsa : m. perfuming, perfume, Vikr.; Mālatīm.; Car.

[p. 947,2] : Gendarussa Vulgaris, L. (also f(ā). )

[p. 947,2] 2. vāsa : m. (fr. √ 4. vas) a garment, dress, clothes (mc. for vāsas), MBh. (cf. kṛṣṇavāsa).

[p. 947,2] 3. vāsa/ : m. (fr. √ 5. vas) staying, remaining (esp. ‘overnight’), abiding, dwelling, residence, living in (loc. or comp.; cf. Pāṇ. vi, 3, 18, Sch.), abode, habitation, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 947,2] : ifc. = having one's abode in, dwelling or living in

[p. 947,3] : vāsaṃ-√ vas, to take up one's abode, abide, dwell

[p. 947,3] : place or seat of (gen.), R.

[p. 947,3] : a day's journey, ib.

[p. 947,3] : state, situation, condition, Hariv.

[p. 947,3] : = vāsa-gṛha, bed-chamber (see -sajjā)

[p. 947,3] : = vāsanā, imagination, idea, semblance of MBh.
