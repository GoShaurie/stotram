[p. 1122,3] saṃ-gha : &c. See s.v.

[p. 1129,3] saṃ-gha : m. (fr. sam + √ han) ‘close contact or combination’, any collection or assemblage, heap, multitude, quantity, crowd, host, number (generally with gen. pl. or ifc., e.g. muni-s˚, ‘a multitude of sages’, BhP.; śatru-s˚, a host of enemies, Rājat.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1129,3] : any number of people living together for a certain purpose, a society, association, company, community

[p. 1129,3] : a clerical community, congregation, church, Mn.; Sāh. &c.

[p. 1129,3] : (esp.) the whole community or collective body or brotherhood of monks (with Buddhists; also applied to a monkish fraternity or sect among Jainas), Buddh.; Sarvad.; MWB. 176.
