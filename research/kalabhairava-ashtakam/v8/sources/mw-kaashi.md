[p. 280,2] kāśi/ : is, m. ‘shining’, the sun, L.

[p. 280,2] : the clenched hand, fist, handful, RV. iii, 30, 5; vii, 104, 8; viii, 78, 10; Kauś.

[p. 280,2] : N. of a prince (the ancestor of the kings of Kāśi, of the family of Bharata, son of Suhotra and grandfather of Dhanvantari, Hariv. 1734; the son of Kāśya and grandson of Suhotra, BhP. ix, 17, 4)

[p. 280,2] : (a/yas), m. pl. (a/yas) the descendants of this prince, BhP. ix, 17, 10

[p. 280,2] : N. of the people of Kāśi, ŚBr. xiii; MBh. &c.

[p. 280,2] kāśi : (is), f. ‘the splendid’, N. of a celebrated city and place of pilgrimage (the modern Benares, usually written Kāśī q.v.), Uṇ. iv, 119

[p. 280,2] : fine cotton or silk (from Kāśi), Divyāv.

[p. 280,3] kāśi/ , : kāśika. See col. 2.
