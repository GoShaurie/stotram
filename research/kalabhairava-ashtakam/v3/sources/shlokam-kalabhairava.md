शूलटङ्कपाशदण्डपाणिमादिकारणं
श्यामकायमादिदेवमक्षरं निरामयम् ।
भीमविक्रमं प्रभुं विचित्रताण्डवप्रियं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे ॥३॥

Transliteration:
śūla ṭaṅka pāśa daṇḍa pāṇimādi kāraṇaṃ
śyāma kāyamādi devamakṣaraṃ nirāmayam .
bhīmavikramaṃ prabhuṃ vicitra tāṇḍava priyaṃ
kāśikā purādhinātha kālabhairavaṃ bhaje ..3..

Simplified transliteration:
shuula tanka paasha danda paanim aadi kaaranam
shyaama kaayam aadi devam aksharam niraamayam .
bhiima vikramam prabhum vichitra taandava priyam
kaashikaa pura adhinaatha kaala bhairavam bhaje ..3..

Translation:
He who bears the trident, the axe, the noose, and the staff in his hands, the primordial cause of all; whose body is dark, the first among gods, the imperishable and unblemished one; of terrible valour, the Lord who delights in his wondrous cosmic dance — to that Kālabhairava, sovereign of the city of Kāśī, I offer worship.

Word meanings:
शूल (śūla) – trident; टङ्क (ṭaṅka) – spade; पाश (pāśa) – cord; दण्ड (daṇḍa) – staff; पाणिम् (pāṇim) – in hand; आदि (ādi) – beginning; कारणम् (kāraṇam) – cause; श्याम (śyāma) – dark; कायम् (kāyam) – body; आदि (ādi) – first; देवम् (devam) – deity; अक्षरम् (akṣaram) – imperishable; निरामयम् (nirāmayam) – free from illness; भीमविक्रमम् (bhīmavikramam) – immensely mighty (bhīma:immense | vikrama:mighty); प्रभुम् (prabhum) – lord; विचित्र (vicitra) – special; ताण्डवप्रियम् (tāṇḍavapriyam) – loves the Tandava dance (tāṇḍava:Tandava dance | priya:loves); काशिकापुराधिनाथ (kāśikāpurādhinātha) – ruler of Kashi city (kāśikā:Kashi | pura:city | adhinātha:ruler); कालभैरवम् (kālabhairavam) – Kalabhairava; भजे (bhaje) – I sing praise;
