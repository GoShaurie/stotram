शूलटङ्कपाशदण्डपाणिमादिकारणं
श्यामकायमादिदेवमक्षरं निरामयम् ।
भीमवक्रिमभ्रुवं विचित्रताण्डवप्रियं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे । ४ ॥ ५६॥

Notes: separate recension (Īśvara speaking to Pārvatī). Its stanza 4 corresponds to our verse 3. Order differs from the Śaṅkara text: its stanzas run 1, 4, 2, 3, 6, 5, 7, 8 in our numbering. The source itself says: “This text is slightly different from the more popularly known Aśṭakam that is attributed to Shankaracharya.”
