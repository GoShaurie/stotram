[p. 950,1] 1. vi—krama : m. (for 2. See vi-√ kram) the absence of the Krama-pāṭha (q.v.), RPrāt.

[p. 955,2] 2. vi-krama/ : m. (for 1. See p. 950, col. 1) a step, stride, pace, ŚBr. &c. &c.

[p. 955,2] : going, proceeding, walking, motion, gait, MBh., Kāś. &c.

[p. 955,2] : course, way, manner (anukrama-vikrameṇa = anukrameṇa, in regular order), MBh.

[p. 955,2] : valour, courage, heroism, power, strength, ib.; Kāv. &c. (˚maṃ-√ kṛ, to display prowess, use one's strength)

[p. 955,2] : force, forcible means, ib. (˚māt ind. by force; nāsti vikrameṇa, it cannot be done by force)

[p. 955,2] : intensity, high degree, VarBṛS.

[p. 955,2] : stability, duration (opp. to ‘cessation’), BhP.

[p. 955,2] : a kind of grave accent, TPrāt.

[p. 955,2] : non-change of the Visarga into an Ūṣman, RPrāt.

[p. 955,2] : the 14th year in the 60 years cycle of Jupiter, VarBṛS.

[p. 955,2] : the 3rd astrological house, ib.

[p. 955,2] : a foot, L.

[p. 955,2] : N. of Viṣṇu, MBh.

[p. 955,2] : of various authors (also with bhaṭṭa), Cat.
