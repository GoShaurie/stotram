[p. 854,2] yugma : mf(ā)n. even (as opp. to ‘odd’), GṛŚrS.; Mn. &c.

[p. 854,2] yugma : n. a pair, couple, brace, ŚāṅkhGṛ.; Yājñ.; R. &c.

[p. 854,2] : twins, Suśr.

[p. 854,2] : (in astron.) the sign of the zodiac Gemini

[p. 854,2] : a double Śloka (cf. yuga), Rājat.

[p. 854,2] : junction, confluence (of two streams), R.

[p. 854,2] : often w.r. for yugya.
