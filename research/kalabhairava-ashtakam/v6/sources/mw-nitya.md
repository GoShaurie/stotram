[p. 547,2] ni/tya : mf(ā)n. (fr. ni; cf. ni-ja) innate, native, MBh. iii, 13941

[p. 547,2] : one's own (opp. to araṇa), RV.

[p. 547,2] : continual, perpetual, eternal, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 547,2] : ifc. constantly dwelling or engaged in, intent upon, devoted or used to (cf. tapo-n˚, dharma-n˚, dhyāna-n˚, śastra-n˚), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 547,2] : ordinary, usual, invariable, fixed, necessary, obligatory (opp. to kāmya, naimittika &c.), Br.; ŚrS.; Mn. &c. (with samāsa m. a compound the meaning of which is not expressed by its members when not compounded, Pāṇ. ii, 1, 3, Sch.; with svarita m. = jātya, the independent Svarita, TPrāt. ii, 8)

[p. 547,2] ni/tya : m. the sea, ocean, L.

[p. 547,2] ni/tya : n. constant and indispensable rite or act, W.
