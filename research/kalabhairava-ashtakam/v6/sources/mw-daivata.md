[p. 498,1] daivata : mf(ī)n. (fr. devatā) relating to the gods or to a partic. deity, divine, Śr. & GṛS.

[p. 498,1] daivata : m. N. of a prince, VP.

[p. 498,1] daivata : n. (m. g. ardharcādi) a god, a deity (often coll. ‘the deities’, esp. as celebrated in one hymn, cf. g. prajñādi), ŚrGṛS.; Up.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 498,1] daivata : n. image of a god, idol, Kauś.; Mn.; BhP.

[p. 498,1] daivata : mf(ā)n. ifc. having as one's deity, worshipping (cf. ab- [add.], tad-, bhartṛ-).
