[p. 11,1] añjana : m. a kind of domestic lizard, L.

[p. 11,1] : N. of a fabulous, serpent

[p. 11,1] : of a tree, Pañcat.

[p. 11,1] : of a mountain, of a king of Mithilā, of the elephant of the west or south-west quarter

[p. 11,1] añjana : n. act of applying an ointment or pigment, embellishing, &c., black pigment or collyrium applied to the eyelashes or the inner coat of the eyelids

[p. 11,1] : a special kind of this pigment, as lamp-black, Antimony, extract of Ammonium, Xanthorrhiza, &c.

[p. 11,1] : paint, especially as a cosmetic

[p. 11,1] : magic ointment

[p. 11,1] : ink, L.

[p. 11,1] : night, L.

[p. 11,1] : fire, L. (In rhetoric) making clear the meaning of an equivocal expression, double entendre or pun, &c.
