[p. 864,2] : a jewel, gem, treasure, precious stone (the nine j˚ are pearl, ruby, topaz, diamond, emerald, lapis lazuli, coral, sapphire, Gomeda; hence ratna is a N. for the number 9; but accord. to some 14), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 864,2] : a magnet, loadstone, Kap., Sch. (cf. maṇi)

[p. 864,2] : water, L.
