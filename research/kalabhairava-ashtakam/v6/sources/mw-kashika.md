[p. 280,2] kāśikā : f. (scil. purī) ‘the city of the Kāśis’, Benares

[p. 280,2] : (with or without vṛtti) ‘the Comm. composed or used in Kāśi’, N. of a Comm. on Pāṇ. by Vāmana and Jayāditya.

[p. 280,3] kāśikā : (f. of kāśika, q.v.)
