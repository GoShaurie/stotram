[p. 827,3] mṛtyu/ : m. (very rarely f.) death, dying, RV. &c., &c.

[p. 827,3] : (deaths of different kinds are enumerated, 100 from disease or accident and one natural from old age; ifc. = ‘d˚ caused by or through’)

[p. 827,3] : Death personified, the god of d˚ (sometimes identified with Yama or with Viṣṇu; or said to be a son of Adharma by Nirṛti or of Brahmā or of Kali or of Māyā; he has also the patronymics Prādhvaṃsana and Sāmparāyaṇa, and is sometimes reckoned among the 11 Rudras, and sometimes regarded as Vyāsa in the 6th Dvāpara or as a teacher &c.), ŚBr.; MBh.; Pur. &c.

[p. 828,1] : N. of the god of love, L.

[p. 828,1] : of a partic. Ekāha, ŚāṅkhŚr.

[p. 828,1] : of the 8th astrol. house, VarBṛS.

[p. 828,1] : of the 17th astrol. Yoga, Col. (mṛtyor haraḥ and mrityor vikarṇa-bhāse N. of Sāmans).

[p. 830,1] mṛtyu : &c. See p. 827, col. 3.
