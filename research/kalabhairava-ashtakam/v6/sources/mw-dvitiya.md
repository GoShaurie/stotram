[p. 506,2] dvitī/ya : mf(ā)n. (fr. dvi, Pāṇ. v, 2, 54; decl. i, 1, 36, Vārtt. 3, Pat. cf. vii, 3, 115) second, RV. &c. &c.

[p. 506,2] dvitī/ya : m. companion, fellow (friend or foe), ŚBr.; MBh. &c.

[p. 506,2] : ifc. doubled or accompanied by, furnished with (cf. a-, chāyā-, dhanur- &c.)

[p. 506,2] : the 2nd in a family (i.e. a son, L.; cf. AitBr. vii, 29)

[p. 506,2] : the 2nd letter of a Varga i.e. the surd aspirate, Prāt.; Pāṇ. &c.

[p. 506,2] dvi/tīya : mfn. (Pāṇ. v, 3, 49) forming the 2nd part or half of anything, with bhāga m. half of (gen.), Mn. iv, 1 &c.

[p. 506,2] dvitī/ya : n. the half (at the beginning or end of a comp.), Pāṇ.; ib., ii, 2, 3, Kāś.
