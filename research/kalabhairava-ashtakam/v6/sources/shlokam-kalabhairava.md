रत्नपादुकाप्रभाभिरामपादयुग्मकं
नित्यमद्वितीयमिष्टदैवतं निरंजनम् ।
मृत्युदर्पनाशनं करालदंष्ट्रमोक्षणं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे ॥६॥

Transliteration:
ratna pādukā prabhābhi rāma pāda yugmakaṃ
nityamadvitīyamiṣṭa daivataṃ nirañjanam .
mṛtyu darpa nāśanaṃ karāla daṃṣṭra mokṣaṇaṃ
kāśikā purādhinātha kālabhairavaṃ bhaje ..6..

Simplified transliteration:
ratna paadukaa prabhaabhi raama paada yugmakam
nityam advitiiyam ishta daivatam niranjanam .
mrityu darpa naashanam karaala damshtra mokshanam
kaashikaa puraadhinaatha kaalabhairavam bhaje ..6..

Translation:
His pair of feet is radiant with the splendour of jewelled sandals; He is eternal, without a second, the cherished deity, untainted and stainless. He is the destroyer of Death's arrogance, and by His fearsome fangs He grants liberation. I worship Kālabhairava, the sovereign lord of the city of Kāśī.

Word meanings:
