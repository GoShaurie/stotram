[p. 1299,2] he/man : &c. See 2. he/man p. 1304, col. 1.

[p. 1304,1] 1. hema/n : n. impulse, RV. ix, 97, 1 (Sāy. ‘gold’)

[p. 1304,1] : water, Naigh. i, 12.

[p. 1304,1] 2. he/man : (see hima/ and next), winter (only used in loc., ‘in the winter’), TS.; Kāṭh.; ŚBr.

[p. 1304,2] 3. heman : n. (of doubtful derivation) gold, Naigh. i, 2; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1304,2] : a gold piece, Kathās.

[p. 1304,2] : (pl.) golden ornaments, Kuval.

[p. 1304,2] : Mesua Roxburghii, Car.

[p. 1304,2] : the thorn-apple, MW.

[p. 1305,1] 4. heman : m. the planet Mercury, L.
