भुक्तिमुक्तिदायकं प्रशस्तचारुविग्रहं
भक्तवत्सलं स्थितं समस्तलोकविग्रहम् ।
विनिक्वणन्मनोज्ञहेमकिङ्किणीलसत्कटिं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे ॥४॥

Transliteration:
bhukti mukti dāyakaṃ praśasta cāru vigrahaṃ
bhakta vatsalaṃ sthitaṃ samasta loka vigraham .
vi nikvaṇanmanojña hemakiṅkiṇī lasatkaṭiṃ
kāśikā purādhinātha kālabhairavaṃ bhaje ..4..

Simplified transliteration:
bhukti mukti daayakam prashasta chaaru vigraham
bhakta vatsalam sthitam samasta loka vigraham .
vi nikvanan manojna hema kinkinii lasat katim
kaashikaa pura adhinaatha kaala bhairavam bhaje ..4..

Translation:
He who bestows both worldly enjoyment and liberation, whose form is praised and beautiful, who is tender toward his devotees, the ever-abiding one, the very embodiment of all the worlds; whose waist gleams with a charming golden girdle of bells that rings melodiously — that Kālabhairava, the sovereign lord of the city of Kāśī, I worship.

Word meanings:
भुक्तिमुक्तिदायकं (bhuktimuktidāyakaṃ) – bestower of desires and salvation (bhukti:desires | mukti:salvation | dāyakaṃ:bestower); प्रशस्त (praśasta) – praiseworthy; चारु (cāru) – beautiful; विग्रहं (vigrahaṃ) – form; भक्तवत्सलं (bhaktavatsalaṃ) – endearing to devotees (bhakta:devotees | vatsalaṃ:endearing); स्थितं (sthitaṃ) – ever-abiding; समस्तलोकविग्रहम् (samastalokavigraham) – form of the entire world (samasta:entire | loka:world | vigraham:form); विनिक्वणन्मनोज्ञ (vinikvaṇanmanojña) – melodious and enchanting (vinikvaṇan:melodious | manojña:enchanting); हेमकिङ्किणीलसत्कटिं (hemakiṅkiṇīlasatkaṭiṃ) – golden waist-thread with bells (hema:golden | kiṅkiṇī:bells | lasat:shining | kaṭiṃ:waist-thread); काशिकापुराधिनाथ (kāśikāpurādhinātha) – ruler of Kashi city (kāśikā:Kashi | pura:city | adhinātha:ruler); कालभैरवं (kālabhairavaṃ) – Kalabhairava; भजे (bhaje) – I sing praise;
