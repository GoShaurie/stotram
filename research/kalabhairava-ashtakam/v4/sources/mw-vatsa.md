[p. 915,3] vatsa/ : m. (prob. originally, ‘yearling’, fr. a lost word vatas) a calf, the young of any animal, offspring, child (voc. vatsa often used as a term of endearment = my dear child, my darling), RV. &c. &c.

[p. 915,3] : a son, boy (see bāla-v˚)

[p. 915,3] : a year (see tri-v˚)

[p. 915,3] : N. of a descendant of Kaṇva, RV.; PañcavBr.; ŚāṅkhŚr.

[p. 915,3] : of an Āgneya (author of RV. x, 187), Anukr.

[p. 915,3] : of a Kāśyapa, Kathās.

[p. 915,3] : of the step-brother of Maitreya (who passed through fire to prove the falseness of Maitreya's allegation that he was the child of a Śūdra), Mn. viii, 116 (Sch.) of a son of Pratardana, MBh.; Hariv.

[p. 915,3] : of a son of Sena-jit, Hariv.

[p. 915,3] : of a son of Akṣa-mālā, Cat.

[p. 915,3] : of a son of Uru-kṣepa, VP.

[p. 915,3] : of a son of Soma-śarman, Kathās.

[p. 915,3] : of the author of a law-book, Cat.

[p. 915,3] : (with carakādhvaryu-sūtra-kṛt) of another author, ib.

[p. 915,3] : of a serpent-demon, VP.

[p. 915,3] : N. of a country (whose chief town is Kauśāmbī), Kathās.

[p. 915,3] : Nerium Antidysentericum, L.

[p. 915,3] : the Kutaja tree, L.

[p. 915,3] : pl. the descendants of Vatsa, ĀśvŚr. (cf. Pāṇ. ii, 4, 64, Sch.)

[p. 915,3] : the inhabitants of the country called Vatsa, MBh.; Kathās.

[p. 915,3] vatsa/ : m. n. the breast, chest, L.

[p. 915,3] vatsa : [cf. vatsara and ἔτος for ϝέτος; Lat. vetus, vetus-tus, vitulus; Germ. widar, Widder; Eng. wether.]
