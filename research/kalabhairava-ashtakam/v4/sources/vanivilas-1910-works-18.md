भुक्तिमुक्तिदायक प्रशस्तचारुविग्रह
भक्तवत्सल स्थिर समस्तलाकाविग्रहम् ।
निक्वणन्मनोज्ञहेमकिङ्किणीलसत्कटिं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरव भज ॥ ४ ॥

Transcriber's notes: This 1910 print systematically omits anusvāra (the dot is missing throughout, e.g. पावनाङ्घ्रिपङ्कज for -पङ्कजं, भजे lines end in भैरव) and sometimes loses reph and e-/o-mātrās; these are typesetting losses, not readings. Transcribed exactly as printed; the normalised reading is given in the note where it differs. समस्तलाकाविग्रहम् as printed (broken ो mātrā; = समस्तलोकविग्रहम्). भज for भजे (lost e-mātrā). निक्वणन् is printed with the old स्व/क्व-type ligature (OCR misreads it as निकणन्); there is no वि- before it. Final लसत्कटिं / v8 जगत्पतिं: a dot-like mark is visible above the last akṣara; read as anusvāra, though the print omits anusvāra elsewhere.
