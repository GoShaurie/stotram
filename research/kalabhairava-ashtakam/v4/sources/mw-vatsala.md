[p. 916,1] vatsala : mf(ā)n. child-loving, affectionate towards offspring (ā f. with or scil. go or dhenu, a cow longing for her calf), MBh.; R.; BhP.

[p. 916,1] : kind, loving, tender, fond of or devoted to (loc. gen. acc. with prati, or comp.), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 916,1] vatsala : m. (with rasa) the tender sentiment in a poem, Sāh.

[p. 916,1] : a fire fed with grass (i.e. quickly burning away), L.

[p. 916,1] : N. of one of Skanda's attendants, MBh.

[p. 916,1] vatsala : n. = next, W.
