[p. 324,2] kvaṇ : cl. 1. P. kvaṇati (pr. p. kvaṇat), to sound, make any sound, tinkle, Amar.; Kathās. lxxxv, 25; Hit.;
to hum (as a bee), Bhaṭṭ. vi, 84;
 (said of Kiṃnaras and Yātu-dhānas), Kum. i, 55 and Bhaṭṭ. (aor. 3. pl. akvāṇiṣur) :
Caus. P. kvaṇayati (pr. p. f. ˚yatī), to cause to sound, make sound (as a musical instrument), blow (the flute), BhP. iii, 15, 21; x, 44, 13 and 16;
to produce a sound with (instr.), iv, 24, 12; x, 60, 8.
