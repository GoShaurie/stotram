[p. 1264,1] sthita/ : mfn. standing (as opp. to ‘going’, ‘sitting’, or ‘lying’; parasparaṃ sthitam, ‘standing opposed to each other’; sthitaṃ tena, ‘it was stood by him’ = ‘he waited’), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1264,1] : standing firm (yuddhe, ‘in battle’), Hariv.

[p. 1264,1] : standing, staying, situated, resting or abiding or remaining in (loc. or comp.; with uccāvaceṣu, ‘abiding in things high and low’; with anityam, ‘not remaining permanently’, ‘staying only a short time’), KātyŚr.; Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1264,1] : being or remaining or keeping in any state or condition (loc., instr. abl. comp., or a noun in the same case, also ind.p. or adv.; vyāpya sthitaḥ, ‘he keeps continually pervading’, Śak.; Vikr.; upaviśya sthitaḥ, ‘he remains sitting’, Vikr.; kathaṃ sthitāsi, ‘how did you fare?’, Vikr. ; evaṃ sthite, ‘it being so’, Pañc.; puraḥ sthite, ‘it being imminent’), MBh.; Kāv. &c.

[p. 1264,2] : engaged in, occupied with, intent upon, engrossed by, devoted or addicted to (loc. or comp.), performing, protecting, Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1264,2] : abiding by, conforming to, following (loc.), ib.

[p. 1264,2] : being in office or charge, Pañcat.; Rājat.

[p. 1264,2] : adhering to or keeping with (loc.), Hariv.

[p. 1264,2] : lasting, RPrāt.

[p. 1264,2] : firm, constant, invariable, Kathās.

[p. 1264,2] : settled, ascertained, decreed, established, generally accepted, ŚBr. &c. &c.

[p. 1264,2] : fixed upon, determined, Śak.

[p. 1264,2] : firmly convinced or persuaded, MBh.; Subh.

[p. 1264,2] : firmly resolved to (inf. or loc.), MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 1264,2] : faithful to a promise or agreement, L. : upright, virtuous, L.

[p. 1264,2] : prepared for or to (dat.), Yājñ.; Kum.

[p. 1264,2] : being there, existing, present, close at hand, ready (sthito hy eṣaḥ, ‘I myself am ready’; agraje sthite, ‘when the elder brother is there’), Mn.; MBh. &c.

[p. 1264,2] : belonging to (gen.), R.

[p. 1264,2] : turned or directed to, fixed upon (loc. or comp.), VarBṛS.; Sarvad.

[p. 1264,2] : resting or depending on (loc.), MBh.; R. &c.

[p. 1264,2] : leading or conducive to (dat.), Pañcat.

[p. 1264,2] : one who has desisted or ceased, Pañcad.

[p. 1264,2] : left over, L.

[p. 1264,2] : (in Vedic gram.) not accompanied by iti (in the Pada-pāṭha), standing alone (pade sthite, ‘in the Pada text’), Prāt.
