## Chosen text

भुक्तिमुक्तिदायकं प्रशस्तचारुविग्रहं
भक्तवत्सलं स्थितं समस्तलोकविग्रहम् ।
निक्वणन्मनोज्ञहेमकिङ्किणीलसत्कटिं
काशिकापुराधिनाथकालभैरवं भजे ॥ ४ ॥

## Variants

- b: **स्थितं** in A C D E F vs **स्थिरं** in B and A(). Minor difference ("abiding" vs "steadfast"). C's Hindi says "स्थिर-शिवस्वरूप" although its text reads स्थितं. S has a different line: नितान्तभक्तवत्सलं समस्तलोकपालकम्.
- c: **निक्वणन्** in B, A() and S (its stanza 2, śl. 54) vs **विनिक्वणन्** in A C D E F. **The chosen text goes against the majority because of the meter.** With वि- the pāda has 16 syllables (LGLGLGLGLGLGLGLG), which breaks the hymn's 15-syllable ra-ja-ra-ja-ra line. Without it the pāda is regular. The sense does not change ("jingling"; vi- only intensifies). **Human to confirm.**
- B prints समस्तलाकाविग्रहम् (broken mātrā = लोक) and भज (= भजे). These are print damage.

Sense-changing: none substantive (स्थितं/स्थिरं is slight).
