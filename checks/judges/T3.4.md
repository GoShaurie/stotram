# T3.4: Roles and heads are right

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
For each pada and adhyāhāra item: is `role` right, and does `head` point at the word it actually depends on?

## Fail when
- A word is attached to the wrong head (an adjective pointed at the wrong noun, an object at the wrong verb).
- A role is wrong for the meaning (sampradāna vs sambandha for a pronoun that clearly means "our").
- A clause has no `kriyā` and no adhyāhāra verb is supplied, where a verb is clearly understood.

## Do not fail for
- Both attachments are grammatical and the other one is recorded as an alternative.
- Upapada-vibhakti (namaḥ + dative, saha + instrumental) that is noted.

## Example
`lambitām` with head `bhujaṅgatuṅgamālikām` (both f. acc. sg.) → pass.
