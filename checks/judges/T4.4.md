# T4.4: Epithets deconstructed

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
For words flagged `epithet` and for divine names: is the name explained (vigraha or derivation and its sense), not just glossed as the deity's name?

## Fail when
- *tripurāntaka* glossed only as "Śiva".
- A name whose meaning the verse is playing on, left unexplained.

## Do not fail for
- Common names where the verse doesn't use the etymology and a note explains the meaning once.

## Example
*śivaḥ* (epithet) with a note that the name means "the auspicious one" → pass.
