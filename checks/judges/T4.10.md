# T4.10: Neutral, attributed, no benefit claims

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
Are devotional and historical claims attributed ("tradition holds", "the phalaśruti says")? Are there no promises of benefits beyond quoting the phalaśruti, and no preaching?

## Fail when
- Promises ("chanting this removes all sins") not attributed to the phalaśruti.
- Preaching ("you should chant this daily").
- Disparaging other traditions.

## Do not fail for
- Quoting and attributing the phalaśruti.

## Example
"The phalaśruti promises steady devotion to Śiva" → pass.
