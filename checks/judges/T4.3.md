# T4.3: Literal vs contextual where they differ

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
For metaphors, epithets, bahuvrīhis and rūpaka/upamā: does the word give the contextual sense in `meaning` and the literal one in `literal` when they differ?

## Fail when
- Only the literal sense is given where it misleads ("lotus-eye-having" for *kamala-netra*).
- Only a figurative sense is given and the literal image is lost where it matters to the verse.

## Do not fail for
- Words where literal and contextual are the same (no `literal` needed).

## Example
*jaṭāṭavī*: meaning "the forest of matted hair" with a rūpaka note → pass.
