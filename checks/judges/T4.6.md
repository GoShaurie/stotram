# T4.6: Segments translate their words

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
For each translation segment in EN and HI: does its text actually translate the words in its `refs`, and do the refs cover what the text says? This drives the hover highlight.

## Fail when
- A segment's text translates a different word than its refs (hover would light the wrong word).
- A segment includes the meaning of a word that isn't in its refs, while that word's own segment is elsewhere.

## Do not fail for
- A segment that groups several words whose meanings are fused in the text, with all of them in refs.
- Segments that only carry a supplied word (adhyāhāra id).

## Example
`{"refs": ["w3"], "text": "On the spot made holy"}` where w3 = pāvita-sthale → pass.
