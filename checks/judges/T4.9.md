# T4.9: Annotation claims are supported

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
For each annotation: is every factual claim supported by a citation whose source excerpt is in the evidence, with a locator?

## Fail when
- A claim (story detail, attribution, textual fact) with no supporting excerpt.
- A locator that doesn't match the excerpt.
- A claim stronger than the source (the source says "some say", the note says "it is").

## Do not fail for
- A claim about another verse of this stotram that links to that verse's annotation (e.g. `[…](#v2-a5)`): the linked note carries the citation.
- Interpretive framing clearly marked as such ("one image carries the whole story").

## Example
a1 claims Śiva caught the Gaṅgā in his hair, citing Rāmāyaṇa Bāla Kāṇḍa with the excerpt present → pass.
