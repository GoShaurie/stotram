# T2.8b: Samāsa type is right

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
For every entry in `compounds`: is `type` (and `subtype`) the correct samāsa for this verse's meaning, and does `vigraha` analyse it accordingly?

## Fail when
- The vigraha implies a different type (e.g. a vigraha with *yasya* is bahuvrīhi, not tatpuruṣa).
- A compound that qualifies another word (an epithet of the deity) is typed tatpuruṣa/karmadhāraya when it is really a bahuvrīhi, or the reverse.
- Karmadhāraya vs tatpuruṣa mislabelled where it changes the meaning.

## Do not fail for
- The outermost type is given and inner steps are only in `structure` or `note`.
- A subtype is missing.

## Example
`pīta-ambara` typed karmadhāraya with meaning "yellow garment" when it is an epithet of Viṣṇu → fail: bahuvrīhi, "he whose garment is yellow".
