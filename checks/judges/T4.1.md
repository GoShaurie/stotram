# T4.1: Meanings match the evidence

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
For each word: is `meaning` (and each component's meaning) supported by at least one dictionary sense **and** consistent with at least one word-by-word source in the evidence?

## Fail when
- The meaning appears in no evidence source and no dictionary sense supports it.
- The sources agree on a different meaning and the data doesn't record an alternative.
- Component meanings contradict the word meaning.

## Do not fail for
- Wording differs but the sense is the same.
- The sources disagree and the data records the alternatives with medium/low confidence.

## Example
`pāvita` = "purified, made holy" with an MW entry for pū causative → pass.
