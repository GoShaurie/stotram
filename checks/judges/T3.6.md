# T3.6: Anvaya reads as good prose

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
Read the padas and adhyāhāra items in `anvaya` order as Sanskrit prose. Would a paṇḍita accept it as the prose order of the verse?

## Fail when
- Qualifiers far from what they qualify, verbs before their subjects in a way prose wouldn't, relative/correlative (yad/tad) out of order.
- A pada missing from where the sense needs it.

## Do not fail for
- Small order differences that are equally idiomatic.

## Example
`ayam … caṇḍatāṇḍavaṃ cakāra; saḥ śivaḥ naḥ śivaṃ tanotu` → pass.
