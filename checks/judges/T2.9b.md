# T2.9b: Compound nesting is right

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
For compounds with 3+ members: does `structure` group members the way the meaning requires?

## Fail when
- The brackets group members that don't form a unit in the meaning (e.g. `((galat-jala)-pravāha)` when the sense is "falling (water-stream)").
- `structure` contradicts `vigraha`.

## Do not fail for
- Either of two groupings is defensible and the note says so.

## Example
`((jaṭā-aṭavī)-(galat-(jala-pravāha)))…` pass: hair-forest, then a falling water-stream from it.
