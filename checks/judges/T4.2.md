# T4.2: Polysemy resolved for this context

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
For words with several dictionary senses: is the chosen sense right for this deity, tradition and line? Check `components[].sense` where given.

## Fail when
- A sense that is valid in the dictionary but wrong here (*hari* as "monkey" or "Viṣṇu" in a line about Śiva where "dispeller (of sorrow)" or "tawny" fits).
- The epithet's sense ignores the deity of the stotram.

## Do not fail for
- A literal sense with the contextual one in `meaning` and the literal one in `literal`.

## Example
*bhujaṅga* = "serpent" in a line about Śiva's garland → pass.
