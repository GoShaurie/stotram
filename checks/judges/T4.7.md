# T4.7: English and Hindi say the same

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
Do the EN and HI translations (and bhāvārtha) convey the same content at comparable granularity?

## Fail when
- One language adds or drops a pada's meaning.
- They choose different interpretations without an alternative recorded.

## Do not fail for
- Natural differences of idiom and word order.

## Example
EN "upon us", HI "हमारे" for naḥ → pass (dative/genitive both reasonable; the alternative is recorded).
