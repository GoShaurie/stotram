# T3.5: Translation is grounded

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
Every content word in the EN and HI translations comes from a pada of the verse or from a declared adhyāhāra item. Nothing is invented.

## Fail when
- The translation adds a concept, attribute or claim found in no pada (e.g. "the destroyer of evil" when no word says so).
- A supplied word (is, he, that, bow) is not declared in `adhyahara` and changes the meaning, not just the grammar.

## Do not fail for
- Function words English or Hindi need (articles, prepositions, auxiliaries, pronouns for agreement).
- Bracketed glosses that are marked as such.

## Example
A segment "the compassionate Lord" for a pada meaning only "lord" → fail: "compassionate" has no source word.
