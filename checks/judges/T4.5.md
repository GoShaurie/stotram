# T4.5: Tradition attributed, not imposed

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
Does the base meaning follow the plain sense of the text, with school-specific readings (Advaita, Viśiṣṭādvaita, Dvaita, Gauḍīya, Śaiva Siddhānta…) attributed in `alternatives` or annotations?

## Fail when
- A doctrinal reading is presented as the plain meaning ("this means the self is Brahman" when the words don't say so).
- A reading is foreign to the hymn's tradition and isn't attributed.
- An attribution is stated as historical fact ("Rāvaṇa wrote this") instead of tradition.

## Do not fail for
- Theology the text itself states.

## Example
An annotation saying "tradition holds" before a devotional claim → pass.
