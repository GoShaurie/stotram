# T4.8: Fluent, natural EN and HI

Follow [README.md](README.md) for inputs, output format and the rules every judge obeys.

## What to check
Is the English clear, reverent and modern? Is the Hindi natural modern Hindi, correctly spelled, not Sanskrit word salad?

## Fail when
- Ungrammatical or stilted sentences; Hindi that just strings Sanskrit words with Hindi case markers.
- Spelling errors in Hindi (nukta, chandrabindu/anusvāra misuse that changes the word).

## Do not fail for
- Sanskrit terms kept on purpose (tāṇḍava, ḍamaru) with a gloss nearby.

## Example
HI "जटा रूपी वन से गिरते हुए जल-प्रवाह से पवित्र हुए स्थान पर" → pass.
